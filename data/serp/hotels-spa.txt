# Rob's rule for the hotel corpora: evidence comes from guides and especially
# BLOGGERS. Booking engines are not sources - they rank by inventory and
# commission, so a listing says nothing about whether anyone rates the place.
# Brand sites are out for the same reason. Hotels.com is a validator only.
#

#   lamplitlondon.com, explorecities.com, luxuryhotel.guide, boutiquehotel.guru,
#   new-hotels-guide.com, where-stay.com, choosewhere.com, hotelradar.co.uk,
#   pmahotels.org, taketravelinfo.com, myboutiquehotel.com, dyme.earth,
#   bestboutiquehotelsworldwide.com, theluxuryeditor.com, blog.hotelslash.com
#
# DROPPED IN THIS PASS
#   londonluxurymag.co.uk - AI SEO farm. No named human ("Editor Niki",
#     "Editor Adeel" are desk pseudonyms with author archives). Almost every
#     title on the index carries a number and most are 5 items: "5 Top Most
#     Luxury Wedding Venues", "Finding The 5 Best Horse Riding Lessons in
#     London", "Top 5 Kids Parks", "5 Top Knightsbridge Restaurants Halal" -
#     that last is keyword order, not English. Complete grid coverage well
#     outside any plausible remit (microneedling, garden furniture covers,
#     aluminium joinery for B2B clients, smart home security). US spelling on
#     a London site ("Flavor Explosion"). The spa page opens with a "Criteria
#     for Selection" section, which is the how-we-choose protest. It also
#     sells a paid hotel "Directory" in the footer.
#   klook.com - booking platform (pre-stripped from the SERP).
#   spahotelsguide.com - aggregator (pre-stripped from the SERP).
