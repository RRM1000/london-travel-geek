# January deals, London: leads not yet confirmed on an operator page

Checked 2026-11-23. Everything here is a lead, not copy. Re-check on the date given, then move confirmed items into `london.json`.

## Strongest recurring schemes

- **Hampton Court Palace half-price winter tickets.** Ran 7 January to 8 February 2026, from £14 an adult on weekdays and £15.45 at weekends, per ianvisits.co.uk (published 17 Dec 2025) and topdogdays.com. Announced mid-December last year. The Historic Royal Palaces site sits behind a Cloudflare challenge, so the operator page could not be read. Re-check https://www.hrp.org.uk/hampton-court-palace/visit/tickets-and-prices/ in the browser on 7 to 14 December 2026. The Tower of London and Kensington Palace run the same winter pricing, so check both pages too. HRP also runs a £1 tickets scheme for benefit recipients (https://www.hrp.org.uk/one-pound-tickets/, unreadable the same way).
- **Official London Theatre New Year Sale 2027.** Launches 5 January 2027, details "coming soon" (https://officiallondontheatre.com/sale/). Last year's sale was announced 11 November 2025 with the show list, priority window 18 November and general sale 25 November. This year the page holds until the launch, so expect the participating-shows announcement on solt.co.uk from late November to December. Re-check weekly from 30 November, and on 5 January for prices.
- **Black Friday show offers for January to March performances.** Black Friday is 27 November 2026. Already live on ATG (Wicked, Moulin Rouge) and Hadestown, and they end 30 November or 14 December. Re-check on 27 to 30 November for Disney's The Lion King, Hamilton, Les Mis, Phantom and the Delfont Mackintosh, LW Theatres and Nimax shows (their offers pages carry no dates yet). London Theatre Geek already runs a Black Friday deals feature at londontheatregeek.co.uk/features/best-black-friday-theatre-deals.
- **Merlin Annual Pass sale.** "Save Up To £70pp" Black Friday sale is live on https://www.merlinannualpass.co.uk/ with no end date shown. Merlin ran a Spring Sale in March 2026 (themeparkguide.co.uk). Nothing found for January 2026. Re-check 30 November and 4 to 8 January.
- **Merlin multi-attraction tickets (London Eye, Madame Tussauds, SEA LIFE, Shrek, Dungeon).** Standing 68% headline saving, in `london.json`. The London Dungeon and Merlin's own annual pass page were not readable or dated; re-check thelondondungeon.com in the browser.

## Looks recurring, nothing published yet

Re-check https://abbavoyage.com/ticketing-information/ after 29 November. Family offer (half-price children) runs to the end of December only.
- **Almeida 25s and under £5.** Per-production weekly windows, with code 25UNDER; the one seen covered 10 to 16 November 2026. Re-check https://www.almeida.co.uk/ for the January production.

- **English Heritage membership.** 25% off annual membership is the Black Friday offer; ends unknown. Re-check https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/ on 30 November.

- **Blue Monday (18 January 2027) offers.** Venues in the Secret London and Metro January 2026 roundups ran one-day Blue Monday deals, all restaurants and bars. None for attractions found. Re-check the week of 11 January.
