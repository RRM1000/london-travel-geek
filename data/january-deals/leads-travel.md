## Transport sales: when they normally land

- **Great British Rail Sale (DfT, nearly all operators).** Operator-of-record is GOV.UK; National Rail's sale page currently reads "The Great British Rail Sale Has Ended".
  - January 2026: sale week 6-12 Jan, discounted travel 13 Jan-25 Mar 2026. GOV.UK announced it on 31 Dec 2025 (https://www.gov.uk/government/news/all-aboard-for-savings-the-great-british-rail-sale-returns).
  - January 2024: sale week 23-29 Jan, travel 30 Jan-15 Mar 2024, announced 20 Jan 2024.
  - First sale was April 2022. No 2027 date announced. Re-check GOV.UK transport news from about 20 Dec 2026 and nationalrail.co.uk/great-british-rail-sale/. Best case it lands the first or second week of January, with only about a week's notice.
- **Eurostar.** Its sale page says it runs "sales and flash sales throughout the year" and that its sale has ended. Press reported a 25% sale (travel 23 Nov 2026-17 Mar 2027, book by 9 Nov), now over. Nothing listed for January 2027. Re-check https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en/deals/eurostar-sale on 28 Dec and again 4 Jan; newsletter sign-up gives first notice. The hotel offer (up to 25% off, stays 4 Jan-31 Mar 2027) closes 30 Nov 2026.
- **Operator January sales.** GWR ran a January sale in an earlier year (Wales Online reported 225,000 lower-cost tickets; year not confirmed). GWR's current homepage carries a Black Friday sale only (book to 2 Nov, travel 6 Nov-17 Dec 2026). Re-check gwr.com, avantiwestcoast.co.uk (Superfare is a separate site, avantisuperfare.co.uk), lner.co.uk and southeasternrailway.co.uk on 28 Dec.
- **Railcards.** railcard.co.uk shows partner offers only (Gousto, tastecard, theatre tickets). Retailer codes (TrainPal, Student Beans 15%) are third-party and not usable as sources. Re-check railcard.co.uk/offers/ in the first week of January.
- **TfL fares.** Tube, DLR, Overground and Elizabeth line fares last changed 1 March 2026 (+5.8%); bus fares changed 1 Nov 2026 (£1.85). Caps frozen until March 2027 per our own fares article. No fare change falls inside January 2027, and no 2027 announcement was found. tfl.gov.uk fare pages are client-rendered, so curl cannot read them. Re-check the TfL fares press release in mid-December and the GOV.UK/DfT announcement of the March 2027 regulated rail fares change (usually November-January).
- **Coaches.** National Express: "100,000 tickets for £7 or less" for travel to 11 Jan 2027; re-check on 28 Dec for a January sale. Megabus (HTTP 403) and FlixBus (no offers on homepage) not resolved; try Bing news "Megabus January sale" again in late December.
- **Thames boats.** Uber Boat BF25 (25% off, bought 20-29 Nov, travel to 31 Mar 2027) is the only dated offer. Thames Rockets' voucher terms mention "January sales"; Thames River Sightseeing's offers page still shows last year's Black Friday code (BLACK40, travel to 31 Mar 2026), which suggests the same pattern. City Cruises homepage shows New Year's Eve "15% OFF ON ALL SIGHTSEEING CRUISES" and Black Friday up to 50%; validity dates not stated, so not recorded. Re-check all three on 28 Dec.
- **Tour buses.** Big Bus Tours (HTTP 403 to curl), The Original Tour, Golden Tours (redirect loop), Bustronome and Brigit's Bakery show no January offer on their homepages. LNER's Discount Days Out lists "London Brunch Bus Tour - Golden Tours" valid until 31 Mar 2027 (see travel.json). Hand the tour-bus section to the other agent's London page.

## Day-trip attractions: likely January deals

- **Roman Baths £1 return** is announced (see travel.json). Cross-check the Thermae Bath Spa and Bath Abbey news pages in late December for a similar scheme.
- **Merlin Annual Pass.** Black Friday sale ends 6 Dec 2026 (Essential £99 vs £139, Gold £169 vs £239, Platinum £229 vs £299). A March 2026 spring sale cut the pass to £99, per press. Chessington and LEGOLAND are shut for most of January, so a pass sale is more likely in February-March. Re-check merlinannualpass.co.uk and legoland.co.uk/annual-passes/ in late January.
- **English Heritage membership.** Black Friday 25% off, code BF2026, no end date shown on the page. A January membership offer is plausible; re-check english-heritage.org.uk/join on 28 Dec. Dover Castle and Stonehenge standing "book early save up to 15%" are in travel.json.
- **National Trust.** Membership £168 a year; winter cottage breaks promoted. No January offer found. Re-check on 28 Dec.
- **Bicester Village.** Outlet pricing is up to 40% off all year; a post-Christmas winter sale is likely but nothing is announced. Re-check the Offers page on 26 Dec.
- **Warwick Castle.** Standing "up to 46% off online" (travel.json); Black Friday Light Trail 20% off ended. The 2027 short-breaks page may carry a January offer.
- **Chessington, LEGOLAND, Thorpe Park, Paultons.** See closures in travel.json. Chessington and LEGOLAND open 11 Feb 2027 for half term; Thorpe Park reopens 19 Mar. Paultons closure (4 Jan-5 Feb 2027) comes from our own article; its opening-times page is client-rendered, so confirm in the browser pane. Possible discrepancy: LEGOLAND's calendar data lists 1 and 2 January only, while our article says the park is open to 3 January; Chessington lists 1-3 January.
- **Edinburgh Castle, Windsor Castle, Canterbury Cathedral, Christ Church, Bodleian, Ashmolean, Blenheim, King's College, Fitzwilliam, Brighton i360, Brighton Palace Pier.** Homepages carry no January offer. Brighton Palace Pier has a "Save up to 20%" promotions page with no current offers listed; Brighton i360 shows a New Year's Eve event only. Windsor Castle 2027 admission tickets are on sale from 1 Jan 2027.
- **Paris.** The Louvre is free to all after 6pm on the first Friday of the month except July and August; 1 January 2027 is the first Friday of January and the museum is closed that day, so the first free evening is Friday 5 February. The Louvre's Zurbarán retrospective runs to 25 Jan 2027. Eiffel Tower site blocked to curl.
- **Bruges.** Winter Glow (Wintergloed) free festival runs 20 Nov 2026-31 Jan 2027.
- **Days Out Guide 2-for-1 with rail.** The site is client-rendered, so curl reads nothing. The rail operators' own pages (SWR, LNER, Lumo, EMR, Avanti, Stansted Express) carry the scheme; Avanti's 2for1 page redirects to daysoutguide.co.uk. Terms vary by operator and attraction; read each before listing.
