The City layers Roman walls, medieval churches and modern towers; Shoreditch and Spitalfields add markets, restaurants, street art and nightlife.

> **Best for:** food, nightlife, history, modern architecture and east-London transport. **Think twice if:** you want quiet weekend streets in the City or a traditional West End atmosphere.

## Best things to do

- Visit the Tower of London and walk beside the Thames.
- Explore St Paul's, the City's lanes and a public viewing gallery.
- Browse Spitalfields Market and Brick Lane.
- Walk Shoreditch street-art routes respectfully.
- Continue to the Barbican or Museum of London Docklands.

Shoreditch rooms can hear bars and street activity. The City is quieter on weekends but may offer better hotel rates.

The Clove Club, Legado and Spitalfields restaurants expand the high-end choice; Brick Lane has good food but quality varies widely.

## Transport and airport fit

Liverpool Street has the Elizabeth, Central, Circle, Hammersmith & City and Metropolitan lines plus Stansted and Southend trains. Shoreditch High Street is on the Overground.

The area is excellent for London City Airport via DLR connections and for Heathrow via the Elizabeth line.
