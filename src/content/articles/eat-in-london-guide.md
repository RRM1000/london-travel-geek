London's food scene is much bigger than conventional restaurants. You can build a meal around a specialist neighbourhood restaurant, try several traders inside a food hall, eat your way around a market or find something quick before a show or train.

This is the starting point for our London food guides. We will organise recommendations around what travellers actually need: **type of food, location, price, occasion and how much time they have**.

> **How these guides work:** recommendations will be researched using established food writers, trusted publications and official venue information. Unless we have visited personally, we will describe them as researched recommendations rather than our own reviews. Every guide will link to its sources and show when it was last checked.

## Markets, food halls and street food

Food markets and halls are especially useful for groups because everybody can choose something different. They also vary considerably: some are outdoor markets with limited seating, while others are permanent indoor venues.

Our guides will distinguish between:

- **food markets**, such as destination markets and weekend neighbourhood markets;
- **indoor food halls**, with multiple kitchens and shared seating;
- **street-food collections**, which may operate only on particular days;
- **department-store food halls**, combining counters, produce and places to eat;
- **seasonal markets and temporary pop-ups**.

The main comparison will cover cuisine range, typical cost, seating, shelter from bad weather, accessibility, family suitability, opening pattern and the nearest station.

## How recommendations will be presented

There will be **no separate page for every restaurant** at this stage. Restaurants will appear inside substantial grouped guides.

Each entry will use a consistent format:

| Detail | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | The type of visitor, meal or occasion it suits |
| Area and nearest station | How it fits into a London day |
| Cuisine | The restaurant's focus or regional speciality |
| Price | A broad £, ££, £££ or ££££ category |
| Dietary information | Notable vegetarian, vegan, halal or gluten-free provision |
| Why it is included | A concise comparison of credible recommendations |
| Useful links | Supporting coverage, official menu and booking page |
| Last checked | When opening details and links were verified |

A restaurant may be mentioned in more than one guide, but we will avoid repeating the same full description across the site.

## Current and temporary recommendations

Permanent restaurants, markets and food halls will live in evergreen guides. Pop-ups, guest residencies and seasonal events will be kept in a separate current guide with:

- opening and closing dates;
- the organiser's official link;
- a prominent last-checked date;
- a warning that dates and traders can change;
- removal or archiving after the event ends.

This keeps temporary information from making the permanent guides unreliable.
