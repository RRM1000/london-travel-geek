An aparthotel, or apartment hotel, is a hotel where the room has a kitchen. Whether that is worth it comes down to **how many nights you are staying and how many of you share the room**.

> 💡 **The Short Version:**
>
> - **A couple, central, a few nights:** a studio at [Wilde Covent Garden](hotel:wilde-aparthotels-covent-garden), off the Strand, with a hob and a dishwasher, or [Native Mayfair](hotel:native-mayfair) on a mews off Grosvenor Square.
> - **A family who wants two bedrooms:** [Native King's Wardrobe](hotel:native-kings-wardrobe) by St Paul's (two- and three-bedroom apartments up to 114 m²), [Citadines Trafalgar Square](hotelscom:186465) (a 70 m² two-bedroom that sleeps six) or [SACO Holborn](hotel:saco-holborn) (two-bedrooms from 44 to 80 m²).
> - **A week or more:** [The Chronicle](hotelscom:978187744) off Fetter Lane, where every suite has a washer-dryer.
> - **Paying less:** [Staycity Greenwich High Road](hotelscom:448739) or [Native Fulham Broadway](hotel:native-fulham-broadway), outside Zone 1.
>
> The universal trade is that **nobody cleans daily**: weekly is the standard at every operator here. The format pays for itself around the **third or fourth night**, sooner with children.

## Aparthotel, serviced apartment or Airbnb

- **Aparthotel:** booked by the night, with a reception, a lobby and often a bar. Locke, Staycity, Wilde and Citadines.
- **Serviced apartment:** the same idea leaning longer-stay, with a concierge rather than a reception and bigger units. Cheval and Fraser. Operators use both words, so judge by the unit, not the label.
- **Airbnb:** someone's flat, with no front desk and a host who can cancel. A whole home in London let for more than 90 nights a year needs planning permission, so some listings are not legal.

## The trade nobody puts on the booking page

**Housekeeping is weekly, not daily:**

- **Locke** lists weekly cleaning across its London sites.
- **Staycity** does a full clean on day four, then every seven days, on stays of seven nights or more.
- **Supercity** cleans once a week, with a full change of towels and linen.

If you want a made bed and fresh towels every morning, book a hotel.

**The second trade is the layout.** Many London aparthotel rooms are **studios**: one room with a bed, a sofa and a kitchen along the wall, comfortable for two and cramped for four. Separate bedrooms cost more.

Staycity's cots come without bedding, so bring a fitted sheet; Wilde includes one sheet with the travel cot. Wilde makes up the sofa bed before you arrive if you ask at booking, while Staycity leaves the linen in the room for you to do. **Washing is the other family question.** Every Cheval and Supercity unit has a washer-dryer, as do Native King's Wardrobe and Native Bankside. Cove Moorgate's Family Apartment is a separate bedroom plus a sofa bed and a full kitchen, and the building also sells a one-bedroom with its own washer-dryer.

## Studios for one or two

A studio is one room: bed, sofa, table and a kitchen along a wall, with the shower room off it. What varies is the kitchen.

- **[Wilde Covent Garden](hotel:wilde-aparthotels-covent-garden)**, Adam Street. A full kitchen in the studio, with a hob, a dishwasher, a fridge and a Smeg kettle and toaster. Three minutes from the piazza. It also sells a Wilde Bedroom, which is a room rather than a studio, so check the room name.
- **[Citadines Trafalgar Square](hotelscom:186465)**, Northumberland Avenue. A 25 m² studio for two with a hob, microwave, fridge and dishwasher, a few minutes from the National Gallery.
- **[Leman Locke](hotel:leman-locke)**, Whitechapel. A hob, a fridge and a microwave rather than a full cooker, with a sofa area divided from the bed, and Locke's ground-floor café and bar used by people who are not staying there.
- **[The Chronicle](hotelscom:978187744)**, Holborn. A 25 m² Studio Suite with its own washer-dryer, though the kitchen is a kitchen/dining area; a full kitchen arrives from the one-bedroom suite up.
- **[Native Fitzrovia](hotelscom:4331287520)**, Margaret Street. A 24 m² Medium Studio with a kitchenette, a few minutes from Oxford Circus and the Elizabeth line.
- **[Buckle Street Studios](hotel:buckle-street-studios)**, next to Leman Locke. Smaller kitchenettes again, and some of its cheapest Hotel Rooms have no kitchen at all.

**Locke** sites have a ground-floor café and bar. Leman Locke and Bermonds Locke fit the same kit: a hob, a fridge and a microwave (a microwave-grill at Bermonds) rather than a full cooker, with a kettle and basic cookware but no dishwasher. Bermonds Locke has a washer-dryer built in; Buckle Street Studios has a shared laundry room, £5 to wash and £5 to dry. SACO Holborn now sits under Locke too.

**Cheval is the one for size.** Three Quays sits on the river between the Tower and Tower Bridge, and Gloucester Park is eight minutes from the free South Kensington museums. Both come with a full kitchen, a washer-dryer and a 24-hour gym; Gloucester Park adds secure underground parking and a twelve-person private cinema room, which Three Quays does not have.

**Supercity puts a washer-dryer in every suite**, as do Cheval, Bermonds Locke and two of Native's addresses; over two weeks that saves hunting for a launderette. A £100 refundable deposit applies to studios and one-bedroom suites on stays of up to six nights, and £250 to two-bedroom suites or any stay of seven nights or more.

## Paying less for an apartment hotel

**Book the smallest unit with a kitchen.** Several operators sell a grade below the studio, and it is the cheapest way in: Native Fulham Broadway's 11 m² Double Room, Wilde's Hideaway Studio at Liverpool Street, Citadines Barbican's Classic Room, and Staycity Deptford Bridge's Hotel Room. Check whether it has a kitchen: Buckle Street's cheapest Hotel Rooms do not.

**Go outside Zone 1.** Staycity's Greenwich High Road and Deptford Bridge, Native Fulham Broadway and Templeton Place in Earl's Court are all on lines that reach the centre in under half an hour. Staycity's parking, shared between Greenwich and Deptford, is £13 a night and must be booked ahead.

**Stay longer, or book a Saturday in the City.** Staycity and Wilde both offer discounts on longer stays, and the City of London addresses empty out at weekends.

**Count the extras.** Supercity takes a refundable deposit of £100 to £250. An extra clean at Citadines Trafalgar Square is £25 for a studio and £40 for a two-bedroom.

For one or two nights a kitchen rarely pays for itself, and a plain room is cheaper: [the best budget hotels in London](/articles/best-budget-hotels-london/) lists rooms at £150 a night or less, [Premier Inn, Travelodge and ibis](/articles/premier-inn-vs-travelodge-vs-ibis-london/) compares the three budget chains, and [our capsule hotels guide](/articles/pod-hotels-london/) explains why a single night's price is worth so little.

## When it is worth it, and when it is not

**Book an aparthotel if** you are staying four nights or more; you are travelling with children and dreading the breakfast bill; you want to do laundry mid-trip; or you are working from London for a week and need a desk and a kettle at odd hours.

**Book a hotel instead if** the stay is one or two nights, you want daily housekeeping, or you want a restaurant downstairs and room service at eleven. On a short trip the kitchen is dead weight you have paid for.

**One thing that catches people:** several of these buildings are residential, with actual residents. Cove Landmark Pinnacle is partway up a residential tower and the front desk is a concierge rather than a reception. Cove's Huntingdon House in Kensington has a 90-night minimum, so it is not one for a holiday.
