Valentine's Day 2027 falls on a **Sunday**, the same afternoon England play France at Twickenham. The work is in booking early; the cheapest good evening is a free viewpoint at sunset and dinner nearby.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Sunday 14 February 2027, sunset about 5.13pm.** For dinner, book the room rather than the menu: **Clos Maggiore**'s conservatory, **Duck & Waffle** forty floors up (bookings open two months ahead, so mid-December), **Rules** or **Sketch**. For the view, **Sky Garden is free**, with Valentine's week released on **Monday 18 January**; the **London Eye's Cupid's Pod** is a private capsule for two. **Thames dinner cruises** leave Westminster Pier at 7.45pm. **England play France at Twickenham at 3.10pm the same day**, so expect full trains between Waterloo, Clapham Junction and Richmond.

## Dinner

**Book for the room, and book the day its window opens.** These are from our [special occasion restaurants](/articles/special-occasion-restaurants-london/) and [rooftop and riverside restaurants](/articles/best-rooftop-restaurants-london/) guides, which have the full entries.

### Clos Maggiore, Covent Garden

*£££ · 33 King Street, WC2E 8JD · two minutes from Covent Garden*

A **conservatory under real blossom**, with a fire and a retractable roof. The cooking is Provençal and northern Italian: order the **rack of lamb** or the hand-made pasta, and lean on a French wine list of several hundred bins. The restaurant has several rooms and only one is the conservatory, so **ask for it by name when you book** and say it is an occasion.

### Duck & Waffle, City of London

*££££ · 110 Bishopsgate, EC2N 4AY · open 24 hours*

Forty floors up in the Heron Tower with sealed floor-to-ceiling windows, and **open around the clock**. The order is the dish it is named after: confit duck leg, a fried duck egg and mustard maple syrup on a waffle, **£26**. **Reservations open two months ahead**, so 14 February becomes bookable in mid-December. Card only, no cash. Ask for a window table.

### Rules, Covent Garden

*££££ · 35 Maiden Lane, WC2E 7LB · since 1798*

London's oldest restaurant, trading since **1798**. Order the **steak and kidney pudding** or the **jugged hare**; the game comes from the restaurant's own estate in the Pennines. The room is small, walk-ins are unlikely, and the ground-floor room is the one from *Spectre*. **Book well ahead.**

### Sketch, Mayfair

*££££ · 9 Conduit Street, W1S 2XG · three minutes from Oxford Circus*

Four restaurants behind one door. **The Gallery**, rebuilt in yellow and copper by Yinka Shonibare and India Mahdavi in 2022, is the one for a date; the **Lecture Room & Library** upstairs is the three-Michelin-star room. The egg-shaped lavatory pods are in the East Bar, which takes walk-ins. **Book the specific room**: weeks ahead for the Gallery, months for the Lecture Room.

### Le Pont de la Tour, Tower Bridge

*£££ · Butlers Wharf · [book a table](https://lepontdelatour.co.uk/book-a-table/)*

Classical French cooking under **Tower Bridge**: moules marinière, roast native lobster with garlic butter, and a two- or three-course set menu. There is a brasserie-style Bar & Grill and a more formal restaurant, and the terrace faces the bridge. It has traded on the spot for more than thirty years.

### The River Café, Hammersmith

*££££ · eighteen minutes' walk from Barons Court*

Michelin-starred Italian on the Thames, informal in feel and expensive in practice. **The terrace books furthest ahead**, by months; in February you are booking the dining room.

For a view with dinner, our [rooftop restaurants guide](/articles/best-rooftop-restaurants-london/) covers **Aqua Shard** (level 31 of The Shard, Twilight menu from £45), **Hutong** (northern Chinese on level 33) and **Darwin Brasserie** inside Sky Garden, where a table gets you into the garden even after the free tickets have gone.

## Things to do as a couple

### Sunset from Sky Garden

*Free · level 35 of 20 Fenchurch Street · Sunday 11am to 9pm*

Three storeys of planted terraces under a glass roof on top of the Walkie-Talkie, with **The Shard directly opposite** and Tower Bridge downstream. Sunset on 14 February is about **5.13pm**, but the open-air terrace closes at 6pm. Free slots last one hour and come out **every Monday morning, three weeks ahead**: the week of 8 to 14 February is released on **Monday 18 January**. Arrive within 15 minutes of your slot. Our [Sky Garden guide](/articles/sky-garden-guide/) has the booking rules and the paid ways in once the free tickets have gone.

### The London Eye: Cupid's Pod or Champagne

The **[Cupid's Pod](https://ticketing.londoneye.com/en/tickets/london-eye-cupids-pod)** is a private capsule for two adults (18 and over) for a **30-minute rotation**, priced at **£750 a pod** on the November 2026 dates. After booking, a London Eye executive contacts you to add a host, Champagne, flowers, truffles or a photographer; you cannot bring your own food or drink, and the pod cannot be decorated. The Eye says most Cupid's Pods are booked for proposals, and the staff keep them secret.

The cheaper option is the shared **[Champagne Experience](https://ticketing.londoneye.com/en/tickets/london-eye-champagne-experience)**: a glass of Moët & Chandon in the Eye Lounge before boarding, a second glass on the flight, and Fast Track boarding. It was **£49 a person** on November 2026 dates. Our [London Eye guide](/articles/london-eye-guide/) covers the standard tickets.

### A Thames dinner cruise

**City Cruises'** [dinner sailing](https://www.cityexperiences.com/london/city-cruises/london-dinner-cruise-river-thames/) leaves **Westminster Pier at 7.45pm** for three hours: three British courses, a live singer and dancing, smart casual. It cost **£99 to £137** in late September and early October 2026, with a VIP option from £144 that adds Champagne, front seats and a cheeseboard. **Window tables seat two and sell out first.** The shorter evening cruise leaves Tower Millennium Pier at 6.30pm with canapés and live music.

### Comedy

**The Comedy Store Players** play the Comedy Store on **Sundays at 7.30pm**, so 14 February is a show night. It is improv rather than stand-up, by a troupe that has performed there since October 1985 and that Guinness confirmed in 2010 as the world's longest-running comedy show with the same cast. The club is at 1a Oxendon Street, three minutes from Piccadilly Circus. Our [comedy clubs guide](/articles/best-comedy-clubs-london/) has the rest, including the free nights.

### Ice skating

The outdoor rinks at Somerset House, Hampton Court, Leicester Square, Battersea and the Queen's House have **all closed by mid-January**. In February, skate indoors at **Alexandra Palace**, **Queens** in Bayswater or **Lee Valley Ice Centre** in Leyton; our [ice skating guide](/articles/ice-skating-london/) has the details.

## Booking advice

- **Book the day the window opens.** Duck & Waffle's two-month window reaches 14 February in mid-December; Sketch's Gallery and Rules book weeks ahead, the River Café months.
- **Name the room or table.** The conservatory at Clos Maggiore, a window table at Duck & Waffle, the Gallery at Sketch. A booking for "the restaurant" can land you somewhere else in the building.
- **Missed out on the 14th?** It is a Sunday, so Saturday 13th and Monday 15th are the obvious alternatives, and the restaurants and the Eye are the same on either.
- **Sky Garden's free slots go on Monday 18 January.** Set an alarm; if you miss them, a table at Darwin Brasserie or Fenchurch gets you in.

## Valentine's Day on a budget

- **Sky Garden** is free with a timed ticket, and **[Horizon 22](/articles/best-views-london/)**, on level 58 of 22 Bishopsgate, is free too and higher. On Sundays Horizon 22 is open 10am to 4pm, so it is a lunchtime view rather than a sunset one.
- **The Garden at 120**, fifteen storeys up on Fenchurch Street, needs no ticket at all. It shuts in high wind and ice, since it is all outdoors.
- **Waterloo Bridge at dusk.** The sun sets upstream behind Westminster while the City lights come on downstream, from one free spot in the middle of the bridge.
- **Uber Boat instead of a cruise.** Westminster to Greenwich is **£12.40** by contactless at weekends, on the commuter catamaran with an open deck at the back. Details are in our [river cruises guide](/articles/thames-river-cruises-london/).
- **Eat early or late.** Clos Maggiore's pre- and post-theatre menu is a fraction of the à la carte; the [special occasion guide](/articles/special-occasion-restaurants-london/) lists the other cheaper ways into serious kitchens.

More free viewpoints are in our [best views in London](/articles/best-views-london/) guide.

> ⚠️ **England v France is at Twickenham at 3.10pm on Sunday 14 February.** Allianz Stadium holds 82,000, and trains from Waterloo to Twickenham run via Clapham Junction and Richmond. Match-day shuttle buses start three hours before kick-off, and the crowd takes two to three hours to clear afterwards, so trains through Richmond are busiest from about noon and again into the evening. Our [Twickenham Stadium guide](/articles/twickenham-stadium-travel-guide/) has the details.
