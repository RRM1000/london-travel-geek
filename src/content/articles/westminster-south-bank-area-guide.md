Westminster and the South Bank place many famous sights within one riverside walk: Parliament, Westminster Abbey, the London Eye, Southbank Centre and the National Theatre.

> **Best for:** first-time visitors, landmark photography, short stays and travellers who want to walk. **Think twice if:** you want neighbourhood nightlife or low hotel prices.

## What belongs to this area?

Use Westminster or St James's Park for Parliament and the abbey; Victoria for rail, coaches and Buckingham Palace access; Waterloo for the South Bank; and Embankment for both banks of the Thames.

## Best things to do

- Tour Westminster Abbey and walk around Parliament Square.
- Cross Westminster Bridge for the classic river view.
- Walk the South Bank to the National Theatre, Tate Modern and Borough Market.
- Visit Churchill War Rooms or the National Gallery.
- Continue through St James's Park to Buckingham Palace.

## Hotels

| Tier | Hotel | Why consider it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium | Conrad London St James | Excellent for Westminster Abbey, St James's Park and government landmarks |
| Mid-range | Park Plaza Westminster Bridge | Large property beside the South Bank with family-room options |
| Value-focused | citizenM London Victoria | Compact modern rooms near Victoria's transport |

“Value” is relative in central London. Compare room size, view supplements and breakfast before booking.

## Restaurants

| Restaurant | Style | Why go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Cinnamon Club | Modern Indian | A special setting in the former Westminster Library |
| OXO Tower Restaurant or Brasserie | Modern British | River views; reserve and specify which dining room |
| Bancone Borough Yards | Italian pasta | Useful after a South Bank walk towards Borough |

For a quick meal, Lower Marsh behind Waterloo has independent cafés and casual restaurants. Avoid choosing solely from a photo menu beside the largest attractions.

## Transport and airport fit

Westminster, Waterloo, Embankment, St James's Park and Victoria provide multiple Tube lines. Victoria is useful for Gatwick/Southern trains and coaches; Waterloo serves southwest England.

Heavy events, protests and state occasions can close roads around Westminster. Use TfL status information and walk across the river when practical.

## Suggested half-day route

Start at Westminster Abbey, cross Westminster Bridge, follow the South Bank to Tate Modern and finish at Borough Market or London Bridge. Reverse it when an abbey entry slot dictates the morning.
