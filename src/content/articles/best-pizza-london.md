There is no single best pizza in London any more, and pretending otherwise is what most guides get wrong. The city is cooking at least five different things under one word — Neapolitan, New York, Detroit, Chicago deep dish and Romana — and they are not better and worse versions of each other. They are different foods.

That split is recent, and it is the real story of London pizza in 2026. Cross-reference the three big lists published this year and the Neapolitan establishment holds the formal rankings while almost everything the critics are actually excited about is American in shape and served out of a pub, an arch or a market stall.

So this guide is arranged **by style**, then by how hard the table is to get. Once you know whether you want a foldable slice or a puffy cornicione, most of the argument disappears.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Napoli on the Road** in Chiswick is the highest-ranked pizzeria in Europe and worth the journey west. **50 Kalò** is the best pizza you can reach from a tourist itinerary without trying. **Short Road Pizza** in a Leyton pub holds National Pizza of the Year and is the most interesting meal on this page. **Crisp Pizza** is the New York one the critics rate highest. **Ria's** is the only serious Detroit pizza in the city. And **Rudy's** or **Yard Sale** is where to go when you just want good pizza without a plan.

> 📘 **How we choose these (editorial note)**
> No paid placements. These are drawn from a wide spread of sources rather than any single list - the food and travel press, the big listings sites, independent blogs and specialists, video, and what Londoners recommend themselves - and cross-referenced against each other. Prices, hours and residencies change faster in this category than any other on this site; check before you travel.

## What the rankings actually say

Two award systems matter here and they disagree, which is useful rather than confusing.

**50 Top Pizza** is the Italian-run ranking that judges Neapolitan technique — dough, ferment, oven, cornicione. Its 2026 Europe list put four London pizzerias in the top 50, all of them Italian in method.

**The National Pizza Awards** are British and judge the pizza rather than the tradition, which is why they keep being won by people working out of pubs and market stalls. Between them, Short Road Pizza and Bad Boy Pizza Society have taken National Pizza of the Year in 2022, 2024 and 2025.

## Neapolitan: the ranked pizzerias

The formal end. Long-fermented dough, a wet centre you are supposed to eat with a knife and fork, and a puffed, blistered rim.

### Napoli on the Road, Chiswick

*££ · Chiswick · 8 min from Turnham Green · #1 pizzeria in Europe, three years running*

Michele Pascarella was named Best Pizza Maker in the World by 50 Top Pizza in 2023, and his Chiswick pizzeria has held first place in Europe every year since 2024. It started as a truck touring London's food markets, which is worth remembering when you are on the District line wondering how far west this is.

It is a genuine pilgrimage — forty minutes from the centre, in a quiet stretch of Chiswick — and it is the only entry on this page where the ranking and the reputation agree completely.

### 50 Kalò, Trafalgar Square

*££ · Trafalgar Square · 1 min from Charing Cross · #4 in Europe*

Ciro Salvo's London outpost, and the answer to the question this guide gets asked most: where is the genuinely excellent pizza that does not require a journey. Fourth in Europe, one minute from Charing Cross, and surrounded by some of the worst-value restaurants in London.

If you are staying central and want one very good pizza with no planning at all, this is it.

### L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele, Marylebone

*££ · Marylebone · 2 min from Baker Street · #28 in Europe*

The London arm of the Naples institution founded in 1870 — the one from *Eat Pray Love*, if that is a reference point. The menu is deliberately, almost aggressively short, because in Naples the argument is that a pizzeria doing forty toppings is not concentrating.

Baker Street is the branch named in the 2026 rankings.

### Connie's Pizza, Peckham

*££ · Peckham · 1 min from Queen's Road Peckham · #48 in Europe, One to Watch 2026*

Entered the European top 50 at 48 within a year of opening and took the 'one to watch' award with it — the only London pizzeria on that list south of the river. It sits in the railway arches directly under Queen's Road Peckham station, in the old Little Kudu site.

Andrea Asciuti, who founded 081 Pizzeria, calls the style "Bri-talian": a crisp London base built with Italian method, which is a more honest description of what most good London pizza now is than "Neapolitan" would be.

## The one with a tasting menu

### Napoli on the Road Soho, Soho

*£££ · Soho · 140 Wardour Street · book weeks ahead · London's first pizza tasting menu*

Seven courses, four of them pizza, in a basement under Wardour Street — the only pizza tasting menu in the city. Around £65 a head, with the wine pairing charged on top. It opens with fritti, then works through a white pizza with asparagus and anchovy colatura before it gets anywhere near a margherita.

> ⚠️ **Book the basement, not the building.** The ground floor is a separate à la carte restaurant and is what you get by default. The tasting menu is downstairs and has to be booked specifically.

## New York: what London is actually cooking now

Thinner, wider, foldable, sold by the slice. Every 2026 list leads with this section, which is a reversal from three years ago.

### Crisp Pizza at The Marlborough, Mayfair

*££ · Mayfair · 4 min from Bond Street · the critics' highest-rated pizza in London*

Carl McCluskey's New Haven-style pizza — charred, thin, deliberately scorched at the edge — moved from Hammersmith into the basement of a relaunched Mayfair pub run by the team behind The Devonshire. All three published guides name it and The Infatuation has rated it higher than any other pizza in the city.

Order the grandma pie: square, thicker, feeds two or three.

> ⚠️ **No pizza on Mondays.** The pub upstairs opens 11:00–23:00 seven days a week; the pizzeria downstairs does not run on Mondays. Pizza is Tue–Thu 11:30–21:30, Fri–Sat to 22:30, Sun to 20:30. The pub is walk-in; the basement books through SevenRooms.

### Alley Cats Pizza, Marylebone

*££ · Marylebone · Paddington Street · named by all three guides*

The only New York-style pizzeria that Time Out, The Infatuation and Country & Town House all name, which makes it the strongest consensus pick in this section. Eighteen-inch pies, Southern Italian ingredients, and a room that behaves like a Brooklyn slice joint rather than a trattoria.

One catch worth knowing: despite the framing, the restaurants sell whole pies rather than slices. A second site trades at 342 King's Road in Chelsea.

### Spring Street Pizza, Borough

*££ · Borough · Arch 32, Southwark Street · from a Michelin-starred chef*

Tom Kemble left fine dining — he held a star at Bonhams — to make eighteen-inch New York slices under a railway arch off O'Meara Street. The dough is a biga fermented past forty-eight hours, which is where the thin, foldable, properly charred base comes from.

A few minutes from Borough Market, London Bridge and the Shard, which makes it the most useful entry on this page for anyone doing the South Bank.

### Vincenzo's, Shoreditch

*£ · Shoreditch · 122 Bethnal Green Road · walk-in only*

Twenty-inch pies cut into slices you eat standing up. This is the closest London gets to an actual New York slice shop rather than a restaurant doing New York pizza, and it is the cheapest serious pizza in this guide.

Slices are grab-and-go from 5pm; whole 16-inch pizzas are takeaway only. The original is in Bushey, outside London.

### Dough Hands, Hackney

*££ · Hackney · 7 min from London Fields*

A residency in the back of The Spurstowe Arms, sitting somewhere between New York and Neapolitan — crisp base, generous toppings, no restaurant attached. Also operates in Dalston and Hackney Wick.

> ⚠️ It runs on the pub's **kitchen** hours, which are shorter than the pub's own. Check before travelling.

## Detroit, Chicago and Romana

The shapes almost nobody else in London attempts.

### Short Road Pizza, Leyton

*££ · Leyton · William The Fourth, 816 High Road · walk-in · National Pizza of the Year 2025*

The most decorated pizza in Britain right now, cooked in the back of a brewery pub in Leyton. Romana bases — thin, long-fermented, crisp right to the edge — with American toppings on them, which is not a combination anyone was making here five years ago.

The SRP Marinara won National Pizza of the Year in 2025 and was ranked the third best pizza in the world by Time Out in February 2026. It has no cheese on it.

> ⚠️ **A residency, not a restaurant.** Short Road cooks inside Exale Brewery's two pubs — William The Fourth in Leyton and Three Colts in Bethnal Green — so the kitchen keeps shorter hours than the bar. Founded by Ugo and Kate on Short Road in Leytonstone, which is where the name comes from.

### Ria's, Notting Hill

*££ · Notting Hill · Detroit-style and natural wine*

Thick, crisp-edged Detroit pies, baked in a rectangular steel pan so the cheese caramelises hard against the sides into what Detroit calls the frico edge. Matched to a natural wine list. Essentially nothing else in London commits to this style.

Founded in 2023 by a husband-and-wife pair who came out of tech. A second site opened in Soho at 29 Foubert's Place with a wine cave downstairs.

### Japes, Soho

*££ · Soho · 22–25 Dean Street*

Chicago deep dish, which almost nothing else in central London attempts — a pie built in a pan deep enough that the cheese pull becomes the entire point of it. Also in Greenwich and Camden.

Deep dish is slow to cook. Expect a wait that a Neapolitan pizzeria would never ask of you, and do not arrive starving.

## Neapolitan you can walk into

Excellent, everywhere, and no planning required. This is the section most people actually need.

### Rudy's Pizza Napoletana, Soho

*££ · Soho · 80 Wardour Street · walk-in · AVPN accredited*

Naples by way of Manchester, and accredited by the Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana — meaning the dough, the flour and the oven all meet the Neapolitan association's own written standard. Very few London pizzerias bother to get that.

The most reliable cheap Neapolitan pizza in central London, with sites on Wardour Street, Shoreditch High Street and Spital Square. Consistent enough that the branch barely matters.

### Yard Sale Pizza, Clapton

*££ · Clapton · 105 Lower Clapton Road · walk-in · named by all three guides*

Started in a back-yard oven in Homerton in 2014 and grew into east London's default good pizza without ever becoming a chain you would avoid. British ingredients, a properly considered vegan menu, and consistent across every branch.

Six London sites: Clapton, East Dulwich, Hackney Road, Leytonstone, Walthamstow and Finsbury Park. Clapton is the original.

### Zia Lucia, Holloway

*££ · Holloway · 7 min from Highbury & Islington*

Four different doughs, including a 48-hour ferment and a charcoal base, which makes this the most useful entry here for anyone avoiding standard wheat flour. Named by both Country & Town House and The Infatuation.

**Gluten-free base available** — though a separate base is not a separate preparation area, so ask if it matters medically.

### Berberè, Stratford

*££ · Stratford · Italian sourdough*

An Italian group doing sourdough bases with crisp bottoms and a light, fluffy rim, split into halves so a table can order two things. Five London locations. Named by both Time Out and Country & Town House.

### Homeslice, Covent Garden

*££ · Covent Garden · 2 min from Covent Garden*

Twenty-inch pizzas sold whole or by the slice, with toppings split across halves. Built for groups rather than couples — two people will struggle and four will do fine. Neal's Yard is the original.

### Pizza Pilgrims, Soho

*££ · Soho · 5 min from Tottenham Court Road · walk-in*

Started as a Soho market cart in 2012 and now runs across London, ranked seventh in the 50 Top World Artisan Pizza Chains list. Reliable rather than remarkable — but genuinely consistent, and you can walk into any of them.

### Santa Maria, Ealing

*££ · Ealing · 7 min from Ealing Broadway*

A small Ealing pizzeria that built its reputation on strict Neapolitan method years before the current wave arrived. Worth knowing if you are anywhere in west London.

![The white frontage of Santa Maria pizzeria at night](../../assets/articles/best-pizza-london/santa-maria.jpg)

## Cheap slices and market stalls

Everything here is under £15 and none of it takes a booking.

### Bad Boy Pizza Society, Covent Garden

*£ · Covent Garden · Seven Dials Market · walk-in · National Pizza of the Year 2022 and 2024*

Twenty-two-inch New York pies sold by the slice from a counter inside Seven Dials Market — and, improbably for a market stall, a two-time National Pizza of the Year winner. It began in 2018 as a parody university society in Southampton set up to get students discounted pizza.

The brand also runs residencies at Vinegar Yard and The Railway in Tulse Hill, and opened its first permanent restaurant, Bad Boy Pizzeria, at 419 Bethnal Green Road in August 2025.

![The two-level hall of Seven Dials Market, with communal benches and festoon lighting](../../assets/articles/best-pizza-london/seven-dials-market.jpg)

*Seven Dials Market, where Bad Boy trades from a counter on the lower level. You eat at the shared benches rather than at the stall.*

### Breadstall, Soho

*£ · Soho · Berwick Street · walk-in*

A pizza slice counter on the Soho market street rather than a dining room. Slow-fermented dough with a crisp bottom and a puffed, almost naan-like crust. A second site trades in Battersea.

### Sud Italia, Spitalfields

*£ · Spitalfields · Old Spitalfields Market · walk-in*

Wood-fired pizza from a stall in Old Spitalfields Market — one of the few genuine market pizzas in central London. Market trading hours only.

### Franco Manca, Soho

*£ · walk-in · the cheapest sourdough in London*

Sourdough pizza at a price nothing else central matches, from an operation that began on a Brixton Market stall in 2008. Around 50 branches across the UK, with a good spread across central London.

## Booking and practical essentials

* **Almost nothing here needs booking.** Rudy's, Pizza Pilgrims, Yard Sale, Vincenzo's, Breadstall, Bad Boy, Sud Italia, Franco Manca and Short Road are all walk-in.
* **Book weeks ahead** for the Napoli on the Road Soho tasting menu, and book the basement specifically.
* **Crisp Pizza is two venues in one.** The pub is walk-in, the basement pizzeria books through SevenRooms — and there is no pizza at all on Mondays.
* **Residencies keep shorter hours than their pubs.** Short Road and Dough Hands both stop serving well before the bar closes.
* **Market stalls trade on market hours.** Bad Boy at Seven Dials and Sud Italia at Old Spitalfields both close when the market does.
* **For gluten-free**, Zia Lucia is the one with a dedicated alternative base. Confirm the preparation separately if it is a medical requirement.
* **Service charge** of 12.5% is discretionary and standard on London restaurant bills, though counters and stalls generally do not add it.
