**January 2027.** London's January deals fall into three kinds: sales with a 2027 date already set, offers that run all year, and sales that ran in January 2026 and may come back.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** In January 2027, **the West End New Year Sale launches on the 5th** at [Official London Theatre](https://officiallondontheatre.com/sale/). **Wicked is £20 off a ticket** on selected seats for Monday to Friday performances from 5 January to 31 March, if you **book by 14 December 2026**. **Renoir and Love at the National Gallery is pay what you wish on Friday evenings to 15 January.** In Bath, **visit the Roman Baths before 31 December and go back in January or February for £1**. **Eurostar Standard fares start at £39 each way** on a return for travel to 14 February, and **LNER's £19 London to Newcastle fares come back from 4 January**. Under-30s and anyone on Universal Credit get the deepest cuts: **£1 at Hampton Court Palace**, **£5 at Tate exhibitions** for 16 to 25s. **LEGOLAND, Chessington and Thorpe Park are shut for most of the month.**

## Theatre in January 2027

**Official London Theatre's New Year Sale launches on 5 January 2027**, with special offers, exclusive ticket prices and dining deals. The show list and closing date go up on the [New Year Sale page](https://officiallondontheatre.com/sale/), which has a mailing list for first notice. The Society of London Theatre runs it; in 2026 it priced selected shows at £10, £20, £30, £40, £50 or £60.

- **[Wicked](https://www.londontheatregeek.co.uk/shows/wicked)**, Apollo Victoria: **£20 off a ticket** on selected seats for Monday to Friday performances from **5 January to 31 March 2027**, booked by 11.59pm on **14 December 2026** through the [ATG Tickets latest-deals page](https://www.atgtickets.com/offers/latest-deals/).
- **Official London Theatre's [Special Offers page](https://officiallondontheatre.com/special-offers/)** collects the current discounts on West End shows, each with its own book-by date. On 23 November 2026 the lowest was [Into the Woods](https://www.londontheatregeek.co.uk/shows/into-the-woods-noel-coward) from £23, up to 52% off.
- **Royal Court Theatre, £15 Mondays**: every seat in both theatres is £15 on a Monday, sold online only from 9am on the day, two per booking ([Royal Court](https://royalcourttheatre.com/visit/box-office-ticketing/ways-to-save/)).
- **Donmar Warehouse, £20 tickets for 16 to 35s**: register an account with your date of birth and join the 35 and under list ([Donmar](https://www.donmarwarehouse.com/your-visit/tickets/35-and-under-20-tickets/)).

London Theatre Geek keeps the [current West End offers](https://www.londontheatregeek.co.uk/guides/theatre-offers) and the [cheap ticket routes](https://www.londontheatregeek.co.uk/guides/cheap-theatre-tickets), from day seats to lotteries.

## Museums and exhibitions in January 2027

The national museums are free all year; see [free things to do in London](/free/). The paid exhibitions have these cuts in January:

- **Renoir and Love, [National Gallery](/articles/national-gallery-guide/)**: **pay what you wish, from £1, every Friday evening to 15 January 2027**, which leaves 8 and 15 January (the gallery is closed on 1 January). Otherwise £20 off-peak Sunday to Thursday, £25 on Friday and Saturday, £15 from 5pm to 6pm Sunday to Thursday, and free for under-18s. The exhibition closes on 31 January 2027 ([National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/renoir-and-love)).
- **National Art Pass**: half price at major exhibitions and free entry to more than 250 places, including £10 off-peak at Renoir and Love. It is a paid annual membership ([Art Fund](https://www.artfund.org/national-art-pass)).
- **[Science Museum](/articles/science-museum-guide/)**: **50% off IMAX documentaries** with a Wonderlab or Power Up ticket for the same day ([Science Museum](https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/visitor-discounts-free-days-offers-savings)).
- **[Hampton Court Palace](/articles/hampton-court-palace-guide/)**: prices from 6 January 2027 are **£30 for an adult on a weekday**, £33 at weekends, £15 and £16.50 for children aged 5 to 15, and free under 5 ([Historic Royal Palaces](https://www.hrp.org.uk/hampton-court-palace/visit/tickets-and-prices/)).

## Attractions and river trips in January 2027

- **[Sky Garden](/articles/sky-garden-guide/)**: **free** with a booked ticket, 10am to 6pm on weekdays and 11am to 9pm at weekends; walk-ins go in if there is space ([Sky Garden](https://skygarden.london/booking/)).
- **[London Eye](/articles/london-eye-guide/) multi-attraction tickets**: **five attractions at £12 each**, multi-attraction tickets from £49, valid for seven days, and student tickets from £17 through Student Beans ([London Eye](https://www.londoneye.com/ticket-deals/discount-offers/)). Madame Tussauds' Spirit of London taxi ride is closed for maintenance from **4 to 15 January 2027**.
- **[SEA LIFE London Aquarium](/articles/sea-life-london-aquarium-guide/)**: **up to 28% off** booked online in advance, five attractions from £59 on a 90-day ticket, students from £16 ([SEA LIFE](https://www.visitsealife.com/london/ticket-deals/discount-offers/)).
- **[London Zoo](/articles/london-zoo-guide/)**: **the cheaper of two tickets is free** with two valid National Rail tickets, pre-booked through the Days Out Guide link ([ZSL](https://www.londonzoo.org/plan-your-visit/london-zoo-offers)).
- **[Tower Bridge](/articles/tower-bridge-guide/)**: **two visitors for one adult price** with a train ticket each, booked as the "National Rail Days Out - Two Visitors" ticket; residents of Southwark, Tower Hamlets and the City pay **£1** with photo ID and proof of address ([Tower Bridge](https://www.towerbridge.org.uk/whats-on/concessions-and-offers)).
- **[Tottenham Hotspur Stadium](/articles/tottenham-hotspur-stadium-travel-guide/) tours**: from £23, **children free with each paying adult**, and 15% off booked in advance ([Tottenham Hotspur Stadium](https://www.tottenhamhotspurstadium.com/attractions/stadium-tour/)).
- **[Uber Boat by Thames Clippers](/articles/how-to-use-london-river-boats/)**: **25% off in the app** and **30% off return tickets online** ([Thames Clippers](https://www.thamesclippers.com/)).
- **Thames River Sightseeing**: **15% off for groups of 15 or more**, taken off at checkout ([Thames River Sightseeing](https://thamesriversightseeing.com/)). More boats in our [Thames river cruises guide](/articles/thames-river-cruises-london/).

The 2FOR1 scheme has more attractions, and January blackout dates: Hampton Court is excluded to 3 January 2027 and Kensington Palace from 1 to 6 January. Our [National Rail 2FOR1 guide](/articles/national-rail-2for1-london-attractions/) has the full list and the rules.

## Day trips in January 2027

> ⚠️ **Closed in January.** [LEGOLAND Windsor](/articles/legoland-windsor-day-trip/) is open on 1 and 2 January 2027 only, and [Chessington](/articles/chessington-world-of-adventures-day-trip/) from 1 to 3 January; both reopen on 11 February. [Thorpe Park](/articles/thorpe-park-day-trip/) reopens on 19 March 2027. [Leeds Castle](/articles/leeds-castle-day-trip/) opens at weekends only from 4 January to 11 February, and the castle itself is shut from 4 to 6 January. [Highclere Castle](/articles/downton-abbey-highclere-castle-day-trip/) has no public opening until March, only booked Wintertime guided tours, and Sudeley Castle in the Cotswolds reopens in March 2027.

- **[Roman Baths, Bath](/articles/bath-day-trip/)**: **visit between 1 November and 31 December 2026, then go back between 1 January and 28 February 2027 for £1**, which includes the Coins, Kings and Conquests exhibition that opens on 8 January. Book the first visit online; the discount code comes in the confirmation email. It is not valid on walk-up tickets, groups or guided tours ([Roman Baths](https://www.romanbaths.co.uk/visit-november-or-december-and-come-back-1-pound)).
- **[Dover Castle](/articles/canterbury-dover-day-trip/)**: **20% off if you arrive by train, bus or bike**, and up to 15% off booked early ([English Heritage](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/dover-castle/)). [Stonehenge](/articles/stonehenge-day-trip/) has the same up to 15% for booking early ([English Heritage](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/stonehenge/)).
- **Warwick Castle**: **up to 46% off** general admission online, valid for 90 days ([Warwick Castle](https://www.warwick-castle.com/offers/discount-offers/)).
- **[Bicester Village](/articles/bicester-village-day-trip/)**: up to 40% off original retail prices every day, up to 70% on selected brands ([Bicester Village](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/bicester-village/en/offers/)).
- **[Evan Evans Tours](https://evanevanstours.com/en-gb/our-tours/special-offers)**: lower prices on selected dates for its Stonehenge, Windsor, Oxford and Bicester trips, shown on the date picker with no code.
- **[Bruges](/articles/bruges-day-trip/)**: the free Winter Glow festival runs to 31 January 2027 ([Visit Bruges](https://www.visitbruges.be/en)).
- **[Paris](/articles/paris-day-trip/)**: the Louvre's free first-Friday evening skips January in 2027, because 1 January is a closed day; the next is Friday 5 February, after 6pm ([Louvre](https://www.louvre.fr/en/visit/hours-admission)).

## Getting there and around in January 2027

- **Eurostar**: **Standard tickets from £39 each way** on a return between London and Paris, Brussels, Lille, Amsterdam or Rotterdam, for travel from 2 December 2026 to 14 February 2027, as listed on 12 November 2026 ([Eurostar](https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en/find-lowest-fares)). [Eurostar Snap](https://snap.eurostar.com/uk-en) sells Standard seats at **up to 50% off** seven days before departure; you pick a time slot and Eurostar picks the train.
- **LNER**: the **£19 London to Newcastle fare** (£12.60 with a Railcard, £9.50 for a child) comes back on selected trains **from 4 January 2027**, and the 04:40 York to King's Cross is from £14 Monday to Thursday ([LNER](https://www.lner.co.uk/news/lner-extends-new-super-low-fares-to-more-destinations-on-its-route/)). Advance tickets booked four weeks ahead save up to 45%; King's Cross to Edinburgh on Monday 4 January 2027 is from £34.40 ([LNER](https://www.lner.co.uk/)). See our [Edinburgh day trip](/articles/edinburgh-day-trip/).
- **National Express**: **100,000 tickets at £7 or less**, for travel to 11 January 2027, with London to Cheltenham from £5 one way. A Coachcard takes a third off, and there is no booking fee when logged in ([National Express](https://www.nationalexpress.com/en/offers/cheap-coach-tickets)).
- **Railcards** take a third off adult fares; the 16-25 and Two Together cards are £35 a year, and the Family & Friends card gives 60% off children aged 5 to 15 ([Railcard](https://www.railcard.co.uk/)).
- **Train tickets as attraction discounts**: [LNER's Discount Days Out](https://www.lner.co.uk/tickets/offers-and-discounts/discount-days-out/), [Lumo](https://www.lumo.co.uk/plan-your-journey/2-for-1-offers), [East Midlands Railway](https://www.eastmidlandsrailway.co.uk/discover/offers), [South Western Railway](https://www.southwesternrailway.com/destinations-and-offers/2for1) and [Stansted Express](https://www.stanstedexpress.com/tickets-fares/offers) all give 2-for-1 or money off London attractions with their tickets. Stansted Express also takes up to 45% off for groups of three or four.
- **[Heathrow Express](/articles/heathrow-airport-to-london/)**: **10% off your first purchase** in the app after creating an account, Standard Class only ([Heathrow Express](https://www.heathrowexpress.com/offers)).
