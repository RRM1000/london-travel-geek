**This guide covers the bars and pubs in London built around watching live sport**: big screens, a fixtures list, and tables you can book for the match.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** Saturday 3pm Premier League kick-offs aren't shown on UK TV, so no pub will be showing that match. For everything else, the most-named sports bars are the **Greenwood** in Victoria (30-plus screens and a 150-inch LED wall, sound on), **BOX Piccadilly** (33 screens over three floors, opened October 2025), **Big Penny Social** in Walthamstow (a beer hall for 700), **Brigadiers** (Indian barbecue in the City), **Belushi's** and **Boxpark**. Time Out ranks **The Hercules Pillars** in Holborn the best football pub, and named **Café Kick** the best bar in London in 2026. **Book for any big match**: most of these take online bookings, and some seat you by the screen showing your game only if you name it when you book.

> 📊 **The evidence behind this guide.**
> **22 independent sources carrying 563 citations** across **370 named venues**. **90 venues are named by two or more independent sources.**
> **Built on:** the Great British Pub Awards' Best Pub to Watch Sport (London finalists, 2023–2025); Time Out's ranked football and rugby lists and its 50 Best Bars; DesignMyNight, the Evening Standard, SquareMeal and The Infatuation; Londonist, Secret London, Hot Dinners and London blogs; supporters' sites; and YouTube and TikTok.
> *Evidence built 5 November 2026 · [How we rank →](/how-we-rank/)*

## Booking at a glance

* **Book online, and name your match:** Sport London's six pubs, BOX Piccadilly, Big Penny Social, Brigadiers, Bloodsports and The Side Hustle. At Famous Three Kings put your game in the Special Requests box; at Pub on the Park choose the sports booking type, or you won't be in view of a screen.
* **Buy a ticket:** Boxpark sells entry to big screenings through Eventbrite, with no refunds.
* **Walk in:** Café Kick takes no bookings and is small, so arrive early. Bloodsports keeps space for walk-ins but recommends booking for big fixtures.
* **Watch the hours:** Vinegar Yard opens Wednesday to Saturday only, and Brigadiers closes between 2.45pm and 5pm Monday to Wednesday.

### Sport London: six pubs, Greenwood in Victoria is the flagship

*[Book a table](https://www.sportlondon.com/book) · official NFL watch-party bars*

Sport London runs six sports pubs with the same set-up: big screens, a fixtures list on its site, and online booking by match. The NFL lists all six as official UK watch-party bars, and Sport London's own pages list the NFL, F1, cricket (the Ashes, the T20 Blast) and AFL, with "surround sound" for the Aussie rules.

- **Greenwood**, 170 Victoria Street, SW1E 5LB · Cited by 7 sources · 30-plus HD screens and a 150-inch LED wall over two floors, matches shown with the sound on; open Monday to Saturday 11am to midnight. Londonist warns it "gets fearsomely busy on big match days".
- **Beechwood**, 1A Principal Place, Worship Street, Shoreditch, EC2A 2FA · Cited by 4 sources · #31 of 42, Time Out football pubs · a terrace as well as the screens inside.
- **Westwood**, Westfield London, Ariel Way, W12 7HB · Cited by 3 sources · a 145-inch LED wall and a terrace.
- **Redwood**, London Bridge Station, SE1 9SP · Cited by 3 sources · a 145-inch LED wall and a terrace, inside the station.
- **Goldwood**, 30–32 Old Jewry, Bank, EC2R 8DQ · Cited by 2 sources.
- **Broadwood**, 25 Old Broad Street, Liverpool Street, EC2N 1HN · Cited by 1 source · "full sound for key fixtures".

### Big Penny Social, Walthamstow — a beer hall for 700

*1 Priestley Way, E17 6AL · Cited by 7 sources · [book a table](https://bigpennysocial.co.uk/book-now)*

A beer hall near Blackhorse Road station with a capacity of 700 and tables bookable for groups of up to 60. The NFL held its official Super Bowl LX party here in February 2026, and it opens Time Out's list for watching the PDC World Darts Championship. The Evening Standard writes of "booming sound" and "a mix of families" on match days.

It closes at 8pm on Sundays, so a Sunday evening kick-off is out; it is open 12pm to 11pm Monday to Thursday and until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays.

### Brigadiers, Bank — Indian barbecue with screens

*1–5 Bloomberg Arcade, EC4N 8AR · Cited by 7 sources · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/brigadiers/reservations/create/search/)*

An Indian barbecue restaurant that doubles as a sports bar: private rooms for 6 to 25 with their own screens and self-pour beer taps, and a partly covered terrace with screens. On Saturdays a sports menu runs from 12pm to 4pm. Time Out sent darts fans here, the Evening Standard sent rugby and World Cup fans, and The Infatuation lists it for big groups.

It closes between 2.45pm and 5pm Monday to Wednesday, so check a weekday kick-off against the hours.

### Belushi's London Bridge — the Dugout

*161–165 Borough High Street, SE1 · Cited by 7 sources · #41 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [Belushi's site](https://belushis.com/london-bridge)*

A bar chain with branches across London; the London Bridge site has the Dugout, a basement sports room seating 125 (250 standing) with multiple screens and two large projectors. It is open until 2am Sunday to Thursday and 3am on Fridays and Saturdays, the latest of any bar here. Londonist's tip: "They're strict with IDs, so bring one."

### Boxpark Shoreditch, Wembley and Croydon — ticketed screenings

*Shoreditch: 2–10 Bethnal Green Road, E1 6GY · Cited by 7 sources · [fixtures and tickets](https://www.boxpark.co.uk/sports-bar)*

Food halls built from shipping containers, with big screens over communal tables. Boxpark sells entry tickets for its big screenings through Eventbrite, marks each fixture "Sound on", and shows football, rugby, F1, NFL and boxing. The Evening Standard calls it "the first point of call for many football fans in London"; Boxpark Wembley sits a few hundred feet from the stadium.

Tickets are non-refundable, and Shoreditch takes table bookings only for six or more.

### The Faltering Fullback, Finsbury Park — the Irish pub both Time Out lists rank second

*19 Perth Road, N4 3HB · Cited by 6 sources · #2 of 42, Time Out football pubs · #2 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.falteringfullback.com/)*

A family-run Irish pub on a residential side street off Stroud Green Road, its front covered in vines. Inside it is "cluttered with gimcrack and TVs" (Londonist); out back is a garden on several levels with outdoor screens, and a room called the Sin Bin. The kitchen serves Thai food. It is a regular stop on Arsenal matchday crawls, filmed by the YouTube channel Hannah and Martin on the Globe, and the home pub of London's Limerick GAA community.

### Flat Iron Square, London Bridge — screens inside and out

*45 Southwark Street, SE1 9HP · Cited by 6 sources · #3 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [book a table](https://flatironsquare.co.uk/book-a-table)*

A yard of railway arches and street-food stalls under the Shard that calls itself "London's home of sport", with a large heated outdoor screen. Londonist counts 19 screens showing every Sky Sports and TNT Sports game, inside and out; the Evening Standard says it was "outrageously busy" during Euro 2024. The food is from the traders: Greek souvlaki, Korean fried chicken and New York-style pizza slices.

## The award finalists and the Time Out winners

The Great British Pub Awards' Best Pub to Watch Sport went to pubs in Nantwich (2026), Leeds (2025) and Norwich (2024), and the 2026 shortlist had no London pub. Two recent London finalists are below, then Time Out's two number ones.

### The Minories, Tower Hill — 2025 finalist

*64–73 Minories, EC3N 1JL · Cited by 2 sources · Great British Pub Awards finalist, Best Pub to Watch Sport, 2025 · [the pub's site](https://www.socialpubandkitchen.co.uk/pubs/minories-london)*

A pub under the railway arches by Tower Hill, showing Sky Sports and TNT Sports across numerous plasma screens and two large projector screens, plus outdoor screens in a heated beer garden. The TikTok channel Historic Pub Crawls says it has "the most amount of TVs I've ever seen outside of a US sports bar".

### Directors Box, Canary Wharf — 2023 finalist

*1 Crossrail Place, E14 5AR · Cited by 2 sources · Great British Pub Awards finalist, Best Pub to Watch Sport, 2023 (as Sports Bar & Grill Canary Wharf) · [the pub's site](https://www.socialpubandkitchen.co.uk/pubs/directors-box-canary-wharf)*

A sports bar in the Crossrail Place building at Canary Wharf, with HD screens, Sky Sports, TNT Sports, LaLiga TV and NFL Game Pass, plus a dartboard and pool table. It was the 2023 finalist under its old name.

### The Hercules Pillars, Holborn — Time Out's best football pub

*18 Great Queen Street, WC2B 5DG · Cited by 2 sources · #1 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.herculespillars.co.uk/)*

A pub on Great Queen Street since 1703, with a gilded Hercules over the bar and Greek-style sculptures, a projector and four HD screens. Its fixtures list marks each match's sound, and many go out "Sound Off", so a quiet pint over the football is possible here. There is a function room for 100.

### Café Kick, Exmouth Market — Time Out's best bar in London, 2026

*43 Exmouth Market, EC1R 4QL · Cited by 3 sources · #1 of 50, Time Out 50 Best Bars · #17 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [Café Kick's site](https://www.cafekick.co.uk/)*

A small continental-style café-bar, open since 1997, with table football and screens showing the football. Time Out made it number one of its 50 Best Bars in London for 2026. Most matches are shown without sound.

It takes no bookings ("first come first served"), is too small for large groups, and is open until 11pm every day. Arrive early for a seat.

## New sports bars, opened 2025 and 2026

### BOX Piccadilly, Shaftesbury Avenue — 33 screens over three floors

*21–23 Shaftesbury Avenue, W1D 7EF · Cited by 7 sources · opened October 2025 · [book a table](https://www.theboxbar.co.uk/bars/london-piccadilly/bookings)*

The London branch of Box, a sports bar chain from the north of England, opened in October 2025 by Piccadilly Circus: 33 screens across three floors and 7,200 square feet, room for 550 with seats for 250. It is the NFL's official UK sports bar partner and shows NFL Sundays and the Super Bowl, with commentary on key fixtures. Electric darts and shuffleboard are bookable by the hour.

Seven sources name it within a year of opening, level with the most-cited bars. Book for NFL nights and big football; it is open 12pm to midnight on weekdays.

### Bloodsports, Covent Garden — sport and horror films until 2am

*27–29 Endell Street, WC2H 9BA · Cited by 6 sources · #37 of 50, Time Out 50 Best Bars · opened February 2025 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/bloodsports/reservations/create/search/)*

From the team behind MeatLiquor: 30-plus screens, live sport from around the world on some and cult horror films on others. The nine screens in the main bar never have commentary, but other areas play sound on request when you book. It is open every day from 12pm to 2am.

It keeps space for walk-ins but "strongly recommend[s] booking or showing up early for big fixtures", and authorises your card when you book. The YouTube channel Axl and Sean reviewed it unsponsored in May 2026.

### The Side Hustle, St Paul's — opened June 2026

*130 Wood Street, EC2V 6DL · Cited by 1 source · opened 5 June 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sidehustlesportsbar.co.uk/book)*

A City sports bar with "massive screens", wings, and a fixtures list that marks each match "Sound on". Hot Dinners listed it for the 2026 World Cup. Happy hour runs 4pm to 9pm on weekdays.

Check the hours for a late game: it shuts at 10pm on Mondays and 11pm on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and midnight Thursday to Saturday.

### LaCage, Kensington — Montréal's sports brasserie

*201–203 Kensington High Street, W8 6BA · opened autumn 2026 · [book a table](https://lacage.uk/restaurants/london/booking)*

The first UK branch of LaCage, a Quebec sports-brasserie chain with more than 35 sites: a sit-down restaurant with live sport on big screens, burgers and sharing plates, and private rooms. It is open from 9am to 11.30pm, and 10am to 10.30pm on Sunday.

## By sport

### Football

Famous Three Kings, the Duke of Edinburgh, Pub on the Park and the Leyton Star are all on Time Out's football list.

#### Famous Three Kings, West Kensington — 30-plus screens

*171 North End Road, W14 9NL · Cited by 4 sources · #23 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.famousthreekings.co.uk/)*

A pub rebuilt in 1902 and now given over to sport, with more than 30 UHD screens showing Sky Sports and TNT Sports. When you book, write your match in the Special Requests box, so you're seated in view of the right screen. It's open 12pm to 11pm. The Evening Standard calls it the pub for Wales fans.

#### The Duke of Edinburgh, Brixton — the garden screen with no commentary

*204 Ferndale Road, SW9 8AG · Cited by 4 sources · #21 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.dukeofedinburghpub.com/)*

A Brixton pub with a huge beer garden, a projector and seven big screens inside and out. Commentary plays only indoors, never in the garden. Book early: the pub takes card details for groups, charges them only if you don't arrive, and fills bookings first come, first served. It opens at 4pm on Mondays.

#### Pub on the Park, London Fields — three decks over the park

*19 Martello Street, E8 3PE · Cited by 5 sources · #20 of 42, Time Out football pubs · #16 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.pubonthepark.com/)*

A pub on the edge of London Fields with three decks, screens, heaters and a projector. When you book, choose the sports booking type, or you won't be seated in view of a screen. On spring and summer Saturdays it takes one table per booking (up to ten people), with the last booking at 3pm.

#### The Leyton Star, Leyton — group bookings for fan groups

*116 High Road, Leyton, E15 2BX · Cited by 3 sources · #22 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [fan group bookings](https://leytonstar.co.uk/fan-group-bookings/)*

A big East London pub with a large projector and smaller screens, outdoor screens in spring and summer (Londonist), and a booking page for supporters' groups. It's open until 11pm.

#### The Prince, West Brompton — The Loft

*14 Lillie Road, SW6 1TT · Cited by 4 sources · [the pub's site](https://www.theprincelondon.com/)*

A Fulham pub with an indoor garden on the ground floor and The Loft, a live sports bar, upstairs. DJs play on Fridays and there is a bottomless brunch on Saturdays, so expect a party crowd at weekend matches.

### Rugby

The Six Nations runs from February to March, and the autumn internationals in November. Flat Iron Square, Brigadiers and the Faltering Fullback, above, are all on Time Out's rugby list.

#### The Ship, Wandsworth — riverside, 13 screens

*41 Jews Row, SW18 1TB · Cited by 5 sources · #8 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.theship.co.uk/)*

A Young's pub on the Thames, showing international rugby on screens inside and out; the Evening Standard counted 13, including a cinema screen, for the World Cup. Time Out mentions cabins under a wooden pergola outside. Book for a Six Nations weekend.

#### The Alma, Wandsworth — a Victorian rugby pub

*499 Old York Road, SW18 1TF · Cited by 3 sources · #13 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.almawandsworth.com/)*

A large Victorian Young's pub by Wandsworth Town station, built around a low island bar, which calls itself an "iconic sports pub". It has bedrooms upstairs.

#### The Orange Tree, Richmond — on the way to Twickenham

*45 Kew Road, TW9 2NQ · Cited by 3 sources · #6 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.orangetreerichmond.co.uk/)*

A Young's pub near Richmond station, a stop for pre-match food on the way to [Twickenham](/articles/twickenham-stadium-travel-guide/) and for watching away games on its large screens. The Evening Standard names it the pub for Scotland fans.

### NFL and American sports

BOX Piccadilly and the Sport London pubs are the NFL's official UK bars. The Super Bowl is on a Sunday night in February, UK time; Time Out, the Evening Standard and London Cheapo all list Super Bowl nights; for the London games themselves, see our [NFL London games guide](/articles/nfl-london-games/).

#### Passyunk Avenue — Philadelphia bars in four places

*Fitzrovia, Waterloo (Leake Street), Battersea and Kensington · Cited by 5 sources · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/passyunkgroup/reservations/create/search/)*

A Philly-themed dive bar chain serving cheesesteaks, wings, hoagies and Pabst Blue Ribbon, with big screens and a giant projection screen showing baseball, basketball and American football. Passyunk Play in Waterloo adds baseball batting cages. Londonist calls it a "baseball and basketball dive bar".

#### Long Arm Pub & Brewery, Shoreditch — two giant projectors

*20–26 Worship Street, EC2A 2DX · Cited by 3 sources · #9 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [book a table](https://www.longarmpub.co.uk/bookings/)*

A brewpub that brews its beer on site and pours it from tanks, at £5 a pint all day. Sport goes out on two giant projectors and other big screens: football, rugby, the NFL, boxing and F1. It's open every day 12pm to midnight.

### F1, cricket and AFL

Sport London lists F1, with burger specials on Grand Prix weekends, cricket (the Ashes, the Cricket World Cup, the T20 Blast) and AFL at its six pubs. DesignMyNight's F1 list adds BOX Piccadilly, Beechwood and Long Arm.

### Darts and boxing

Time Out's darts list for the 2026 PDC World Championship puts **Big Penny Social** and **Brigadiers** first and second, then Sheephaven Bay in Camden, the King's Head in Tooting and the Green Man in Soho. BOX Piccadilly has its own electric darts oches bookable by the hour. For boxing, Boxpark lists "the biggest boxing match ups" among its screenings, and Long Arm shows boxing.

## Watch your home team

These are pubs that a source or the venue itself ties to one country's fans or one club's.

### Ireland

The Faltering Fullback (above) is the Irish family pub tied to London's Limerick GAA community.

#### The Porterhouse, Covent Garden — Irish-owned, 18 screens

*21–22 Maiden Lane, WC2E 7NA · Cited by 3 sources · #15 of 16, Time Out rugby pubs · [book a table](https://porterhouse.london/bookings/)*

Irish-owned and founded in Dublin, with its Covent Garden pub open since 2000: a building of several bars and floors, a terrace, and 18 screens around the venue. It shows all major sporting events "particularly rugby"; Londonist says it shows hurling too, and DesignMyNight lists it for the GAA finals. It takes groups of 10 to 100. It closes at 10pm on Sundays.

#### Gibney's, Old Street — the Irish basement bar

*Basement, 70–74 City Road, EC1Y 2BJ · Cited by 2 sources · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/experiences/daffodilmulligan/gibneys-london-bookings-4936143358803968)*

An Irish bar in the basement under Daffodil Mulligan, with live sport on five big screens, private booths and Irish and local live music Thursday to Saturday. The Evening Standard lists it for Irish rugby fans.

### Scotland and Wales

The Evening Standard sends Scotland fans to **The Orange Tree** in Richmond and Wales fans to **Famous Three Kings** in West Kensington, both above.

### The Netherlands

- **De Hems**, 11 Macclesfield Street, W1D 5BW · Cited by 2 sources · #19 of 42, Time Out football pubs · on the edge of Chinatown, it calls itself "London's one and only authentic Dutch pub", and Time Out sends Netherlands supporters here. Londonist calls it a "bustling multi-level pub"; it is open 11am to midnight Monday to Saturday, and 12pm to 10.30pm on Sunday.

### Canada and the USA

#### The Maple Leaf, Covent Garden — the Canadian bar

*41 Maiden Lane, WC2E 7LJ · Cited by 2 sources · #32 of 42, Time Out football pubs · [the pub's site](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/pubs/greater-london/maple-leaf)*

A Canadian pub open since 1986, with maple leaves, ice hockey shirts and baseball memorabilia on the walls and 14 screens showing Sky Sports and TNT Sports. It shows ice hockey and other North American sports, and football too. Book a table for a big game.

LaCage in Kensington (Montréal) and Passyunk Avenue (Philadelphia) are above.

### Australia and New Zealand

Sport London shows AFL, with surround sound, at its six pubs.

### Supporters' pubs

#### Kings Arms, Fulham — Liverpool Supporters Club London

*425 New Kings Road, SW6 4RN · [book a table](https://www.kingsarms-fulham.co.uk/make-a-booking)*

An official London venue of the Liverpool FC Supporters Club (LFC London), a Wadworth pub showing every available Liverpool match on Sky Sports, TNT Sports and Amazon Prime Video across ten HD screens. Like every UK pub, it does not show Saturday 3pm games.

#### The Sporting Page, Chelsea — a Chelsea sports pub

*6 Camera Place, SW10 0BH · Cited by 3 sources · [reservations](https://www.thesportingpage.co.uk/reservations)*

A corner pub on Camera Place that calls itself "your Chelsea sports pub", with screens showing Sky Sports and TNT Sports and a page for Chelsea FC fixtures. Open 11am to 11pm Monday to Saturday and 12pm to 10pm on Sunday.

For Arsenal, the Hannah and Martin on the Globe matchday crawl starts at the Faltering Fullback. For a ticket to the match itself, see our [Premier League tickets guide](/articles/premier-league-tickets-london/).

## Outdoor and big screens

Flat Iron Square, the Duke of Edinburgh, Pub on the Park, The Minories and The Ship, all above, have screens outdoors; Sport London's Beechwood, Redwood and Westwood have terraces.

### Hope & Anchor, Brixton — screens in private garden huts

*123 Acre Lane, SW2 5UA · Cited by 3 sources · [the pub's site](https://www.hopeandanchorbrixton.co.uk/)*

A Young's pub on Acre Lane with a large beer garden of more than ten tiki-style huts, showing all live sport; for the November 2026 autumn internationals it is screening every big fixture from 6 to 29 November. DesignMyNight describes the huts as private viewing huts, so book one for a big match. Food is served from 12pm to 9pm, and DJs play on Friday and Saturday nights.

### Vinegar Yard, London Bridge — the yard by the station

*72–82 St Thomas Street, SE1 · Cited by 5 sources · [book a table](https://www.vinegaryard.london/reservations)*

An open-air yard of street food and bars beside London Bridge station that puts up big screens for tournaments; the Evening Standard and Secret London listed it for the Six Nations and the World Cup. In November 2026 it opens Wednesday to Saturday only, so it is closed for Sunday and Monday matches.

### KERB Sports Bar, Spitalfields — terrace and private rooms

*SP1A, Old Spitalfields Market, 4 Horner Square, E1 6EW · Cited by 5 sources · [book a table](https://kerbsportsbar.com/book-a-table/)*

The KERB street-food people's sports bar, formerly KERB Social Club: big screens in a clubhouse bar for 50, an upstairs terrace for 120 with air hockey, a pool table and arcade machines, five private rooms with their own screens, and Banjax Burger in the kitchen. Each fixture on its listings has its own booking button. It opens at 4.30pm on weekdays, so a lunchtime kick-off is out.

### Freight Brixton — the rooftop

*33 Brixton Station Road, SW9 8PB · Cited by 3 sources · [Freight's site](https://www.freightbrixton.com/)*

A music and street-food venue by Brixton station, with a rooftop, that calls itself "South London's newest home of sport", screening football and rugby. Former England flanker James Haskell hosted its 2026 Six Nations final-day party, the Evening Standard reported. Freight also runs ticketed club nights and Bongo's Bingo, so check its listings for a clash and book a table for the match.

## Before you go

- **Saturday 3pm kick-offs aren't on UK TV.** Pubs show the 12.30pm and 5.30pm Saturday games and the Sunday and midweek matches.
- **Name your match when you book.** Pubs with many screens show several games at once, and some seat you by screen.
- **Check the sound.** Fixtures lists at Boxpark, The Side Hustle and The Hercules Pillars say whether each match is sound on or off.
- **Bring ID.** Belushi's is strict about it.
- **Check the closing time against the kick-off.** American games run late at night UK time, and several bars here close at 10pm or 11pm on weeknights.
