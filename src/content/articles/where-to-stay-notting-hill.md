Notting Hill and Bayswater are one stretch of stucco streets along the north side of Kensington Gardens, from Lancaster Gate in the east to Portobello Road in the west. Notting Hill is where the boutique hotels are. Bayswater, ten minutes' walk east, is where most of the rooms are and where the prices drop.

The Central line runs under the whole strip, stopping at Notting Hill Gate, Queensway and Lancaster Gate, and Paddington, for Heathrow, is one stop from Bayswater on the Circle line. What decides where you sleep is the market, the park and one weekend in August.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** For Notting Hill itself, **[The Laslett](hotel:the-laslett)** is about £240 in five listed townhouses a minute from Notting Hill Gate station. **[The Portobello Hotel](hotel:the-portobello-hotel)** is about £260 for the round beds and freestanding baths, but every room takes two people only. On a budget, **Vancouver Hotel and Studios** on Prince's Square lists studios with kitchenettes from £97. With children, **[Royal Lancaster London](hotel:royal-lancaster-london)** sells interconnecting family rooms opposite Kensington Gardens. For the top end, **Six Senses London** is about £700 inside the old Whiteleys department store. And check the calendar: **Notting Hill Carnival is on 29 and 30 August 2027**.

## Which part of Notting Hill and Bayswater

**Notting Hill Gate and Pembridge** is the main road and the streets just north of it. The station is here, Portobello Road starts at its top end, and The Laslett, Ruby Zoe and The Lost Poet are all within five minutes of the platforms.

**Portobello and the Ladbroke estate** is the grid of garden squares either side of Portobello Road. It is residential and quiet at night; the market fills the road on Fridays and Saturdays. The Portobello Hotel is here.

**Westbourne Grove and the Bayswater squares** runs east from Portobello to Queensway: Prince's Square, Kensington Gardens Square and Hereford Road. This is where the hostels, the studios and the small independent hotels are, three to six minutes from Bayswater station.

**Queensway and Inverness Terrace** is Bayswater's high street, with restaurants open late, the rebuilt Whiteleys at the top and Kensington Gardens at the bottom. Queensway and Bayswater stations are three minutes apart on different lines.

**Lancaster Gate** is the eastern edge, facing the park across Bayswater Road. It is the nearest part to Paddington and the furthest from Portobello Road, about twenty minutes' walk.

Our [Notting Hill area guide](/articles/notting-hill-area-guide/) covers the market days and where to eat, and the [colourful houses walk](/articles/notting-hill-colourful-houses/) maps the pastel streets.

### The Laslett — five listed townhouses by the station

*About £240 · 8 Pembridge Gardens, W2 4DU · Notting Hill Gate 1 min · Breakfast extra · [Hotels.com](hotel:the-laslett)*

Five Grade II-listed Victorian townhouses on the street beside the station. It is named after Rhaune Laslett, the community activist whose street festival helped start the Carnival, and the bar, The Henderson, after Russell Henderson, the Trinidadian steel pan player who led the band through it. The walls carry work by local artists, including Brian David Stevens's photographs of Carnival sound systems.

## Hotels by Portobello Road

### The Portobello Hotel — the round bed in Room 16

*About £260 · 22 Stanley Gardens, W11 2NG · Notting Hill Gate 10 min · [Hotels.com](hotel:the-portobello-hotel)*

Open since 1971 on a residential street of the Ladbroke estate, west of Portobello Road, and long used by musicians. The hotel repeats the story that Johnny Depp and Kate Moss once filled the bath in Room 16 with champagne.

Rooms are graded Good (15 square metres, at the front on the ground and second floors) through Better, Great, Marvellous and Outstanding to the **Best Room, 36 square metres with a round bed and a freestanding Victorian bath**. There is an honesty bar in the drawing room, and breakfast runs 7.30 to 10.30am, until 11 at weekends, downstairs or in your room.

## Notting Hill Carnival

**Carnival 2027 is on Sunday 29 and Monday 30 August**, the August bank holiday weekend. The UK National Panorama steel band competition, which needs a ticket, is on Saturday 28 August. Sunday is the family day, with J'ouvert from 6am and the children's parade; Monday is the adults' parade. Sound systems play from noon to 7pm on both days. The [official Carnival site](https://nhcarnival.org/carnival-info) publishes the full schedule.

At the 2026 Carnival, TfL ran these station restrictions on both days:

- **Ladbroke Grove**: closed all day.
- **Notting Hill Gate**: no entry from 11am to 6pm.
- **Westbourne Park**: no entry from 11am.
- **Queensway**: exit only from 11am to 6pm.
- **Royal Oak**: no entry from 11am to 6pm, then closed.
- **Holland Park**: no entry from 11am to 3pm, then closed.

TfL pointed visitors to Paddington instead, with signed walking routes to the Carnival. Road closures ran until 6am on the Tuesday, with traffic diverted onto a ring route including Holland Park Avenue and Kensington Church Street.

For where to sleep, that means:

- **The Notting Hill hotels are in the crowd.** The Laslett, Ruby Zoe, The Lost Poet and The Portobello Hotel are minutes from the route, and their nearest station has no entry all afternoon.
- **For a quiet night, book Lancaster Gate.** The Columbia and the Royal Lancaster are about twenty minutes' walk from Portobello Road, and Lancaster Gate station was not on TfL's restriction list.
- **Book early and read the minimum stay.** The Portobello Hotel may require three nights over a bank holiday, and Space Apart takes two nights at any time.
- **If you are not coming for Carnival, avoid the weekend.** The streets close and shops board up.

## Budget rooms in Bayswater

The side streets between Queensway and Paddington are lined with small hotels in converted townhouses, and the rooms vary from building to building. Before booking on price, check three things:

- **The room size in square metres.** Singles at The Columbia start at 10 and at Inhabit at 8.
- **Whether there is a lift.** Vancouver has none, and neither does The Lost Poet.
- **Whether the bathroom is in the room.** Garden Court sells a single with its bathroom outside it, and at Urbany every bathroom is shared.

For a private room on a budget, Vancouver's studios start at £97 and Space Apart's at about £140, both with kitchens. Our [budget hotels guide](/articles/best-budget-hotels-london/) covers the cheapest options across London, and our [aparthotels guide](/articles/aparthotels-london/) compares studios with kitchens.

## Getting in and out

**Notting Hill Gate** has the Central, Circle and District lines. The Central line runs direct to Oxford Circus in five stops, and on to Tottenham Court Road, Bank and Liverpool Street.

**Queensway** and **Lancaster Gate** are Central line only; **Bayswater** is Circle and District. **Ladbroke Grove** and **Westbourne Park**, on the Circle and Hammersmith & City lines, serve the north end of Portobello Road.

**King's Cross St Pancras**, for the Eurostar, is direct on the Circle line from Notting Hill Gate and Bayswater.

**Heathrow:** the Heathrow Express, 15 minutes, or the Elizabeth line to Paddington, then the Circle or District line one stop to Bayswater or two to Notting Hill Gate. From Lancaster Gate, walk to Paddington.
