Bloomsbury combines garden squares and the British Museum with London's most useful long-distance rail district at King's Cross and St Pancras.

> **Best for:** museum lovers, Eurostar, families, practical transport and quieter evenings. **Think twice if:** your priority is luxury shopping or walking home from every West End theatre.

## Best things to do

- Visit the British Museum with a focused object list.
- Explore Bloomsbury's squares and literary history.
- Walk to the British Library.
- See the restored St Pancras station and Coal Drops Yard.
- Follow Regent's Canal towards Camden.

## Transport and airport fit

King's Cross St Pancras has six Underground lines, national trains and Eurostar. The Piccadilly line runs directly from Heathrow; Thameslink serves Gatwick and Luton Airport Parkway. Euston is nearby for west-coast trains.

Avoid planning a five-minute transfer between separate mainline stations. Corridors and entrances are long.
