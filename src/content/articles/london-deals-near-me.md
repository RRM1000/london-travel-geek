> 💡 **The Short Version:** Type a station or press **Use my location**, choose a day, and the finder lists every deal with a branch nearby, nearest first: happy hours, set menus, bottomless brunches, kids-eat-free offers, cinemas, gyms and games. Each result gives the offer, its days and times, the conditions and the walk from where you are. Chains count at their nearest branch.

*Deals leave the finder the day they end. Every one is also listed on its own page, linked from each result.*

## Using the results

- **Distance** is a straight line from the station or your location to the nearest branch, and the walking time allows for streets that don't run straight. Both are a guide for choosing, not a route.
- **Days** come from each offer's own terms. An offer that names no weekday, such as a birthday meal or a school-holiday deal, shows on every day: read its line for when it applies.
- **Conditions** such as booking, a minimum spend or a code to show are on each result. The venue's link goes to the page that sets out the offer.
