April 2027 starts in the second week of the Easter school holidays and ends with the biggest weekend of the London sporting spring: a two-day Marathon on the same Saturday as the FA Cup semi-finals at Wembley. The Boat Race is in between, on 11 April, and Rembrandt, Ink and a Barbican fashion show open. Easter 2027 falls in March, so there is no April bank holiday.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **The Easter school holidays run to Friday 9 April**; Easter Sunday was 28 March, so April has no bank holiday. **The Boat Race is on Sunday 11 April**, with races at **3.40pm and 4.40pm**, free from the bank. **The London Marathon runs over two days, Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 April**, on the same weekend as the **FA Cup semi-finals** at Wembley, so book a hotel early. **Vaisakhi on the Square** is in Trafalgar Square on **Saturday 17 April**, free, and the **Ideal Home Show** is at Olympia from **2 to 11 April**. **Rembrandt** opens at the Royal Academy on **9 April**; **Van Eyck** at the National Gallery and **Vanessa Bell & Duncan Grant** at Tate Britain close on **11 April**. **Bayeux Tapestry tickets for April to July** are due for release in early 2027.

## The end of the Easter holidays

**Easter Sunday was 28 March, so April 2027 has no bank holiday**; the next is Monday 3 May. The Easter school holidays in [Camden](https://www.camden.gov.uk/term-dates), Lambeth and Royal Greenwich run to **Friday 9 April**, and the summer term starts on Monday 12 April. The first nine days of the month are school-holiday busy at the family attractions; the second week is quieter. Our [London with children guide](/articles/london-with-children/) has what to book ahead.

## The Boat Race — Sunday 11 April

**[The Boat Race](https://www.theboatrace.org/) is on Sunday 11 April 2027, with races at 3.40pm and 4.40pm**, on the Thames from Putney to Mortlake, in the centenary year of the Women's Boat Race. It is free to watch from either bank. Putney and Hammersmith Bridge are the crowded spots and the towpath pubs fill hours ahead; Barnes Bridge, near the finish, is quieter. The rest of the year's free sport is in [London sporting events 2027](/articles/london-sporting-events-2027/).

## The London Marathon — Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 April

**For the first and only time, the [TCS London Marathon](https://www.londonmarathonevents.co.uk/london-marathon) runs over two days, Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 April 2027**, with 100,000 runners across the weekend on the course from Greenwich to The Mall. It is free to watch from anywhere along the route. Where to stand along the course is in our [London Marathon guide](/articles/london-marathon-guide/).

> ⚠️ **Saturday 24 April is the busiest day of the month.** Marathon day one is the same weekend as the FA Cup semi-finals at Wembley, so hotels across the centre and east fill early. Book a room as soon as your dates are fixed.

## The FA Cup semi-finals — weekend of 24 April

**Both FA Cup semi-finals are at Wembley on the weekend of Saturday 24 April.** Tickets go on sale through the clubs once the ties are known ([The FA](https://www.thefa.com/competitions/thefacup/round-dates)). Our [Wembley Stadium travel guide](/articles/wembley-stadium-arena-guide/) covers the walk from Wembley Park and the queues after the match.

## Festivals and shows

### Vaisakhi on the Square — Saturday 17 April

**[Vaisakhi on the Square](https://www.london.gov.uk/events/vaisakhi-square-2027) is in Trafalgar Square on Saturday 17 April, from 12pm to 6pm**, the Mayor of London's free celebration of Sikh culture and the founding of the Khalsa. There are performances and a parade, langar cooking demonstrations, and free vegetarian food and tea from the langar and tea tents.

### The Ideal Home Show — 2 to 11 April

**The [Ideal Home Show](https://www.idealhomeshow.co.uk/) is at Olympia from Friday 2 to Sunday 11 April**, with its Eat & Drink Festival inside. Tickets are sold through See Tickets, linked from the show's site. **Grand Designs Live** follows at ExCeL from 30 April to 3 May ([Grand Designs Live](https://www.granddesignslive.com/)); the rest of the year's shows are in our [exhibitions and conventions guide](/articles/exhibitions-conventions-london/).

### Gigs

**Olivia Rodrigo plays nine April nights at The O2, from 5 to 20 April**, and Teddy Swims has four from 21 April. Chase & Status, James Blunt, Thomas Rhett and Thirty Seconds to Mars are there too. The full list, with how each venue sells, is in [London concerts 2027](/articles/london-concerts-2027/).

## Exhibitions

### Opening in April

- **Rembrandt**, Royal Academy, Sackler Wing, **9 April to 19 September**. Rembrandt the printmaker: copper plates, acid and etching needles, and the risks he took with them. £17, or £19.50 with a donation. [Royal Academy](https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/exhibition/rembrandt).
- **Characters of Fashion**, Barbican Art Gallery, **15 April to 29 August**. How writers from Jane Austen and Virginia Woolf to James Baldwin and Zadie Smith used clothes, for themselves and their characters, with garments from McQueen, Prada and Simone Rocha. Tickets go on sale in spring 2027 on the [Barbican's page](https://www.barbican.org.uk/whats-on/2027/event/characters-of-fashion).
- **Ink**, Tate Modern, **22 April to 30 August**. Tate's first exhibition of ink painting. Price to be confirmed on [Tate's page](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/ink).
- **South Asia Now: Fashion. Art. Design.**, V&A East Museum, **from 24 April**. Over 200 works of art, fashion, architecture and design from contemporary South Asia. Tickets are listed as available soon on the [V&A's page](https://www.vam.ac.uk/exhibitions/south-asia-now-fashion-art-design).

### Closing in April

- **Es Devlin: Other Worlds**, Design Museum, to **4 April**: the artist and designer's first retrospective in Europe, covering 30 years of sculpture, stage design and light; adults from £17.09.
- **Van Eyck: The Portraits**, National Gallery, to **11 April**: all of Jan van Eyck's portraits together for the first time, £18, under-18s free. Entry is by timed ticket from the [National Gallery's page](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/van-eyck-the-portraits).
- **Vanessa Bell & Duncan Grant: Inside Bloomsbury**, Tate Britain, to **11 April**, £20.
- **Tarek Atoui: Hold**, the Hyundai Commission in Tate Modern's Turbine Hall, free, to **11 April**: a sound installation of bamboo pipes blown by wind, Korean drums struck by mechanised mallets and textiles up to 30 metres long moving in the hall's air currents.
- **Pierre Alechinsky: In Paperland**, Serpentine South, free, to **18 April**.
- **Queen Elizabeth II: Her Life in Style**, the King's Gallery, Buckingham Palace, to **18 April**: the largest exhibition of the late Queen's clothes yet mounted. Adults £24 in 2027. [Royal Collection Trust](https://www.rct.uk/event/queen-elizabeth-ii-her-life-in-style).
- **Becoming America: How Science Shaped a Nation**, Science Museum, free with a booked ticket, to **25 April**.
- **Metamorphoses: Porcelain Sculpture by Rachel Kneebone**, a free display at the Wallace Collection, to **25 April**.

### Still running

**Monet: Painting Time** at Tate Modern runs to 27 June at £26, **German Expressionism** at the National Gallery to 1 August, **Mary Cassatt** at the National Portrait Gallery to 13 June and **Punk to Pop** at the V&A from 13 March. The **Bayeux Tapestry** is at the British Museum until 11 July; adult tickets are £33, and the April to July slots are due for release in early 2027 on the [British Museum's page](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry). Our [Bayeux Tapestry guide](/articles/bayeux-tapestry-british-museum/) has the prices, member rules and what is on the cloth. ## Practical: April in London

**Weather.** The Met Office's Heathrow average for April (1991 to 2020) is a daily high of **15.0C** and a low of **6.0C**, with **42.3mm of rain** and **169 hours of sunshine**, against 120 in March. Air frost is down to under one day in the month. Our [London in the rain guide](/articles/london-in-the-rain/) has the indoor fallbacks for showers.

**Daylight.** British Summer Time all month: sunset is **7.32pm on 1 April**, **7.56pm on the 15th** and **8.21pm on the 30th**, with sunrise moving from 6.35am to 5.34am. The day grows from 12 hours 57 minutes to 14 hours 47 minutes.

**Closed.** The **Royal Observatory** in Greenwich is shut for refurbishment until the [Meridian Courtyard reopens in summer 2027](https://www.rmg.co.uk/royal-observatory/transformation).

**Getting around.** Our [getting around London guide](/articles/getting-around-london-transport-guide/) covers the Tube and the alternatives, and [London transport costs](/articles/london-public-transport-costs-and-fares/) has the fares and caps.

**Choosing your month?** [Best time to visit London](/articles/best-time-to-visit-london/) compares April with the rest of the year, and [March's guide](/articles/things-to-do-in-london-in-march/) covers the month before, with the Easter weekend and the clocks going forward.
