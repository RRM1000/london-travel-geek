## New Year's Day

### The London New Year's Day Parade — Friday 1 January

**The Parade runs 1pm to 4.30pm along Piccadilly, Regent Street, St James's, Pall Mall, Trafalgar Square and Whitehall, and the general public watches free-standing.** The organiser expects more than 8,500 performers, from marching bands and cheerleaders to stunt riders and samba dancers, and 600,000 spectators along a 2.2-mile route. Ticket holders arrive from noon. Covered grandstand seats and VIP seats in the finale arena are sold through [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.co.uk/london-parade-tickets/artist/2287579), and a new Fun Zone at Waterloo Place has first-come fold-out chairs, space for your own blanket and toilets. Route and timing are on the [London Parade site](https://londonparade.com/parade-location-timing/); the fireworks the night before are in our [New Year's Eve guide](/articles/new-years-eve-london/).

> ⚠️ **The National Gallery is closed on 1 January**, and the King's Cross Winter Market is shut that day.

Check your train before you travel: the Paddington, Liverpool Street and Charing Cross works below run through the first weekend.

## The Bayeux Tapestry: sold out to March 2027

*British Museum, Room 30 · to 11 July 2027*

**You cannot buy a ticket for a January visit.** Every ticket from 10 September 2026 to 31 March 2027 has sold out, and tickets for April to July 2027 are due for release in early 2027. Tickets are on the [British Museum's Bayeux Tapestry page](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry). The room is open Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 6pm and Thursday to Saturday 10am to 9pm. The rest of the museum is in our [British Museum guide](/articles/british-museum-guide/).

## The last of Christmas

Most of the festive attractions run to the first weekend, so plan them for 1 to 3 January.

### Winter Wonderland — to Sunday 3 January

**Hyde Park Winter Wonderland closes on Sunday 3 January with a Family Fun Day.** It is open every day to then, with a timed ticket, and it closes at 10pm. On 28 December to 1 January the gates open at 11am; on the 2nd and 3rd they open at 10am. The [Winter Wonderland guide](/articles/hyde-park-winter-wonderland/) has the gates and prices.

## Festivals and fairs

### London Art Fair — 19 to 24 January

**London Art Fair is on at the Business Design Centre in Islington from Tuesday 19 to Sunday 24 January, in its 39th edition.** Galleries from around the world sell modern and contemporary work straight from their stands, with live performances, installations and talks. **Tuesday 19th is the preview evening, 5pm to 9pm, with complimentary drinks** (£42.75 in advance, £47 on the door). Public days are Wednesday 20th 11am to 9pm, Thursday to Saturday 11am to 7pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm. A day ticket is **£27.75 in advance including the £4.25 booking fee**, or £28 on the door; the Wednesday Late session, 5pm to 9pm, is £21.25 in advance. Large bags and rucksacks are not allowed, and there is a cloakroom. [Tickets are on the fair's site](https://www.londonartfair.co.uk/tickets/). Our [Islington guide](/articles/islington-area-guide/) covers the area.

### MimeLondon — 12 January onwards

**MimeLondon is what became of the London International Mime Festival**, which ended with its 47th edition in 2023. Its two directors now run a season of wordless, visual theatre with the Barbican, Sadler's Wells, Southbank Centre and The Place, from 9 January to 6 February. The January dates are:

- **Modo Grosso, TOUT/RIEN**, Barbican Pit, 12 to 16 January. A London premiere in which Loïc Faure juggles chains, needles, wool, volcanic stones and sand to show time passing.
- **Michael Zandl, FOOD**, Southbank Centre's Purcell Room, 13 to 17 January. A circus-theatre feast about consumption; it runs about 55 minutes and the age guidance is 8 and over.
- **Gecko, KIN**, Sadler's Wells East, 20 to 23 January. A story of families fleeing and arriving.
- **Ockham's Razor, COLLABORATOR**, The Place, 19 to 23 January. A trapeze duet by the company's two artistic directors.
- **Andrew Dawson, ABSENCE AND PRESENCE**, Barbican Pit, 19 to 23 January, about loss and memory.
- **Miet Warlop, INHALE DELIRIUM EXHALE**, Southbank Centre's Queen Elizabeth Hall, 23 and 24 January. A UK premiere that mixes dance-theatre, live art and fabric.
- **Buster Keaton's *The General***, Barbican Cinema 1, Sunday 31 January at 3pm, with live music by Haiku Salut.

Each show is booked through its own venue, linked from the [MimeLondon site](https://mimelondon.com/).

## Theatre

### The New Year Sale

**Official London Theatre's New Year Sale launches on 5 January**, with special offers, exclusive ticket prices and dining deals across West End shows. The Society of London Theatre runs it, and the [New Year Sale page](https://officiallondontheatre.com/new-year-sale/) is where the offers appear. Our [London theatre guide](/articles/london-theatre-guide/) covers choosing a show.

### Opening in January

- **English National Ballet's Romeo & Juliet**, London Coliseum, 14 to 24 January. It runs 2 hours 55 minutes with two intervals and is for ages 10 and over; tickets are from £28. [English National Ballet](https://www.ballet.org.uk/production/romeo-juliet/).
- **Three and a Bit**, Almeida Theatre, from Thursday 14 January to 13 March. [Almeida](https://almeida.co.uk/whats-on).
- **The Seagull**, Sam Wanamaker Playhouse, from 15 January to 10 April: Chekhov in the Globe's candlelit indoor theatre. [Shakespeare's Globe](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/whats-on/).
- **Purpose**, Royal Court, 16 January to 27 February: Branden Jacobs-Jenkins's Pulitzer and Tony-winning play, directed by Phylicia Rashad. [Royal Court](https://royalcourttheatre.com/whats-on/).
- **The Guilty**, Harold Pinter Theatre, from 18 January to 3 April, with Russell Tovey, after a sold-out run at the Donmar. From £32.27 on [Official London Theatre](https://officiallondontheatre.com/show/the-guilty-111478069/).
- **Dirty Dancing**, Prince Edward Theatre, 19 January to 24 April. [Delfont Mackintosh](https://www.delfontmackintosh.co.uk/whats-on/dirty-dancing-the-classic-story-on-stage).

### Christmas shows ending

**The Royal Ballet's Nutcracker ends at the Royal Opera House on Wednesday 13 January**, and **Matthew Bourne's Cinderella ends at Sadler's Wells on Sunday 17 January**. **English National Ballet's Nutcracker at the London Coliseum finishes on Sunday 10 January**, and **A Christmas Carol Goes Wrong at Wyndham's Theatre runs to 23 January**. **The London Palladium's Cinderella pantomime runs to 10 January**, and Cirque Alice at the Royal Festival Hall ends on 3 January. Dates, prices and age guidance for all of them are in our [Christmas shows guide](/articles/christmas-shows-london/).

Other productions end in the first fortnight: **Into the Woods** at the Noël Coward Theatre and **Jesus Christ Superstar** at Theatre Royal Drury Lane both close on 9 January, and Beetlejuice at the Prince Edward Theatre closes on 2 January, with Dirty Dancing taking over the theatre.

**The Masters is invitation-only, for the world's top 16 ranked players, in the West Hall**, with doors at 11.30am and a crowd of just over 2,000. It has been at Alexandra Palace since 2012. ## Trains over the New Year

**Network Rail's engineering runs through the first weekend of the year.**

- **London Paddington:** Great Western Railway trains cannot use the station until Monday 4 January. Long-distance services start and finish at Reading, and some divert to Euston. Work at Dolphin Junction near Slough affects Heathrow and Thames Valley trains until Sunday 3 January.
- **Liverpool Street to Shenfield:** no Greater Anglia trains run, and Elizabeth line services are affected, until Sunday 3 January.
- **London Charing Cross:** engineering work affects trains on 1 to 3 January.
- **Redhill:** the railway through the town is closed until the early hours of Monday 4 January.

National Rail's [Christmas and New Year page](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/travel-information/christmas/) lists every route and its [engineering works calendar](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/engineering-works/) shows the rest of the month. Our [London Underground guide](/articles/how-to-use-the-london-underground/) covers the lines.

## Practical: January in London

**Weather.** The Met Office's Heathrow average for the month (1991 to 2020) is a daily high of **8.4C**, the lowest of any month, and a low of **2.7C**, with **58.8mm of rain**, **6.8 air-frost days** and **59 hours of sunshine**. Our [London in the rain guide](/articles/london-in-the-rain/) has the indoor fallbacks.

**Daylight.** Sunset is **4.01pm on 1 January**, **4.20pm on the 15th** and **4.47pm on the 31st**, with sunrise moving from 8.05am to 7.40am. The day lengthens from 7 hours 56 minutes to 9 hours 7 minutes over the month.

**Crowds.** Schools are back by 4 January, and 1 January is the only bank holiday. The attractions that are open are quietest on weekdays after the first weekend.
