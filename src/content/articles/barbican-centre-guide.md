> 💡 **The Short Version:** The Barbican is the City of London's arts centre: home of the **London Symphony Orchestra** and a London base of the **Royal Shakespeare Company**, with a concert hall, two theatres, three cinemas and an art gallery under one roof. Its full programme is below and updates daily. Come to the **Silk Street** entrance, four minutes from **Barbican** station or seven from **Moorgate** on the Elizabeth line. Buy at the desk on **Level G** and you skip the **£4** online booking fee. If you're **16 to 29**, sign up free to Young Barbican first: tickets start at **£6**. Bring one small bag, or leave the rest in the free cloakroom on **Level -1**.

## Tickets: the fee, and how to skip it

Booking online or by phone costs **£4 per transaction**, or **£1.50** for cinema, the Art Gallery and The Pit, a studio theatre. It's charged once per booking however many tickets are in it; a basket with several events pays the highest single fee. In person there's no fee at all: the **Ticket and Information Desk on Level G** is open **10:00 to 19:30 every day** (12:00 to 19:30 on bank holidays).

- **Young Barbican.** Free for anyone aged **16 to 29**, and signing up takes a minute. It gives discounted tickets **from £6** for films, exhibitions, concerts and shows, for you and one friend in the same age range, **20% off at Barbican Kitchen** and 10% off in the shop. [Sign up on the Barbican's site](https://www.barbican.org.uk/join-support/young-barbican).
- **Can't go?** Tickets can be exchanged for Barbican credit, valid for a year, up to **24 hours** before the event, for a **£3 per ticket** admin fee. There are no refunds unless the event is cancelled.
- **Resale.** Tickets bought from resale or auction sites aren't valid for entry. Buy from the Barbican or one of its approved agents.

## Getting there

All the Barbican's recommended routes lead to the main entrance on **Silk Street**. Walk times are the Barbican's own:

| Station | Walk | Lines |
|---|---|---|
| **Barbican** | 4 min | Circle, Hammersmith & City, Metropolitan |
| **Moorgate** | 7 min | Northern, Circle, Hammersmith & City, Metropolitan, Elizabeth line |
| **Farringdon** | 8 min | Elizabeth line, Thameslink |
| **St Paul's** | 11 min | Central |
| **Liverpool Street** | 11 min | Elizabeth line, Central, national rail |

The **153** bus stops on Silk Street itself; the 4 and 56 stop at Barbican station. On the Elizabeth line, use the Moorgate exit of Liverpool Street station.

**Driving.** The Barbican's car parks are open 24 hours. Evenings after 4pm on weekdays (leaving before 6am) and all weekend are a fixed **£13**; weekdays before 4pm are charged by the hour. A limited number of spaces can be booked ahead through SABA, and **Blue Badge holders park free** if they book. Drop-offs for any venue on Level G or above use the Silk Street entrance.

## Finding your way inside

The Centre is spread over many levels, and it helps to know three of them. **Level G** is the ticket desk and Barbican Kitchen. **Level -1** is the Stalls floor, with the main cloakroom. **Level 3** has the Art Gallery and the Conservatory entrance. **Cinemas 2 and 3** are in a separate building across **Beech Street**, where the Urban Baristas café is. The Barbican publishes [venue maps and step-free wayfinding videos](https://www.barbican.org.uk/your-visit/general-info/getting-here) from Farringdon and Moorgate.

## Bags and the cloakroom

Only **one reasonably sized handbag per person** goes into the auditoria, and all bags may be searched. The Barbican suggests nothing larger than **32 x 18 x 22cm**. Anything bigger goes in the **free cloakroom on Level -1**, open from **90 minutes before** a performance until 30 minutes after; a **£2 donation** is asked for but voluntary. In the galleries, small backpacks have to be worn on the front.

## Eating before a show

**In the building:**

- **Barbican Kitchen** (Level G) serves **9am to 8pm every day**: toasted sandwiches and salads by day, hand-stretched pizza and small plates in the evening. Under-10s eat free from the kids' menu with every adult meal over £10.
- **Barbican Bar & Grill** (level 1, run by Searcys) has a pre-theatre menu and a terrace over the lake from April to September. Last orders are **9pm Monday to Saturday and 5pm on Sunday**, so on a Sunday eat early or eat elsewhere. [Its page on the Barbican's site](https://www.barbican.org.uk/your-visit/eat-drink/barbican-bar-grill) has the menus and table booking.
- **The Martini Bar** on the first floor looks over the foyers and the lake, for a drink before curtain up.

**A short walk away**, around Smithfield, St John Street and Old Street:

- **[St John](https://www.stjohnrestaurant.com/)** on St John Street: Fergus Henderson's whole-animal cooking, open since 1994.
- **[Luca](https://luca.restaurant/)**, further up St John Street: Italian cooking through British produce, from the Clove Club team.
- **[Ibai](https://www.ibai.london/)** on Bartholomew Close: Basque cooking over fire. It books up weeks ahead, so reserve when you buy the tickets.
- **[Kennedy's](http://www.kennedyslondon.co.uk/)** on Goswell Road: pies baked on the premises and fish and chips, walk-in.
- **[Monohon Ramen](http://www.monohonramen.com/)** on Old Street: no reservations, so walk in and wait.

## Staying nearby

Shoreditch is about 15 minutes' walk north-east. Four hotels there:

- **[The Z Hotel Shoreditch](hotel:z-hotel-shoreditch)** on City Road: compact rooms from about £110, the nearest of these. The cheapest rooms have no window, so pay up a grade unless you don't mind.
- **[nhow London](hotel:nhow-london)**: from about £150, with a punk-meets-high-tech interior that includes a Big Ben rocket in the lobby.
- **[The Hoxton, Shoreditch](hotel:the-hoxton-shoreditch)** on Great Eastern Street: from about £200, with breakfast included as a bag hung on your door.
- **[Aethos London Shoreditch](hotel:aethos-london-shoreditch)** on Willow Street: from about £250, the former Nobu Hotel, with an underground spa.

For other ways to split a trip between the City and the West End, see [where to stay in Shoreditch](/articles/where-to-stay-shoreditch/).

## Free at the Barbican

- **The Conservatory.** London's second-largest conservatory, a tropical garden inside the Centre, open on listed dates only. Entry is free but ticketed: tickets are released online **a month ahead**, and a few day tickets go on sale at **9.30am** each open day. Large bags, prams and buggies aren't allowed. [See dates and book](https://www.barbican.org.uk/whats-on/2026/event/visit-the-conservatory).
- **Concrete and Clay: Archiving the Barbican**, a free display in the foyers about the building's history, runs until **1 January 2027**.
- **The Barbican Library**, a public lending library inside the Centre.

## Access

The Barbican runs **captioned**, **BSL-interpreted**, **audio-described** and **relaxed** performances; the listings above mark captioned and relaxed ones. [Visual stories](https://www.barbican.org.uk/your-visit/accessibility) are available for the Hall, the Theatre, the cinemas, the Art Gallery and The Curve gallery. For BSL performances, stalls seats near the interpreter are held for BSL users; book early and ask for them.
