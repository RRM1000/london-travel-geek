All-you-can-eat in London is a fixed price for as much food as you can order inside a set time, and the time limit and the table rules matter as much as the price. Each entry below gives the price, the days and the limit as the restaurant lists them, plus the one condition that decides the visit.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Mr Wu** in Soho has the lowest listed price, a Chinese buffet at £9.95 to eat in. **Chutneys** and **Diwana Bhel Poori House** serve vegetarian Indian buffets at £14.95 in Euston. The longest stated limit is two hours, at **FeiEr Cottage** (hot pot or dim sum, £38), **DAIU** and **Jimmy's World Grill**. For sushi, **Sushi Cafe** in Battersea is £25.80 for 90 minutes. For Korean barbecue, **Keonbae** in Royal Wharf is £32 or £45 on Tuesdays. For hot pot, **Da Long Yi** in Fitzrovia is £28.99 with walk-ins welcome. For rodízio, **Preto** has an £18.95 weekday lunch. For pizza, **Pizza Pilgrims** and **PizzaLuxe** run 90-minute sessions. For dim sum, **Ping Pong** is £29.95 on Sundays.

> ⚠️ **Many of these run on one day only.** Keonbae is Tuesday or Wednesday, Big Easy changes its offer each weekday, Las Iguanas and Pick & Cheese are single weekdays and YO! Sushi's Bottomless Belt is Thursday afternoon and evening. Check the day before you travel.

*Prices and times as listed by each restaurant, November 2026.*

## All-you-can-eat sushi

### Sushi Cafe, Battersea

*Battersea · £25.80 Sunday to Thursday, £27.80 Friday and Saturday · 90 minutes · every day*

A [buffet menu of over 100 Japanese dishes](https://www.sushicafe.co.uk/), including teriyaki chicken, tempura, nigiri and rolls, ordered as much as you like. The limit is 1 hour 30 minutes and booking is by phone.

### Highyaki, Chinatown

*Chinatown · £24.99 at lunch, £29.99 at dinner · every day, 12pm to 11pm*

[Japanese barbecue and sushi](https://www.highyaki.co.uk/all-you-can-eat), with over 50 dishes unlimited. Lunch runs until 3.30pm and dinner starts at 5pm. The restaurant asks you to order only what you can eat.

### Sushi Shimai, Herne Hill

*Herne Hill · £26.80, £13.40 for children under 120cm · evenings, closed Mondays*

[An evening all-you-can-eat sushi and Japanese buffet](https://sushishimai.co.uk/). Lunch is a different deal: 50% off the à la carte menu, not a buffet.

### YO! Sushi, Bottomless Belt

*Participating restaurants · £34.95, £17.50 for children 12 and under · 60 minutes · Thursdays 4pm to 8pm, and daily 7 to 13 December 2026*

The [Bottomless Belt](https://yosushi.com/restaurants/offers/bottomless-belt) is 60 minutes of sushi, rolls, nigiri and desserts off the belt. Dine-in only, and dishes eaten after the 60 minutes are charged. The slot ends 60 minutes before closing, so it stops at 7pm in restaurants that close earlier.

### Shiro, Liverpool Street

*Liverpool Street · from £39 · Saturday and Sunday, all day*

[Weekend brunch with unlimited sushi](https://shirosushi.co.uk/events/shiro-brunch/). A bottomless drinks package is an add-on.

### Akatuki, Covent Garden

*Covent Garden · £48, £68 or £88 · Friday to Sunday, 12pm to 3.30pm*

[All-you-can-eat sushi and a Japanese lunch buffet](https://akatuki.co.uk/japanese-buffet-london), cooked to order and brought to the table: rolls, salmon sashimi, prawn and mussels, ribeye steak, seafood udon, yakisoba and dessert, plus one sashimi platter. The £48 tier includes a glass of Prosecco or the Cocktail of the Day, £68 adds a premium main and £88 adds bottomless Prosecco. Last booking is 3.30pm; online booking covers up to 10 people.

### Nobu Portman Square, Marylebone

*Marylebone · Marble Arch · £90 · 2 hours · Sundays, 12.30pm to 5.30pm*

[Sunday sushi brunch](https://www.nobuhotels.com/london-portman/offers/sunday-brunch/): an unlimited sushi buffet of nigiri, crispy rice, salmon tacos and sashimi, one main course from the menu and a dessert bar, with a live DJ. Children aged 6 to 12 are £45 (buffet only £27) and under-5s are free, one child per paying adult. The reservation is two hours and the last is at 3.30pm.

### Nobu Old Park Lane, Mayfair

*Mayfair · Hyde Park Corner · Saturdays*

[Saturday brunch](https://www.noburestaurants.com/london-old-park-lane/home) with an unlimited sushi and sashimi buffet (Yellowtail Jalapeño, Sea Bass Ponzu, nigiri, cut rolls, Lobster Ceviche, Toro and Caviar hand rolls), a main dish such as Black Cod Miso, and a dessert buffet.

## Korean barbecue

*Royal Wharf · Silver £32, Gold £45 · 90 minutes · Tuesdays, 5pm to 10pm*

[Unlimited Korean barbecue and hot dishes](https://www.keonbae.com/buffet), with sushi and dessert, in two tiers. Everyone on a table must buy the same tier. Children up to 14 are half price and under-3s are free. A 12.5% discretionary service charge applies, and food left over costs £10 a head.

### Keonbae, Kidbrooke

*Kidbrooke · £35 · 90 minutes · Wednesdays, 5pm to 10pm*

[Unlimited Korean barbecue](https://www.keonbae.com/buffet), hot food, sushi, a cold bar and dessert. Children up to 14 are £17.50 and under-3s are free. The service charge and the £10 no-waste charge are the same as at Royal Wharf.

### 113 K-BBQ & Karaoke, Euston

*Euston · £38 on weekdays, £40 at weekends · Tuesday to Sunday, barbecue 5pm to 10pm*

A [Korean barbecue buffet sold with karaoke](https://113ktv.co.uk/menu/). Closed Mondays; reserve by email or phone.

### Kalbi, Clerkenwell

*Clerkenwell*

A [Korean barbecue buffet](https://www.kalbi.co.uk/) on Rosebery Avenue.

### Sanshun, Hammersmith

*Hammersmith · every day*

[Hot pot and Korean barbecue](https://sanshun.co.uk/) in one buffet: beef, pork, lamb, seafood and chicken to grill, plus sushi, bibimbap and desserts. Closed on 24 and 25 December.

## Hot pot

### Da Long Yi, Fitzrovia

*Fitzrovia · £28.99 · every day, Sunday to Thursday 12pm to 11pm, Friday and Saturday to 11.30pm*

[All-you-can-eat Sichuan hot pot](https://www.dalongyi.uk/all-you-can-eat-mobile): pick a broth, then unlimited meats, seafood, vegetables and hot pot favourites. Walk-ins are welcome and no booking is needed. Last orders are an hour before closing.

### FeiEr Cottage, Westminster

*Westminster · £38, £20 for children under 9 · 120 minutes · every day*

[Unlimited hot pot](https://www.feier-restaurant.co.uk/unlimited-hot-pot) for two hours: a choice of broths, thin-sliced meats, vegetables, dumplings, fish balls and sauces. Weekdays it runs 11am to 4pm and 5pm to 10pm; weekends 12pm to 10pm. The same price and time limit apply to its [unlimited dim sum](https://www.feier-restaurant.co.uk/unlimited-dimsum), listed under dim sum below.

### DAIU, Wembley

*Wembley · £35, £25 for children · 2 hours · every day*

A [buffet with hot pot and barbecue cooked at the table](https://daiu.co.uk/wembley/). The child price applies to under-13s and under 150cm, measured at reception. Last admittance is 90 minutes before closing, drinks are extra and there is a 5% discretionary service charge. DAIU also has branches in Bexleyheath, Croydon, Ilford and Wimbledon; the price here is from the Wembley page.

## Rodízio and Brazilian grills

Rodízio means waiters circulate with skewers of grilled meat and carve it onto your plate until you signal that you have had enough, with a salad and hot-food bar alongside.

### Preto, Angel

*Angel · £18.95 weekday lunch, £35.95 dinner · Monday to Friday lunch*

A [continuous rodízio](https://www.preto.co.uk/app/uploads/2026/07/Dinner-Menu-Angel.pdf) of beef, pork, chicken and lamb carved at the table, with the salad bar and hot sides. The salad bar and hot sides alone are £27.95 at dinner. Children up to 10 are £9.95, and one child up to 10 eats free with each full-paying adult on weekdays from 4pm and all weekend. A 12.5% discretionary service charge applies. Preto also has branches in Queensway, Victoria and the West End; the prices are from the Angel menu.

### Picanha Steakhouse, Tooting

*Tooting · £29.99 · Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday lunch*

A [Brazilian rodízio](https://www.picanhasteakhouse.co.uk/) with unlimited picanha and spit-roasted beef, chicken and pork carved at the table, plus a salad and sides bar. The £29.99 Premium rodízio deal runs Tuesday to Thursday and at Saturday lunch; outside it the Premium is £31.99. Closed Mondays.

### Rodizio Rico, The O2

*North Greenwich · £48 dinner · every day*

[Grilled meats carved at the table](https://rodiziorico.com/rodizio-rico-london.html) plus a hot dish and salad buffet. Prices are £34 for weekday lunch, £38 for Saturday lunch, £43 on Sunday all day and £48 at dinner. A December price list runs until 26 December 2026, with a one-price dinner of £39.10 (£44.10 with dessert) every day. Children aged 5 to 12 are £20.50 and under-5s are free; a vegetarian rodízio is £34 at dinner.

### Fogo de Chão, Bishopsgate

*Bishopsgate · £54 at lunch, £68 at dinner*

[Brazilian churrasco in the rodízio style](https://fogodechao.uk/location/london-bishopsgate/): continuous tableside service of grilled cuts, priced by session. Happy hour runs 3pm to 7pm daily in Bar Fogo and the restaurant.

### Braza London, Romford

*Romford (Havering)*

[Brazilian charcoal-grilled meats](https://brazalondon.com/) with an unlimited buffet of hot sides and salads, on Arcade Place.

### Hong Kong Buffet, Chinatown

*Chinatown*

A [Chinese buffet at one set price](https://www.hongkongbuffet.org.uk/) on Lisle Street, with a rotating menu of rice and noodles, meat, seafood and vegetable dishes.

### Aroma Buffet, Shepherd's Bush

*Shepherd's Bush · £18 weekday lunch, £28 at dinner and weekends*

An [unlimited pan-Asian buffet](https://www.aromabuffet.co.uk/prices) at West 12 Shopping Centre. Lunch is Monday and Friday only, 12pm to 3pm; dinner runs Monday to Friday 5pm to 10pm, Saturday 12pm to 10.30pm and Sunday 12pm to 10pm. Children are £9.50 under 150cm (£15 at dinner) and £3.50 under 90cm. Last entrance is 2.15pm at lunch and 9.15pm at dinner, and tables are vacated at the close of each session.

### Jimmy's World Grill and Bar, The O2

*North Greenwich · £17 weekday lunch, £23 Monday to Thursday dinner, £25 Friday dinner and weekends · 2 hours*

A [multi-cuisine buffet](https://jimmystheo2.co.uk/prices/) of Chinese, Indian, Mexican and Italian dishes, salads and dessert, with live cooking stations. Children aged 3 and under are free and those 10 and under are half price. The two-hour seating does not apply to arrivals after 2pm at lunch or 8pm at dinner. Weekday lunch tables are vacated by 4.15pm, and half-term and school holidays use evening prices all day.

### JRC Global Buffet, Wembley

*Wembley · £26.99 Monday to Friday evenings, £29.99 Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays · 1 hour 45 minutes*

A [global buffet](https://jrc-globalbuffet.com/restaurants/wembley/) with an evening sitting Monday to Friday and all-day sessions at the weekend. The child price is £14.99 for under-13s and under 150cm, measured at reception. Last admittance is 90 minutes before closing, and there is a 5% discretionary service charge. Croydon and Ilford list the same prices.

### Cosmo, Romford

*Romford (Havering) · £21.99 to £23.99 depending on the day*

A [world buffet with live cooking stations](https://www.cosmo-restaurants.co.uk/restaurants/romford/) at The Brewery. Monday to Thursday dinner is £21.99, Friday £22.99, and Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays £23.99. Children under 155cm are £11.99. A 3% discretionary service charge applies, and last entrance is 60 minutes before closing.

## Pizza

### Ballie Ballerson, Shoreditch

*Shoreditch · from £45 · 120 minutes of pizza · Thursday to Sunday*

The ball-pit bar's bottomless brunch includes 120 minutes of unlimited pizza and 90 minutes of drinks. The price is £45 on Thursday and Sunday, £49 on Friday and £65 on Saturday. It takes a fifty per cent deposit at booking, with the balance a week before.

### Pizza Pilgrims, Liverpool Street

*Liverpool Street · 90 minutes · weekdays from 3pm, all day Saturday*

A [bottomless pizza and drinks package](https://www.pizzapilgrims.co.uk/pizza-party/) at the Liverpool Street pizzeria: 1.5 hours of Neapolitan pizzas (Margherita, Mushroom and Truffle, Double Pepperoni and Spicy Honey, Nduja and Marinara, with halal and vegan options) plus prosecco, beer, soft drinks and cocktails. Tickets are bought in advance.

### PizzaLuxe

*London branches · £39.95 · 90 minutes · every day, 12pm to 7pm*

[Ninety minutes of unlimited Roman-style pizzas](https://pizzaluxe.com/bottomless-brunch/) with dough balls, plus an Italian spritz, draught beer, mocktails or soft drinks. A birthday guest is free when five friends celebrate together; ID is required.

### Bocconcino, Soho

*Soho · from £49 · 90 minutes · Sundays*

A [Sunday bottomless brunch](https://bocconcinorestaurant.co.uk/soho/menus/bottomlessbrunchsunday) of unlimited pasta and pizza with a charcuterie board and bottomless bubbles. Truffle pizza and truffle tagliatelle carry supplements, and the Aperol or Limoncello spritz version costs more.

## Tapas, tacos and cheese

### Las Iguanas, Tapas Tuesdays

*Most branches · from £17 (£20 at Royal Festival Hall) · 90 minutes · Tuesdays*

[Ninety minutes of unlimited tapas](https://www.iguanas.co.uk/bottomless-tapas) from the Bottomless Tapas menu. Pre-booking is essential, it is dine-in only and the whole table must take part. Dishes arrive at most three per two people at a time, and sides and desserts are extra. It may not run on some days at event locations (The O2, Wembley, Royal Festival Hall).

### Pick & Cheese, Covent Garden

*Covent Garden · Covent Garden · £29.50 · 75 minutes · Wednesdays*

[Bottomless Plates](https://www.pickandcheese.com/news/bottomless-plates): unlimited plates of cheese, charcuterie and pickles from the conveyor belt, all day. Yellow dessert plates and off-belt dishes are not included, and booking is recommended.

### Inamo, Soho and Covent Garden

*Soho and Covent Garden · 90 minutes · daily*

[Unlimited Asian tapas and sushi](https://www.inamo-restaurant.com/in-house-vouchers/unlimited-tapas/) from a long menu, ordered from the interactive table. It must be pre-booked and pre-paid with a voucher bought at least four hours ahead, for up to 25 guests (20 at peak times). Free-flowing wine or Asahi beer can be added, and some dishes carry a supplement.

### Market Place Food Hall, Vauxhall

*Vauxhall · Vauxhall · £50 for two · 60 minutes · Monday to Saturday, 11am to 8pm*

[Bottomless Tacos at Streat Latin](https://www.marketplacefoodhall.com/events/streat-latin-experience-bottomless-tacos): 60 minutes of unlimited chicken, beef, chorizo or jackfruit tacos for two people, each with a welcome Jarritos drink and nachos to share. Tacos arrive three per person at a time, it is dine-in only and late arrivals do not extend the slot. Cancellations are refunded with three or more days' notice. A second site is at St Paul's.

### Chuku's, Tottenham

*Tottenham · Seven Sisters · £30 · Sunday 22 and Sunday 29 November 2026, 12pm to 4pm*

[Bottomless sharing plates](https://www.chukuslondon.co.uk/nigerian-tapas-restaurant-chukus-10th-birthday): unlimited reorders of selected Nigerian tapas plates, as part of the restaurant's 10th birthday. Advance booking is required, the whole table must take part, tables are limited and the Lamb Ayamase is excluded.
