Allianz Stadium holds **82,000**, and most of that crowd arrives through one suburban station in Zone 5, into a mile-wide parking zone, down roads that close 90 minutes before kick-off. None of it is hard. It just takes longer than the stadium's own page suggests.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** Come by train. **Twickenham station** is **four trains an hour from Waterloo, 22 to 23 minutes**, then a **1.3km walk**: the stadium says 10 minutes, TfL 17, and a full crowd makes it more. **Whitton is 20 minutes' walk** and has the shorter queue home. Don't park on the street: an event-day **controlled parking zone** covers about **a mile** around the ground from **11am to 11pm**, visitors **can't buy a permit**, and the penalty is **£140 to £160**. Stadium car parks must be **pre-booked** and **close five hours after kick-off**. Going home takes **two to three hours** to clear, and the **last train to Waterloo is 00:11**. The only all-night service is the **Piccadilly line from Hounslow East** on Friday and Saturday nights, a 30 to 35 minute walk away.

| Station | Zone | Walk | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Twickenham** | 5 | **10 min** (1.3km) | Everyone: Waterloo, Reading and Windsor trains, and the match-day queuing system |
| **Whitton** | 5 | **20 min** | The shorter queue home. No ticket gates, step-free |
| **St Margarets** | 4 | **20 min** | A Zone 4 fare. Only partly step-free |
| **Strawberry Hill** | 5 | **32 min** | Teddington, Hampton and Kingston |
| **Hounslow** | 5 | **30 min** | The other end of the Hounslow loop |
| **Hounslow East** | 4 | **30–35 min** | The Piccadilly line, and the only train that runs all night |
| **Richmond** | 4 | **45 min** | District line, Overground and trains, and the free shuttle after the match |

*Walk times are the stadium's own, except Strawberry Hill (TfL: 32 minutes, 2.4km).*

### Twickenham: the default, and the pinch point

From **Waterloo** there are **four trains an hour** (about :03, :18, :33 and :48), taking **22 to 23 minutes** via Clapham Junction and Richmond. **Six an hour** go back, taking **24 to 33 minutes** depending on which way round the loop your train runs. The station is step-free, with taxi ranks at both entrances.

> ⚠️ **The 10-minute walk is not 10 minutes.** [The stadium's travel page](https://allianzstadiumtwickenham.com/getting-here) says 10; TfL's journey planner makes it **17 minutes over 1.3km**, and the stadium's other walk times match TfL exactly. Allow 20 minutes each way, more once the pavements fill.

### The other stations

- **Whitton**, 20 minutes west of the ground, is on the **Hounslow loop**, so trains reach Waterloo either way round: **29 minutes** direct, twice an hour. **No ticket gates**, step-free. Worth the walk against a two-hour queue.
- **St Margarets** is **Zone 4** and 20 minutes' walk. Step-free only for trains towards Twickenham, and not at all when unstaffed.
- **Strawberry Hill** is 32 minutes' walk, on the Shepperton branch and Kingston loop; 38 minutes to Waterloo. Ramps to both platforms.
- **Richmond** (45 minutes) and **Hounslow East** (30 to 35) are too far to walk comfortably; they're the shuttle-bus stations. Both are **Zone 4**; Richmond is step-free throughout.

## The RFU shuttle bus

On **any event day with a crowd over 35,000** (every international, the Nations Championship finals and Harlequins' Big Game), the RFU runs [electric shuttle buses](https://help.englandrugby.com/support/solutions/articles/103000350104-event-day-shuttle-buses) on two routes.

| Route | Fare | Coming in from | Going home from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Richmond** | **£5** before, **free** after | The **A316 by Pools in the Park**, 5–10 minutes from Richmond station | **Rugby Road**, beside the stadium |
| **Hounslow East** | **£5** before, **free** after | **Hounslow East station, Kingsley Road** | **Whitton Dene**, north of the stadium |

They run from **three hours before kick-off** until **three hours after the final whistle**.

## Buses

The **281**, **481**, **681** and **110** stop within a minute of the ground; the **H20** is five minutes away and the **267** eight. The **110 and 267 both run to Hammersmith** (Piccadilly and District lines), a slow way back that avoids the station queue.

> ⚠️ **The 281 and 481 get diverted** once the road closures go in, about 90 minutes before kick-off.

## Driving and the parking zone

The stadium is off the **Whitton Road roundabout** on the **A316**. Sat-nav **TW2 7BA**. It's inside the **Ultra Low Emission Zone**: [£12.50 a day](https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/driving/ultra-low-emission-zone) for a non-compliant vehicle.

On event days a one-day controlled parking zone, the **Twickenham Event (R) Zone**, covers about **a mile** around the ground. Richmond and Hounslow councils run it together.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | **11am to 11pm** on event days |
| **Trigger** | Crowds of **25,000 or more**. Over 30,000 gets the [full zone](https://www.richmond.gov.uk/services/parking/event_day_parking); around 30,000 gets a smaller sub-zone opposite the ground |
| **Visitor permits** | **None.** Only residents and businesses inside the zone get them |
| **Penalty** | **£160** in Hounslow; **£140 or £160** in Richmond. Halved if paid within **14 days** |
| **Removal** | Possible, in some cases |

Every England international this autumn is an 82,000 crowd, so the full zone applies. Blue Badge holders can park in the zone as usual.

**The roads close too.** Whitton Road, Rugby Road and London Road (between King Street and Whitton Road) close about **90 minutes before kick-off** and for up to **two to three hours** afterwards. The car parks stay reachable, but the stadium asks you to be parked **90 minutes before kick-off**.

## Where to park

Every RFU car park **must be booked in advance**, through the parking tab on the fixture's page at englandrugby.com. Prices vary by event. They **open five hours before kick-off and close five hours after it** (09:10 to 19:10 for the 14:10 kick-off on 21 November). No camper vans or overnight stays.

| Car park | Sat-nav | Surface | Walk | Takes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **North** | **TW1 1DZ** | Gravel, uneven | On site, via Whitton Dene | Cars; free **motorbike** area. Not for impaired mobility |
| **Cardinal Vaughan** | **TW7 7LT** | Grass | On site, via Whitton Dene | **Cars only**, height limit **6ft 1in**. Security checks on the footbridge in, so allow extra time |
| **Rosebine Avenue** | **TW2 7PS** | Grass | **10–15 min**, off the A316 | Overflow. Not for impaired mobility |
| **Tesco, Mogden Lane** | **TW7 7JY** | **Hard standing** | **300m** from Gate D | **Blue Badge and Access Card holders only** |

**The fallback is Arragon Road multi-storey** in Twickenham town centre (**TW1 3NG**, 437 spaces, 1.9m height limit), a 22-minute walk, at a **special events rate of £26.45**. Open 7am to midnight Monday to Saturday, 9.30am to 6pm Sunday.

> ⚠️ **Don't drive to a station either.** Twickenham's car park has **21 spaces**, Richmond's 55 (£15.40 on a Saturday). Whitton, St Margarets and Strawberry Hill have none.

Bike racks are in the **south-east corner by the ticket office**; bring a lock.

## Getting home

**The trains don't change for the match**: **six an hour to Waterloo** on a match Saturday, the same as any other, for a crowd of 82,000. Expect the station queue and the road closures to take **two to three hours** to clear.

- **Walk to Whitton** (20 minutes): two trains an hour, 29 minutes to Waterloo, no ticket gates. The north side of the ground empties faster.
- **Take the free shuttle**, from **Rugby Road** to Richmond or **Whitton Dene** to Hounslow East, for three hours after the whistle.
- **Taxis can't reach the ground.** Private hire pickups are at the **Twickenham Stoop** across the A316; there's also a rank at Twickenham station.

### The last trains, and the one that runs all night

| From | Last departure | Arrives | After that |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Twickenham** → Waterloo | **00:11** | 00:35 | Nothing until **06:51** |
| **Whitton** → Waterloo | 23:50 | 00:19 | — |
| **Richmond** → Waterloo | 00:15 | 00:35 | — |
| **Hounslow East** → Green Park | **All night Friday and Saturday** | 37 minutes | Night Tube, about every **10 minutes** |

*Times for Saturday 21 November 2026, England v New Zealand.*

> ⚠️ **There is no night train from Twickenham.** After 00:11 nothing runs until 06:51. The only all-night option is the **Piccadilly line from Hounslow East**, Friday and Saturday only, a 30 to 35 minute walk after the shuttle has stopped. It matters most on **Friday 27 November 2026**, when the second Nations Championship final kicks off at **20:10**.

**Check Sunday trains.** Engineering work hits this line on some Sundays: on 18 October 2026, Twickenham to Waterloo is an hour with a change instead of 25 minutes. See [South Western Railway's engineering calendar](https://www.southwesternrailway.com/plan-my-journey/planned-improvements/planned-engineering-works).

## Bags, entry and the rules that catch people out

| | Allianz Stadium |
| --- | --- |
| **Bag limit** | **A4 or smaller.** Every bag is searched |
| **Bigger than A4** | Refused entry without refund, unless needed for accessibility or small children |
| **Bag drop** | **Webb Ellis House, Rugby Road**: **£10** for the September 2026 international |
| **Not allowed in** | Alcohol, glass, cans, knives, air horns, professional cameras, **pushchairs and buggies**, flagpoles of 1.5m or more, large amounts of food |
| **Re-entry** | **None** |

> ⚠️ **Arrive at least an hour before kick-off.** You can only enter through the gate printed on your ticket, and entry **isn't guaranteed less than 60 minutes before kick-off**. Gates open **three and a half hours** before an England men's international (10:40 for the 14:10 kick-off against New Zealand).

- **Cashless throughout.** One cash machine, to the right of the England Rugby Store.
- **Free tap water** at any bar, and water stations in the north-east and south-east corners.
- **No smoking or vaping** anywhere inside the perimeter fence.
- **Alcohol-free blocks**: L15, L16, U14 and U15; tick the box when you buy. Not in use for the finals weekend.
- **Babies** need a front carrier (no prams or carry seats); under-2s need no ticket. The RFU advises against the steep upper tier for children under 8.
- **Drinks can't be taken out** of the ground.
- **On the day**: text **HELP to 60886** to report anti-social behaviour. Lost property: **020 8831 6527**.

## Step-free access

**Stations:** Twickenham, Whitton and Richmond are step-free to every platform. Twickenham has staff on hand to help (06:15 to 22:45 Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 20:00 Sunday) and a wheelchair to borrow. **St Margarets** is only partly step-free. Lifts can fail, so check the station page on the day.

**The shuttle buses** are the accessible route from Richmond; they leave from **Rugby Road** going home.

**Parking** is the Tesco car park on **Mogden Lane, TW7 7JY**, the only hard standing, 300m from Gate D. It's limited, must be booked, and is for Access Card holders or people who've sent in both sides of a Blue Badge (which also gets **50% off** parking at future events). **Give your registration to the Horizon Parking steward** on arrival, or you may get a penalty notice.

**Inside the ground:**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Wheelchair bays** | **62**, on three raised covered terraces reached by lift |
| **Accessible seats and bays** | **214** in uncovered pitch-side enclosures |
| **Accessible toilets** | **70**, RADAR key (ask any steward) |
| **Changing Places** | South-west corner by the Rugby Store, electric hoist and changing table. **Bring your own sling** |
| **Audio description** | Free receivers from the Ticket Office (south-east) or the Accessible Support Booth (north-east, by Gate D). First come, first served |
| **Chaperone service** | Stewards will take you from the turnstile to your seat. Arrange in advance, or text HELP to 60886 on the day |
| **Wheelchair hire** | **None** |
| **Assistance dogs** | Welcome, with a free ticket: tell the Ticket Office when you apply |

Accessible seating is sold through a **[Nimbus Disability Access Card](https://help.englandrugby.com/support/solutions/articles/103000348442-how-do-i-apply-for-an-access-card-for-allianz-stadium-)**: free if valid only here, paid if you want it at other venues too. If you can't manage stairs but aren't registered, buy **lower tier, rows 4 to 15**. Ramped "drums" in the south-east and south-west corners reach the middle and upper tiers without a lift, but they're slow.

## Where to stay

**[Radisson RED London Twickenham](https://www.radissonhotels.com/en-us/hotels/london-twickenham-stadium)** is the one hotel at the stadium, built into the **South Stand**, with suites overlooking the pitch. Guest parking is free on **non-event days**; on an event day, ask the hotel (**020 8891 8200**).

Otherwise **Richmond** has the nearest choice of rooms, one free shuttle ride away. **[Bingham Riverhouse](hotel:bingham-riverhouse)** is a Georgian townhouse on the Thames at 61–63 Petersham Road, with a well-regarded restaurant; lift access is limited. See our [Richmond area guide](/articles/richmond-area-guide/), or [where to stay in London](/articles/best-areas-to-stay-in-london/) if you'd rather stay central and travel out on the day.

## The museum and stadium tour

The **[World Rugby Museum](https://worldrugbymuseum.com/plan-your-visit)** is in the South Stand, with a scrum machine and a kicking machine. On match days it opens 09:30 to 14:00 for a 15:00 kick-off; otherwise Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 17:00, Sunday 11:00 to 17:00, closed Mondays outside Richmond school holidays.

| | Museum only | With a stadium tour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult (16–59) | **£12.50** | **£32.00** |
| Concession (60+, adults with a disability) | £10.00 | £27.00 |
| Student (16+, full-time) | £10.00 | £27.00 |
| Child (5–15) | £7.50 | £21.50 |
| Infant | Free | Free |

The **tour** covers the England changing room, the tunnel, the Royal Box and pitchside. A **free carer ticket** can be added when booking, and the route is lift-served. **Legends Tours** with a former England player (Jason Leonard on 27 February 2027) cost **£149.50**.

On **non-match days**, parking in the West or North car park is free if you register your number plate at the England Rugby Store.
