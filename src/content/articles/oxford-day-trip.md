**An Off-Peak return from London to Oxford is £37.70, and the coach is £28 and never stops running.** Oxford's main museums are free; its best-known college charges £22.95.

Oxford is 55 minutes from Paddington and small enough to cross on foot in twenty minutes.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Train from Paddington, 55 minutes**, Off-Peak return **£37.70**, Advance singles from **£18**. **Oxford Tube coach from Victoria, £28 return**, every 10 minutes, **24 hours a day**. **Christ Church £22.95** on a term weekday (its Great Hall shuts 12–2pm for student lunch), **New College £12**, **Magdalen £10**, **Trinity £7**, **Merton £5**. **Lincoln, Keble and St John's are free**, all afternoons only. The **Ashmolean, Pitt Rivers and Natural History Museum are free**. **Punting is £25–£35 an hour**, or £50 per half hour with someone else doing the work. Driving means a **£5 congestion charge** — park and ride instead, **£3 for the car and up to five people**. **Or book a guide:** the <a href="https://www.getyourguide.com/activity/-t29999?partner_id=WWP7I0R&amp;cmp=oxford-day-trip" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" data-affiliate="gyg">£27 walking tour</a> includes one college's entry.

> ⚠️ **Great Western trains cannot run to or from Paddington from Sunday 27 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, or on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November, for the Dolphin Junction upgrade.** Diverted Bristol and South Wales trains run to and from Euston and cannot be used at Oxford, Didcot Parkway or Reading; on 28 and 29 November, Chiltern Railways trains run between Marylebone and Oxford. Details on [National Rail](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/dolphinjunction/) and [Network Rail](https://www.networkrailmediacentre.co.uk/news/most-of-britains-railway-open-for-christmas-with-passengers-advised-to-plan-ahead-around-engineering-work).

**Most coach days pair Oxford with places that have no station.** The Cotswolds is the clearest case: there is no railway between the villages and the buses largely stop on Sundays. [The Cotswolds from London](/articles/cotswolds-day-trip/) sets out which villages you can reach yourself, and one starts at this end: the X52 runs from Oxford through Witney to Burford and on to Cheltenham. [Cambridge](/articles/cambridge-day-trip/) needs no coach: an Off-Peak return from King's Cross is £32.40 and the fast train takes 49 minutes, so give it a day of its own.

### Train from Paddington, which is the fast one

**Great Western Railway runs Paddington to Oxford in 55 minutes** direct. Trains with a change at Reading take about the same, 58 minutes.

| Fare | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Off-Peak return**, either London terminus | **£37.70** |
| Off-Peak single from Paddington | £37.60 |
| Off-Peak single from Marylebone | £40.60 |
| **Advance single from Paddington** | **from £18.00** |
| **Advance single from Marylebone** | **from £15.00** |

**Buy the return, not two singles.** An Off-Peak single from Paddington is £37.60, ten pence less than the £37.70 return. The return covers any permitted route, so you can go out from Paddington and come back into Marylebone.

If your times are fixed, Advance singles are cheaper: from £18 from Paddington and £15 from Marylebone. Chiltern advertises Advance fares **from £5.40 one-way**.

### Train from Marylebone, which is the slow one

**Chiltern's direct trains from Marylebone take 1 hour 18 to 1 hour 22.** Oxford Parkway, the station north of the city, is 1 hour 11 minutes.

Marylebone has cheaper Advance fares and direct trains where Paddington's stopping services make you change. **Off-peak starts after 09:30 on weekdays.** A **PlusBus** add-on for unlimited Oxford buses is £4.50 adult, £2.50 child.

Oxford station is a **10-minute walk** from the colleges.

### The Oxford Tube, which is cheaper and runs all night

**Stagecoach's Oxford Tube runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, up to every 10 minutes, with no last coach.** There is no peak pricing and no need to pre-book.

| Oxford Tube ticket | Adult | Child | Concession |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Return** | **£28.00** | **£14.00** | £25.00 |
| Single | £18.50 | £9.00 | £16.50 |
| **Family return** | **£70.00** | — | — |

Concession covers students, young persons and seniors. A **Day ticket is £28** for an adult and also covers Stagecoach West local buses.

It picks up at **Victoria, Marble Arch, Notting Hill Gate, Shepherd's Bush, Acton Main Line and Hillingdon**, plus Baker Street on weekday morning express runs, and terminates at **Gloucester Green** in the middle of Oxford — closer to the colleges than the railway station.

> 💡 **Who should take which.** Two adults on fixed times: the train, on Advance singles, for £36 each way or less. A family, or anyone whose day might run long: the coach, because £70 covers the lot and there is no last one to miss. Anyone flying in: Oxford Bus Company's **airline** coach runs between Oxford and the Heathrow and Gatwick terminals, 24 hours a day, and skips London.

### Driving, and why not to

**Oxford's traffic filter trial replaced the £5 congestion charge on 28 September 2026.** Cars without a permit are fined £70 (£35 if paid within 21 days) for driving through one of the six filters; the first offence in the trial's first six months gets a warning notice instead. The filters run 7am to 7pm at St Clement's Street, St Cross, Hythe Bridge Street and Thames Street, and 7 to 9am and 3 to 6pm at Hollow Way and Marston Ferry Road. They apply to cars only, and with Botley Road open every part of the city can be reached without passing one.

**Park and ride costs £3.** A **£3 combined ticket** buys up to 16 hours' parking for one car plus return bus travel for **up to five people of any age**, at Redbridge, Pear Tree, Seacourt, Thornhill and Oxford Parkway. Buy it at the machine or on RingGo, choosing the Parking & Bus option, and show it to the driver both ways. It runs to 31 March 2027 and is not valid on the airline or the Oxford Tube.

Parking alone at Thornhill is free for the first hour and £2.50 up to 16 hours — so the combined ticket costs 50p more than parking and gets five people into town.

### Christ Church, the expensive one

**A multimedia guide is required and included.** The price moves with the date:

| Christ Church, on the day | Weekday | Weekend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adult, Michaelmas term** (4 Oct–12 Dec 2026) | **£22.95** | £24.95 |
| Adult, Christmas vacation (13–31 Dec 2026) | £24.95 | £26.95 |
| Senior or student, term | £20.50 | £22.50 |
| Child 5–17, term | £17.50 | £19.50 |

**Book ahead and take £2 off every ticket.** Under-5s are free but get no guide, and their tickets are collected on the day. Carers are free with proof of entitlement.

**Tickets are released each Friday at about 10am for the following week**. They are non-refundable, but you can reschedule free online up to two hours before if you made an account, or for £2 a ticket if you did not.

> ⚠️ **The Great Hall closes 12:00–14:00 Monday to Friday** and **10:30–14:00 on Saturdays in term**. On Sundays the Hall opens at 14:00. On Saturdays the Cathedral shuts at 16:45 for choir practice. Christ Church publishes a known-closures page and the booking system labels slots where the Hall or Cathedral is shut — which also cost less.

### The Harry Potter bit

**The staircase is real. The Great Hall is not.** Christ Church's notes say the Hall staircase in Bodley Tower, under its 1638 fan vaulting, has been used in *Lewis* and *Harry Potter*. The Hogwarts Great Hall is a set, and you can walk onto it at the [Warner Bros. Studio Tour](/articles/harry-potter-studio-tour/) instead.

The other genuine location is the Bodleian's **Divinity School**, which the library itself describes as the infirmary in the films, and **Duke Humfrey's Library**, which played the Hogwarts library.

### The other paying colleges

**Magdalen** is £10, with deer, a riverside walk and its tower over Magdalen Bridge. A **joint ticket with the Botanic Garden is £17** and stays valid for one entry at each within 12 months. It closes at 18:30 in July, August and September and at dusk otherwise, and the Hall may shut over lunch. It is closed 22–31 December.

**New College** is £12 for the cloisters and medieval hall. **The Hall is closed daily 11:45–14:00.** From 1 November to 8 March the college is closed on Mondays, open 11:00–15:00 Tuesday to Friday and Sunday (last entry 14:30), and 10:00–16:30 on Saturday. It is closed all day on Saturday 12 December and from 23 December to 4 January.

**Trinity** is £7 and splits its day around lunch — 10:00–12:00 then 14:00–17:00 in term, 13:00 in vacation. Its hours move for private events and it asks you to ring the lodge first.

**Merton** is £5 and keeps its Hall, Fellows' Garden and residential areas closed to everyone, alumni included, so you are paying for four quads and the chapel.

**Balliol** is £6, or £3 concession, and free if you are a prospective applicant. In term — weeks 0 to 9 — and through exams and the admissions period, **groups are capped at eight people including the guide**.

### The free ones, and the one that is not

**Lincoln, Keble and St John's let you in for nothing**, all in the afternoon. Lincoln asks you to ring the Porters' Lodge first and takes no large groups; St John's closes at 16:00 in winter, with the chapel shut on Wednesdays.

**Worcester takes no walk-in tourists.** Access is by official tours led by an Oxford Guild of Tour Guides member, or public events. Residents of OX1 and OX2, applicants and alumni still get in free, 12:30–16:00.

**The Ashmolean** is free with no booking, on Beaumont Street. Its major exhibition, *Aphrodite: The Making of a Goddess*, runs from 8 October 2026 to 11 April 2027; it is ticketed and booking is recommended.

**The Pitt Rivers and the Museum of Natural History share a building** on Parks Road: dinosaurs and a dodo downstairs, 500,000 ethnographic objects up the back. The Pitt Rivers opens at noon on Mondays, so a Monday morning is a Natural History morning.

**The Covered Market** is where to eat without booking: over 50 independents.

## Punting

| Punting | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Magdalen Bridge Boathouse**, self-hire | **£35 per hour**, up to 5 people |
| **Magdalen Bridge Boathouse**, chauffeured | **£50 per 30 minutes**, up to 4 |
| **Cherwell Boathouse**, weekdays | **£25 per hour**, £125 a day |
| Cherwell Boathouse, weekends | £27 per hour, £135 a day |

**Magdalen Bridge Boathouse** is under Magdalen tower at the end of the High Street. Open 7 days a week from 1 February to 30 November, 9:30am to 9pm or an hour before sunset. Booking is not required but is advisable on a busy weekend. Return the boat late and you pay another 15 minutes.

**Cherwell Boathouse** is in north Oxford and has about 80 handmade boats; midweek availability is good, weekends less so. Its season is mid-March to mid-October, so it is closed over the winter. Punt upstream for half an hour and there is a pub, the Victoria Arms; downstream runs through the University Parks.

**Chauffeured suits a first go:** £50 buys half an hour with someone else punting, against £35 for an hour of doing it yourself.

## Does 2FOR1 cover anything here?

The scheme covers a few Oxford attractions, but **no college is in it**, and neither is the Bodleian.

- **2FOR1:** Oxford Castle and Prison, The Story Museum, Soldiers of Oxfordshire Museum.
- **15% off:** the four official Oxford tours — University and City, the Ghost Tour, the C.S. Lewis and J.R.R. Tolkien tour and the On Screen tour.
- **Listed but free anyway:** the Ashmolean and the History of Science Museum.

It needs a National Rail ticket and two people. [How National Rail 2FOR1 actually works](/articles/national-rail-2for1-london-attractions/) has the voucher process.

## How long the day takes

![A narrow Oxford lane between a college wall and a stone boundary wall, autumn leaves on the pavement](../../assets/articles/oxford-day-trip/college-lane.jpg)

*Photo: Jess Buckle, Pexels.*

**Seven hours in Oxford fits a normal day.** Take the 09:30-ish train out and something around 18:00 back, and you get a paying college, a free museum, lunch in the market and an hour on the river.

A working shape:

- **10:30** — arrive, walk up to Radcliffe Square, Divinity School for £3
- **11:15** — one paying college. Christ Church if you want the Hall, Magdalen if £10 and a river appeal
- **13:00** — Covered Market
- **14:00** — the free afternoon colleges open; Lincoln, Keble or St John's
- **15:00** — Pitt Rivers and Natural History, both free, both in one building
- **16:30** — punting, or the University Church tower before its 17:30 cut-off
- **18:00** — train

**Last departures back**, on a weekday: Oxford to Paddington at **23:06**, arriving 00:12, with a **00:10** service arriving 01:16. Oxford to Marylebone, last direct at **22:44**, arriving 00:16. **The Oxford Tube has no last coach**, so take it if you want dinner in Oxford.

> ⚠️ **Check the closures before you book a college.** Christ Church, Magdalen, New College and Merton each publish dated closure lists. Full-day closures ahead include Merton on Saturday 7 November, New College on Saturday 12 December and Christ Church on 25 December. Balliol caps groups at eight in term, and New College closes on Mondays from 1 November.
