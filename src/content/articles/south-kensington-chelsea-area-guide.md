South Kensington contains three major museums within a few streets, while Chelsea adds elegant shopping, gardens and restaurants.

> **Best for:** families, museum visits, refined streets and direct Heathrow travel. **Think twice if:** you want late-night music, budget hotels or immediate access to east London.

## Best things to do

- Divide time between the Natural History Museum, Science Museum and V&A.
- Walk through Kensington Gardens and Hyde Park.
- Explore King's Road, Chelsea Physic Garden or Saatchi Gallery.
- Attend a concert at the Royal Albert Hall.

## Hotels

| Tier | Hotel | Why consider it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium | The Cadogan, A Belmond Hotel | Luxury base between Chelsea and Knightsbridge |
| Mid-range | The Ampersand Hotel | Museum-friendly boutique location near South Kensington station |
| Value-focused | The Resident Kensington | Kitchenette-style facilities and calmer residential streets |

Check whether a “Kensington” hotel is actually close to South Kensington; the district name covers a large area.

## Restaurants

| Restaurant | Style | Why go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ognisko | Polish and eastern European | Elegant rooms near the museums |
| Medlar | Modern European | Accomplished neighbourhood restaurant on King's Road |
| Colbert | French café | Lively Sloane Square option throughout the day |

Restaurant Gordon Ramsay and newer high-end openings make Chelsea a destination for special meals, but reserve well ahead.

## Transport and airport fit

South Kensington and Gloucester Road have District, Circle and Piccadilly lines. The Piccadilly line provides a direct inexpensive Heathrow journey. Sloane Square is on the District and Circle lines.

Museum entrances can be farther from the station than the map suggests. Use the pedestrian tunnel when it is open and follow venue-specific accessibility directions.
