Nothing here is ranked: the people who certify these kitchens already publish the full list, for free. **There are four authorities, each publishes only its own licensees, and checking one register gives you a partial answer with no warning that it is partial.** This guide reads the registers together.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** Four certifying bodies, four separate lists. **The London Beth Din (KLBD) has the biggest** — 35 restaurants and takeaways. **KF Kosher licenses a different set**, including Reubens, which is not on the KLBD list at all. Kitchens are **meat or dairy, never both**, so Bagels Bar and Bagels Bar Grill House sit next door to each other on Brent Street. And almost everything **closes for Shabbat**.

## The four authorities, and what each one lists

| Authority | Register | Roughly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KLBD** — Kashrut Division, London Beth Din | [kosher.org.uk](https://kosher.org.uk/kosher-places/restaurants/) | **35** restaurants and takeaways, each with address and category |
| **KF Kosher** — Federation of Synagogues Beth Din | [kfkosher.org](https://kfkosher.org/establishments-catering/) | **25** establishments, a largely different set |
| **SKA** — Sephardi Kashrut Authority | [ska.org.uk](https://ska.org.uk/licensees) | A smaller list, including the JW3 café |
| **Kedassia** — Union of Orthodox Hebrew Congregations | *no online register* | The Stamford Hill authority |

> ⚠️ **Wrong KLBD site.** `klbdkosher.org` is the London Beth Din's **trade** site, which certifies food manufacturers; read alone, it suggests the KLBD licenses no restaurants. The restaurant register is on `kosher.org.uk`.

**Stamford Hill is the gap in every list.** Kedassia, the authority most used there, publishes no register online, so the published registers show only one certified establishment in the neighbourhood, Tasti Pizza on Amhurst Park, though there are plainly more. Ask locally.

## The distinctions that actually decide where you eat

**Meat or dairy, and it applies to the whole kitchen.** A meat kitchen serves no dairy at all and a dairy kitchen no meat, and the register prints which in the venue's own name. On one street in Hendon:

- **Bagels Bar (Dairy)** — 84 Brent Street. Bagels, salads, pizza, waffles, ice cream, milkshakes.
- **Bagels Bar Grill House (Meat)** — 86 Brent Street. Salt beef, pastrami, burgers, grill and smokehouse.

**Novellino** does the same on Golders Green Road: the dairy restaurant at 103, the meat bistro at 111a.

**Certified is not the same as kosher-style.** Kosher-style means food that looks like Jewish cooking without rabbinical supervision, and a lot of London's salt beef is kosher-style. One London list of "Jewish and kosher" restaurants describes **four of its ten entries as not kosher in its own text**. If you keep kosher, check a register, not a listicle. The salt beef beigel sold 24 hours a day on Brick Lane, in our [best sandwiches in London](/articles/best-sandwiches-london/) guide, is on none of the four registers.

**Almost everything closes for Shabbat**, from Friday afternoon to Saturday night, and for festivals. Novellino is open every day apart from the Jewish Sabbath and festivals. Plan a Friday lunch or a Sunday, not a Saturday.

**Two things the registers guarantee that menus do not mention.** All KLBD dairy restaurants use Chalav Yisrael milk and all its meat restaurants are Glatt. The SKA says the same of its licensees and adds that all use Yashan grain. If you need either, the certification settles it; asking does not.

## Where they are

Kosher London sits in the north-west. The KLBD's listings with a London street address:

| Area | Certified establishments |
| --- | --- |
| **Hendon** | 10 |
| **Golders Green** | 8 |
| **Temple Fortune** | 4 |
| **Edgware** | 4 |
| **Hampstead & Belsize Park** | 3 |
| **Marylebone** | 1 |
| **Stamford Hill** | 1 |

**Brent Street in Hendon and Golders Green Road are the two addresses to know**: each has several certified kitchens within a short walk.

**Central London has very little.** **Tony Page** on Paddington Street is the main KLBD-certified restaurant inside the West End. **Reubens**, the salt beef restaurant, is a few streets away and licensed by **KF Kosher**, not the KLBD.

## What is on the KLBD register

Thirty-five establishments. **Balagan** and **Pumpernick's** are in Borehamwood and **Yofi** is in Hove; **Beit Hamadras** is a Deliveroo-only takeaway.

**Meat kitchens (20).** Bagels Bar Grill House, White House Express, Sami's, Hummus Bar and Kaifeng (the Chinese restaurant on Church Road) in Hendon; Hummus Bar, Novellino Bistro and Tuk Tuk in Golders Green; Burger Bar, Habiba's Smokehouse and Shnitzelhaus in Temple Fortune; Sami's and Yakov's Kitchen in Edgware; Kasa Bar & Grill in Hampstead Garden Suburb; Moss & Maple in West Hampstead; Tish in Belsize Park; Tony Page in Marylebone; Balagan, Yofi and Beit Hamadras, as above.

**Dairy kitchens (15).** Bagels Bar, Bread, Mr Baker, Portoberry and White Fish in Hendon; Beit Café, Eli's Pizza, Novellino, Slice and Taboon in Golders Green; Bread in Temple Fortune; Eli's Pizza and Sababa in Edgware; Tasti Pizza on Amhurst Park; Pumpernick's in Borehamwood.

**KF Kosher alone lists** Reubens, Mazal, Soyo, Pita, One Ashbourne, Aviv, Pizaza, The Kanteen, Ranch, Bracha, Logan's and Headroom Cafe among others.

## How to use this

**Choose meat or dairy first**; two places with the same name may be different kitchens. **Check the register of the authority that certifies the place**: one on none of the four is not certified, whatever else it may be. **Do not plan a Saturday**, and in Stamford Hill ask locally.

If certification is not what you need, our [guide to eating in London](/articles/eat-in-london-guide/) arranges everything else by cuisine, dish and area, and the [vegetarian and vegan guide](/articles/best-vegetarian-vegan-restaurants-london/) is the nearest thing to this one in shape.

*KLBD, KF Kosher and SKA registers as listed on 26 October 2026. Certification is added and withdrawn, so check the register itself before relying on it.*
