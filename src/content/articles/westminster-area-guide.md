Westminster and the South Bank contain London's most iconic skyline. Spanning both banks of the River Thames, this historic corridor pairs political and royal monuments like Big Ben, Parliament, and Westminster Abbey with the pedestrianized South Bank arts promenade, London Eye, and Tate Modern.

> 💡 **At a Glance: Westminster & South Bank**  
> - **Vibe:** Grand, historic, cultural, and scenic along the riverfront.  
> - **Walkability:** 5 / 5 (Extremely pedestrian-friendly riverfront walkways).  
> - **Nearest Stations:** Westminster (Jubilee, District, Circle), Waterloo (Bakerloo, Northern, Jubilee, Rail), Embankment.  
> - **Best For:** Bucket-list monument photography, river walks, royal history, and family attractions.

---

## Top 5 Sights & Activities

1. **Big Ben & Houses of Parliament:** Stand on Westminster Bridge for the classic view of the Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben) and the Victorian Gothic Palace of Westminster.
2. **Westminster Abbey:** Tour the thousand-year-old coronation church of British monarchs, burial site of kings, queens, poets, and scientists.
3. **Ride The London Eye:** Take a 30-minute flight on Europe's tallest cantilevered observation wheel for 360-degree views across London.
4. **Walk the South Bank Promenade:** Stroll from the London Eye past street performers, book markets, the Southbank Centre, and Gabriel's Wharf to Tate Modern.
5. **Churchill War Rooms:** Step underground into the secret WWII bunker where Winston Churchill directed British military strategy.

### 1. Parliament Square & St James's Park
Anchored by statues of Winston Churchill and Nelson Mandela, Parliament Square connects Westminster Abbey to **St James's Park**, leading straight up The Mall to **Buckingham Palace**.

### 2. Southbank Centre & Queen's Walk
The pedestrianized **Queen's Walk** along the south bank is lined with cultural institutions, outdoor food markets, pop-up bars, and skate parks.

### 3. Bankside & Tate Modern
Further east along the river walk lies Bankside, home to **Shakespeare's Globe Theatre** and the former power station turned modern art museum, **Tate Modern**.

## Suggested 3-Hour Self-Guided Walking Route

1. **Start:** Exit **Westminster Station (Exit 4)** directly beneath Big Ben.
2. **Westminster Abbey & St James's Park:** Walk around Parliament Square to **Westminster Abbey**, then stroll through **St James's Park** towards Buckingham Palace.
3. **Cross Westminster Bridge:** Double back and walk across **Westminster Bridge** for the iconic photo of Parliament.
4. **South Bank Promenade:** Turn left onto the **Queen's Walk** past the London Eye and Southbank Centre.
5. **Finish at Tate Modern & Millennium Bridge:** Continue past Shakespeare's Globe to **Tate Modern**, then cross the pedestrian **Millennium Bridge** towards St Paul's Cathedral!

## 5 Common Visitor Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Trying to enter Westminster Abbey on Sundays:** The Abbey is an active church and closed to sightseers on Sundays (open for worship only).
2. **Buying walk-up London Eye tickets:** On-the-day tickets are significantly more expensive and have long queues; book time slots online in advance.
3. **Eating right next to Big Ben:** Restaurants immediately adjacent to Westminster Bridge are overpriced tourist traps. Walk 5 minutes to Lower Marsh or Southbank Market.
4. **Not allowing time for security at Churchill War Rooms:** Book timed entry slots online weeks in advance as daily tickets sell out completely.
5. **Ignoring the Uber Boat:** Instead of taking the Underground, catch a **Thames Clippers river boat** from Westminster Pier to Tower Bridge or Greenwich!
