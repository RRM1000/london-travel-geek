**Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat all deliver restaurant food across London, and all three charge a delivery fee, a service fee and a small-order fee on top of the menu price.** The fees depend on your address and basket, so the only way to know which is cheapest is to put the same order in two apps.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Pick the app that has the restaurant you want**, then compare the total on the last screen. **Just Eat** is the one to use as a visitor: you can check out as a guest, with no account, paying by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. **Deliveroo Plus** (£4.99 a month) and **Uber One** (£4.99 a month or £49.99 a year) waive the delivery fee on baskets over £10 and £15, so they pay back at two or three orders a month. **Just Eat+** does the same over £15. **Collecting the order yourself** skips the delivery fee on all three. **Carry photo ID** if there is alcohol in the basket.

> ⚠️ **The delivery fee is only part of the bill.** Add the service fee, the small-order fee (charged when the basket is under the restaurant's minimum) and any tip before you compare. Uber Eats also charges a higher delivery fee in bad weather and at peak times.

## What each app charges

| Fee | Deliveroo | Uber Eats | Just Eat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery fee | Rises with distance | Lower for nearby restaurants | Varies with distance, store, basket and time of day |
| Service fee | Charged, no rate published | Typically 10% of the basket | 99p to £2.99, by basket size |
| Small-order fee | Under the restaurant's minimum | Under a set amount, restaurant sets it | Under the restaurant's minimum |
| Extras | Extended delivery fee for distant restaurants | Higher delivery fee at peak times; Priority fee | Bag fee where bags are supplied |

Sources: [Deliveroo FAQ](https://deliveroo.co.uk/faq), Uber Eats help on [how the delivery charge works](https://help.uber.com/en-GB/ubereats/restaurants/article/how-does-the-delivery-charge-work-on-uber-eats?nodeId=7d8d6312-df92-4b67-a561-effc6b720359) and [higher delivery fees](https://help.uber.com/en-GB/ubereats/restaurants/article/higher-delivery-fee?nodeId=4938ca61-e0d6-493a-9384-eb005c2eb6e5), Just Eat help on [fees](https://www.just-eat.co.uk/help) and the [service charge](https://www.just-eat.co.uk/help).

A few details that change the total:

- **Restaurants that use their own drivers set their own delivery fee** on Deliveroo.
- **Uber Eats lets you choose** Priority (a fee, and your order is dropped first if the courier is carrying several), Standard or No Rush (a shared courier, a discount, a longer wait).
- **Just Eat's service charge on collection starts at 50p.** Collecting saves the delivery fee, not the service charge.
- **A small-order fee comes back only on a full refund** on Just Eat. On all three you avoid it by adding items.

## How a total builds up

Every order is the same sum:

1. The food, at the restaurant's menu price on that app.
2. The delivery fee.
3. The service fee: about £2 on a £20 basket on Uber Eats, between 99p and £2.99 on Just Eat for courier orders.
4. A small-order fee, if the basket is under the minimum.
5. A tip, if you add one.

The menu price in step 1 can differ between apps. Uber Eats says prices and offers on its app may differ from those at the restaurant, and Just Eat runs a [Price Promise](https://www.just-eat.co.uk/help): if a restaurant's own website or app lists a menu item for less, Just Eat pays twice the difference as a voucher. The promise covers menu items only, not fees or special offers, restaurants only, not grocery partners, and the claim has to be made within 14 days.

| Cost | £4.99 a month or £49.90 a year | £4.99 a month or £49.99 a year | Priced in the app |
| Free delivery | Restaurant orders over £10, shop orders over £15 | Eligible restaurants over £15, grocery over £25 | Orders over £15 at participating places |
| Service fee | Up to 60% off at the closest places | Up to 60% off | Not discounted |
| Leaving | Full refund in the first 14 days | Cancel any time, no refund once billed | Full refund in 14 days if no credit used |

Minimums exclude fees and tips. Deliveroo says the price varies by customer: its FAQ also lists £7.99 a month and £79.90 a year. Uber One's free delivery covers standard delivery within at least 3 miles, with extra charges for Priority and long-range delivery. Just Eat+ is paid by online payment only, so a cash order does not qualify. Sources: [Deliveroo Plus](https://deliveroo.co.uk/plus) and [FAQ](https://deliveroo.co.uk/faq), [Uber One](https://www.ubereats.com/gb/uber-one), Just Eat help on [£0 delivery fees](https://www.just-eat.co.uk/help).

**The sum.** A £4.99 membership pays back when the delivery fees you stop paying reach £4.99:

| Your usual delivery fee | Orders a month to break even |
| --- | --- |
| £1.99 | 3 |
| £2.99 | 2 |
| £3.99 | 2 |
| £4.99 | 1 |

Look at the fee in your own app to find your row. Anything you save on service fees is on top. Paying yearly rather than monthly saves £9.98 on Deliveroo (£49.90 against £59.88) and £9.89 on Uber One (£49.99 against £59.88), if you keep it the whole year.

**Who should skip it.** Anyone ordering once or twice a month, or who mostly orders below the minimum, where the free delivery never applies.

## Which app suits what

**Restaurant range.** Each app lists different restaurants, and a restaurant may be on one and not the others, so search for the place before choosing the app. Deliveroo also runs Editions, kitchens built only for delivery that it labels in the app. All three sell groceries as well: Deliveroo delivers from supermarkets and shops, Uber Eats from grocery shops, and Just Eat lists supermarkets and pharmacies.

**Late at night.** Opening hours are set by each restaurant on all three apps, so what is available after midnight is whatever is still open in your postcode. Deliveroo Hop, Deliveroo's own grocery service, is open 8am to midnight where it covers you. For sit-down meals after dark, see [late-night eating in London](/articles/late-night-eating-london/).

**Alcohol and ID.** Deliveroo asks for date of birth when alcohol is added to the basket and its riders may ask for a passport, driving licence or identity card, from every customer or from anyone who looks under 25. If the rider cannot verify your age they may not hand over the order. Just Eat+ credit cannot be spent on alcohol or tobacco.

**Tipping.** Optional on all three, and 100% goes to the rider or courier.

| App | When you can tip |
| --- | --- |
| Deliveroo | When ordering, or after delivery; a tip to the rider is not refunded |
| Uber Eats | When ordering (editable for an hour after), or up to 30 days after |
| Just Eat | At checkout only, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal |

**When an order goes wrong.**

- **Deliveroo:** Orders, pick the order, Order Help. A refund that was voided within eight hours should show on your statement within 72 hours.
- **Uber Eats:** report wrong or missing items within 48 hours, with a photo of any wrong item. Uber Eats does not replace the order but you may get a refund.
- **Just Eat:** choose "I'm unhappy with my order" in the app, with photos. Refunds usually arrive as account credit within a few days, or in 5 to 7 working days on a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal.

**Student and other offers.** Deliveroo for Students gives verified students aged 18 or over Plus at a discount, and Plus Silver comes free for a year through Amazon Prime or Blue Light Card. For eating out rather than in, Deliveroo Reservations gives £10 credit for booking a table; see [off-peak restaurant apps](/articles/off-peak-restaurant-apps-london/).

## For locals

**Collect instead.** Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat all offer collection from many restaurants. You pay no delivery fee, and you pay the menu price the app shows.

**Check the restaurant's own site.** Menu prices on an app can be higher than on the restaurant's own website, which is why Just Eat pays out on its Price Promise. For a place you order from weekly, the saving is worth a minute.

**Invite codes.** Uber Eats gives a promo to you when a friend places a first order with your invite code.

**Pick one app and one membership.** Free delivery works only on the app that holds it. If you order from one app three times a month, a membership pays back; if you order across three apps, it rarely does. For cheaper ways to eat, see [restaurant deals](/articles/restaurant-deals-london/), [restaurant discount cards](/articles/restaurant-discount-cards-london/) and [cheap eats](/articles/cheap-eats-london/).

## For visitors

**Just Eat is the easiest to start with.** Its guest checkout needs an email address, a delivery address and a mobile number, and takes a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on supported devices, or PayPal. A guest order cannot be paid for in cash. Account sign-in codes go to the email address, so use one you can read.

**Uber Eats** starts sign-up with a phone number or an email address, or lets you continue with Google or Apple. It sends a verification code by text or WhatsApp. Where available it can translate a menu into the language set on your phone, through Google Translate.

**Deliveroo** takes credit and debit cards and other payment methods listed in the app, and Klarna is available.

**Hotel or holiday flat.** Put the street address and postcode in the address field, and the hotel name, room number and your phone number in the delivery notes. Plan to meet the courier at the entrance or reception rather than expecting them at your door, and ask the hotel whether it accepts deliveries. Uber Eats offers three drop-off choices: leave at door, meet at door and pick up outside; you can message the courier from the tracking screen. On Just Eat, an order that carries a delivery code is returned if the courier is not given the code, and a refund may not follow. For a flat, add the floor, door number and any entry code. See [aparthotels in London](/articles/aparthotels-london/) if you are choosing between a hotel and a flat.

**If the app refuses your card,** check the card details and the billing address, which Just Eat lists as the usual causes of a failed payment. If it still fails, try another of the three apps, or collect from the restaurant and pay there. A plan to cover [phone data and a SIM](/articles/travel-sim-esim-guide/) matters because you need a connection to order, track and message the courier.

**Carry photo ID** if the basket includes alcohol.

## Other ways to get food delivered

**Deliveroo Hop** delivers groceries in minutes, with no substitutions, from 8am to midnight where it covers your address. **Uber Eats and Just Eat** also list grocery shops, and Just Eat lists pharmacies.
