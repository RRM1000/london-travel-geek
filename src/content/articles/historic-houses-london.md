London's historic interiors range from a royal palace to a chapel that is the last surviving fragment of a demolished hospital. Several of the best are free, and the most extraordinary one is a private house nobody is allowed to change.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Sir John Soane's Museum** is free and the best small house museum in Britain. **Eltham Palace** is an art deco mansion grafted onto a medieval palace. **The Painted Hall** in Greenwich is Britain's Sistine Chapel. **St Dunstan in the East** is a bombed church filled with vines. And **Evensong at St Paul's** is free and better than the paid visit.

## House museums

### Sir John Soane's Museum, Bloomsbury

*Free · Lincoln's Inn Fields*

The architect's own house, **preserved by Act of Parliament exactly as he left it in 1837** — mirrors, top-lit wells, a sarcophagus in the basement and paintings on hinged walls that swing open.

Free, small, and the best thing on this page.

### Charles Dickens Museum, Bloomsbury

*Ticketed · Doughty Street*

The Georgian terrace where Dickens wrote *Oliver Twist* and *Nicholas Nickleby*, laid out as he lived in it.

### Leighton House, Kensington

*Ticketed*

Frederic Leighton's studio-house, built around an **Arab Hall lined with Damascus tiles** and a gold mosaic frieze.

### Kenwood House, Hampstead

*Free*

A Robert Adam interior on the edge of the Heath, with Rembrandt and Vermeer on the walls, and free to enter.

## Palaces

### Hampton Court Palace

*Ticketed*

Henry VIII's kitchens, the Tudor Great Hall, and Wren's later baroque additions — two palaces in one building. **The maze is included.**

### Eltham Palace

*About £19*

A **1930s millionaire couple built an art deco house onto Henry VIII's childhood palace** and kept a pet lemur with its own heated quarters. The strangest combination of buildings in London.

### Kensington Palace

*Ticketed*

Queen Victoria's birthplace and childhood home, with the King's State Apartments.

### Buckingham Palace State Rooms

*About £35 · summer only*

The nineteen State Rooms the King uses for official entertaining, open **only while the court is away** — normally July to September plus limited winter dates. Sells out months ahead.

Standing outside for **Changing the Guard is free** and year-round.

## Greenwich

### The Painted Hall, Old Royal Naval College

*About £19, under-16s free*

**Forty thousand square feet of baroque painting** that took James Thornhill nineteen years, often called Britain's Sistine Chapel. Lie on the floor — there are mirrors and beanbags for exactly that.

### Old Royal Naval College

*Grounds free*

Christopher Wren's riverside campus. **The grounds and chapel are free**; only the Painted Hall is ticketed. It doubles constantly as a film location — *Les Misérables*, *The Crown*, *Thor*.

## Churches

### St Paul's Cathedral, City of London

*About £26 · free at Evensong*

Wren's 1710 dome, the Whispering Gallery, and **528 steps to the Golden Gallery**.

> 💡 **Evensong is free and daily**, and you sit in the quire rather than filing past it. You cannot climb the dome during a service, but it is much the better way to experience the building.

### St Dunstan in the East, City of London

*Free*

A **Wren church gutted in the Blitz and left as a shell**, now filled with climbing plants and trees growing through the windows.

### The Fitzrovia Chapel

*Free*

The **last surviving fragment of the Middlesex Hospital**, demolished around it. A plain brick exterior hides a gold mosaic interior.

### All Saints, Margaret Street, Fitzrovia

*Free*

William Butterfield's 1859 church, where **every surface is patterned tile, marble or paint** — the loudest interior in London.

### St Paul's, the Actors' Church, Covent Garden

*Free*

Inigo Jones's 1633 church, walled with memorials to actors from Ellen Terry to Charlie Chaplin. The portico is where Eliza Doolittle is introduced in *Pygmalion*.

---

## Still in use

### Royal Hospital Chelsea

*Grounds free*

Christopher Wren's 1692 hospital, **still home to the Chelsea Pensioners** in their scarlet coats. Figure Court is open to visitors.

### Westminster Abbey

*Ticketed · free at services*

Every English monarch since 1066 has been crowned here. As with St Paul's, attending a service costs nothing.

---

## What to know

* **Evensong is free** at both St Paul's and Westminster Abbey, daily, and is the cheapest way inside either.
* **Sir John Soane's is very small** and entry is sometimes timed. Go early.
* **Buckingham Palace is summer-only** and sells out months ahead.
* **Historic Royal Palaces membership** covers the Tower, Hampton Court, Kensington Palace and Kew Palace — worth it if you are doing two or more.
* **The Fitzrovia Chapel and Barbican Conservatory** open limited days only. Check before travelling.
