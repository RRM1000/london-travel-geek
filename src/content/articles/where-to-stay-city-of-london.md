The City of London is the Square Mile, from St Paul's in the west to the Tower in the east; Spitalfields is just outside it, east of Liverpool Street. Its hotels are priced for business travellers from Monday to Thursday, so a **Friday or Saturday night is often cheaper than a Tuesday**. The cost is that some of the City shuts with the offices, so a weekend here needs a plan for dinner.

The range runs from a **£60 Z room on Fleet Street** and a hostel in St Paul's old choir school to **The Ned at about £400**. Seven of the eighteen places below are apartments with kitchens.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** On a budget, **[The Z Hotel City](hotelscom:h103089591)** on Fleet Street is £75 for a double with a window. For a family or a week, **[Locke at Broken Wharf](hotel:locke-at-broken-wharf)** has studios with kitchens by the Millennium Bridge at about £190, and **[Native King's Wardrobe](hotel:native-kings-wardrobe)** has apartments of up to three bedrooms by St Paul's. For the Tower, **[citizenM Tower of London](hotel:citizenm-tower-of-london)** is a minute from Tower Hill station with a ninth-floor rooftop bar. For a big night, **[The Ned](hotel:the-ned)** is a 1924 Lutyens bank with ten restaurants. Book Friday and Saturday nights, and eat in Spitalfields, Leadenhall Market or Shoreditch when the office streets close.

## Weekdays, weekends and the price

**Hotel prices follow the offices.** South Place Hotel's own autumn 2026 offer took up to 20% off Friday and Saturday nights and added breakfast. Price a Friday and Saturday against a Tuesday, and against the West End for the same weekend.

**What closes.** The Bank of England Museum opens Monday to Friday only. The Jamaica Wine House, off Cornhill since 1652, lists no Saturday or Sunday hours. St Paul's Cathedral is open for sightseeing Monday to Saturday; on Sundays it is open for services.

**What stays open.** Leadenhall Market says its restaurants, cafés and pubs now open through the weekend, and it runs free pop-up markets from 12 to 5pm, with SoLo Craft Fair on the second Saturday of each month. Old Spitalfields Market trades daily, Tower Hill is busy all weekend, and One New Change by St Paul's is the standby for food. Shoreditch is ten minutes' walk north of Liverpool Street.

**At night it is quiet**, especially around St Paul's and the river. The exceptions are the public rooftop bars on hotels at Tower Hill and Finsbury Square.

## Which part of the City

**St Paul's and the river** is the west: the cathedral, the lanes of Carter Lane behind it, and the Millennium Bridge to Tate Modern. Most of the apartment buildings are here, and Fleet Street runs west to the Royal Courts of Justice.

**Bank, Moorgate and Liverpool Street** is the middle and north: the Royal Exchange, Leadenhall Market and the towers, with the best train connections in the City.

**Tower Hill** is the south-east: the Tower of London, Tower Bridge and the Roman wall. It is the corner that stays busy at weekends.

**Aldgate and Spitalfields** is the east edge, where the City meets Whitechapel and Brick Lane, and where the weekends are livelier.

**Barbican and Smithfield** is the north-west: the Barbican Centre, Smithfield Market and Farringdon station.

## Hotels at Bank, Moorgate and Liverpool Street

### The Ned — the bank with ten restaurants

*About £400 · 250 bedrooms · 27 Poultry, EC2R 8AJ · On the Bank junction · [check prices](hotel:the-ned)*

The Midland Bank's headquarters, built in 1924 to designs by Sir Edwin Lutyens, whose nickname gives the hotel its name, and reopened in 2017 after a four-year restoration. The **3,000-square-metre former banking hall** holds most of its ten restaurants and is open to anyone, hotel guest or not.

## Hotels at the Barbican and Smithfield

### Montcalm Brewery — Whitbread's brewery, by the Barbican

*235 rooms · 52 Chiswell Street, EC1Y 4SA · Barbican Centre a few minutes · [Hotels.com](hotelscom:h4269455)*

Whitbread's eighteenth-century brewery, now a hotel with Georgian sash windows onto Chiswell Street. **The Jugged Hare**, the restored pub in the building, serves a daily changing menu, and the Chiswell Street Dining Rooms serve modern British food and afternoon tea.

### The Fox and Anchor — six rooms above a Smithfield pub

*6 rooms · 115 Charterhouse Street, EC1M 6AA · Farringdon 4 min · Check-out 1pm · [Hotels.com](hotelscom:h21899561)*

A Young's pub on the edge of Smithfield Market with six bedrooms on the first floor, dressed in damask and velvet. Each has an ensuite shower, an honesty bar and a Nespresso machine. Several also have a freestanding roll-top bath, and **the feature room adds a private terrace and a copper roll-top**.

## Getting in and out

**Liverpool Street** has the Elizabeth, Central, Circle, Hammersmith & City and Metropolitan lines, the Overground, and the Stansted Express. Its Elizabeth line platforms connect underground to **Moorgate**, which adds the Northern line.

**Farringdon** has the Elizabeth line and Thameslink, which runs direct to Gatwick and Luton airports and to St Pancras for the Eurostar. **City Thameslink** and **Blackfriars** are on the same line.

**Bank and Monument** are one interchange: Central, Northern, Waterloo & City, District, Circle and the DLR to London City Airport and Canary Wharf. **Tower Hill** is District and Circle, with Tower Gateway on the DLR next door. **St Paul's** is Central; **Barbican** is Circle, Hammersmith & City and Metropolitan, the direct line to King's Cross St Pancras.

**Heathrow:** the Elizabeth line runs direct to Farringdon and Liverpool Street; our [Heathrow guide](/articles/heathrow-airport-to-london/) compares the routes.

**Across the river:** the Millennium Bridge leads to Tate Modern and Bankside, and London Bridge to Borough Market; our [South Bank guide](/articles/where-to-stay-south-bank/) and [Bermondsey guide](/articles/where-to-stay-bermondsey/) cover the hotels on that side. For how the City compares with the rest of London, see [the best areas to stay in London](/articles/best-areas-to-stay-in-london/).
