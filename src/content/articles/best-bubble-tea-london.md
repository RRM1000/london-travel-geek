**London's bubble tea scene changes faster than the lists that cover it.** Four of Time Out's top ten, ranked in March 2026, aren't serving today. The Alley, named by more sources than any shop except Xing Fu Tang, no longer has a UK store. This guide includes only places we've confirmed are open.

This page isn't our opinion. Every shop here is ranked by how many independent lists, blogs and video reviews name it.

> 🧋 **The short version.**
> **[Xing Fu Tang](#xing-fu-tang)** in Soho for brown sugar pearls, cooked in small batches through the day. **[Kissaten](#kissaten)** for honey pearls and Japanese teas. **[T4](#t4)** for a classic milk tea, from four London branches. For choice, go to **Newport Court in Chinatown**, where [Cuppacha](#cuppacha), [Happy Lemon](#happy-lemon), [Min Tea](#min-tea) and [Lucky Tea](#lucky-tea) are a few doors apart and open late.

> 📊 **The evidence behind this guide.**
> Nothing here rests on one visit. This pass reads **15 sources carrying 126 citations** across **36 named venues**: the editorial mastheads (Time Out, The Infatuation, the Evening Standard, The Handbook), independent London blogs (Wrap Your Lips Around This, Squeeze London, London x London, Hues of Delahaye, King's College's Strand Magazine, Secret London) and five London boba reviewers on YouTube. **23 venues are named by two or more independent sources.** Every shop with an entry was checked as trading on 14 September 2026.

## The winners

### Xing Fu Tang

*£ · Soho · 4 min from Tottenham Court Road · Cited by 10 sources · #1 of 10, Time Out · [xingfutang.co.uk](https://xingfutang.co.uk/)*

**29 Frith Street, W1D 5LG.** The Taiwanese chain every list agrees on. Its speciality is **brown sugar pearls**, which Time Out says are made in hourly batches in a huge cauldron. The house drink is fresh milk with brown sugar syrup, topped with sugar blowtorched to a crème brûlée crust. Time Out ranks it first. Wrap Your Lips Around This calls it the writer's overall favourite for its "soft sweetness and toasted toffee flavour."

It's a small white shop for takeaway, and **the queue forms at opening time**. If you want something to photograph, there's a gold-leaf version of the brown sugar milk tea and a mango smoothie topped with a panna cotta rabbit. There's a second branch, **XFT City**, at 122 Middlesex Street near Liverpool Street.

### The Whale Tea

*£ · Bayswater · 2 min from Bayswater · Cited by 2 sources · #1 of 10, The Strand · [thewhaletea.co.uk](https://thewhaletea.co.uk/)*

**18 Queensway, W2 3RX**, and **45 Wardour Street, W1D 6PZ** in Chinatown. This is the King's College student magazine's number one: "the store's brown sugar boba is the best in London." Its signature is the Flaming Whale, classic brown sugar boba under fresh cheese cream. The Infatuation likes the plain milk tea boba, which "tastes a little like cereal milk in the best way possible."

The Queensway shop has a couple of tables and Hyde Park is at the end of the road. Take the cup to the park.

> 💡 **Two lists, two winners.** Time Out puts Xing Fu Tang first and doesn't mention The Whale Tea. The Strand puts The Whale Tea first and Xing Fu Tang seventh. Both are praising brown sugar boba, so the disagreement is really about which shop does it better.

## Also strongly backed

### Kissaten

*£ · Soho · 4 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 8 sources · #3 of 10, Time Out · [kissaten.co.uk](https://kissaten.co.uk/)*

**72 Old Compton Street, W1D 4UN.** A Japanese-style tea house, pricier than most, and Time Out says it's worth paying extra for. It makes its own **honey tapioca pearls**. There are also yoghurt popping pearls, a matcha range and hojicha with salted cheese. The Infatuation orders the Earl Grey milk tea for "a nice floral twist."

You order on a screen. There are gachapon capsule machines to play with while you wait, and **long queues at busy times**. Older guides give 30 Brewer Street, but it's now on Old Compton Street.

### Cuppacha

*£ · Chinatown · 2 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 8 sources · #9 of 10, Time Out · [cuppacha.com](https://cuppacha.com/)*

**23 Newport Court, WC2H 7JS.** The bright pink shop on Newport Court has been making bubble tea for about a decade. There's a **queue most days**. The Infatuation orders the mango fruit tea with popping boba, where "the sweetness isn't overpowering." Time Out picks the butterfly pea ombré drinks, which fade from blue to pink in the cup.

It's a takeaway counter, not somewhere to sit. There's a second branch at 395 Edgware Road in Cricklewood.

### T4

*£ · Paddington · 2 min from Paddington · Cited by 8 sources · [t4.co.uk](https://t4.co.uk/)*

**4B Praed Street, W2 1JX**, plus **137 Strand**, **Westfield Stratford City** and **Westfield London** in Shepherd's Bush. T4 uses whole tea leaves, never tea bags or powder, and sticks to milk teas rather than fruit. The Infatuation says "no matter which branch you're at, you'll get a great bubble tea," and likes the Oreo potted milk tea best. The Strand orders Earl Grey milk tea with French pudding.

This is the pick if you want a reliable milk tea near where you already are, rather than travelling for one.

### Happy Lemon

*£ · Chinatown · 2 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 7 sources · [happy-lemon.co.uk](https://happy-lemon.co.uk/)*

**24A Newport Court, WC2H 7JS.** A global chain that opened its first European shop here, with a bright yellow front. Despite the name, The Infatuation prefers the milk teas, especially the Oreo cookies and cream. The **rock salt cheese** topping is the signature, a salty cream cap that the Standard says "sounds odd" but works.

Open until about 10:30pm. The Standard says it's "usually packed" and worth pushing through the crowd for.

### Yi Fang Fruit Tea

*£ · King's Cross · 8 min from King's Cross St Pancras · Cited by 6 sources*

**150 Gray's Inn Road.** The **fruit tea** specialist. The recipe comes from the founder's grandmother, who made jam from pineapples. The Strand says it "dominates the fruit tea scene," and Wrap Your Lips Around This calls it the best fruity bubble tea in London.

> ⚠️ **The Chinatown shop has closed.** The lists that give an address point to 104 Shaftesbury Avenue, which Google now shows as permanently closed. The Gray's Inn Road branch is still open.

### Woo Tea

*£ · Chinatown · 3 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 5 sources*

**26 Wardour Street, W1D 6QL.** An **oolong** specialist. Wrap Your Lips Around This picks its roasted oolong as the best in London: "nutty with a buttery texture, and an almost smoky tone." Squeeze London suggests the purple potato oolong tea with tapioca. It's a small walk-in counter serving hot and cold drinks, open until about 10:30pm.

### Bubble Magik

*£ · Ealing · 3 min from Ealing Broadway · Cited by 4 sources · [bubblemagik.com](https://bubblemagik.com/)*

**Unit 20, Ealing Broadway Shopping Centre, W5 5JY**, plus a counter on **the fourth floor of Selfridges**. The best-backed shop outside zone 1. The Infatuation describes a "cupboard-sized" counter where drinks are sealed and shaken by machine, and orders the mango iced tea with passion fruit boba. Wrap Your Lips Around This goes back for the original Assam milk tea. The Ealing counter keeps shopping-centre hours and is open until about 7:30pm.

### Mooboo

*£ · Camden · 3 min from Camden Town · Cited by 4 sources · [mooboo.co](https://mooboo.co/)*

**84 Parkway, NW1 7AN** is the central London branch the lists name. Mooboo is Britain's biggest bubble tea chain, with **over 70 UK shops** according to Squeeze London. There's probably one near you. Order the **taro**, which The Handbook describes as "nutty and sweet" and bright purple. The Camden shop is walk-in and small, and it's open until about 8:30pm.

### HEYTEA

*£ · Soho · 3 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 3 sources · #3 of 10, The Strand*

**Unit 2, Wingate House, 93–107 Shaftesbury Avenue, W1D 5DA.** When it opened, this was the Chinese chain's first shop outside Asia, and it drew huge queues. HEYTEA is known for **cheese foam**, a salted cream-cheese layer on cold tea that The Strand compares to cheesecake. The Infatuation's pick is the supreme brown sugar milk tea with a thick cheese foam top, and it notes that queues often form for the coconut mango. Open until about 10pm.

### Min Tea

*£ · Chinatown · 2 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 3 sources · [mintea.co.uk](https://mintea.co.uk/)*

**34 Newport Court, WC2H 7PQ.** The **cheese foam** specialist on Newport Court, with a whole menu of cheesy milk teas. Wrap Your Lips Around This calls it "the cheesiest bubble tea in London." The Infatuation's pick is the cheesy milk tea with Oreo bits. Of the four Newport Court shops here, it stays open latest, until about 10:45pm.

### Lucky Tea

*£ · Chinatown · 2 min from Leicester Square · Cited by 3 sources · #4 of 10, The Strand*

**35 Newport Court, WC2H 7PQ.** Come here for **slushies** rather than milk tea. The Strand's favourite is the grape slushie, made with real grape pieces and topped with cheese milk foam and crystal boba. There's also a passion fruit Yakult and a matcha slushie with red beans. Two London boba reviewers on YouTube include it as well. Walk-in only, open until about 10:30pm.

## What the widely-shared lists get wrong

The lists that rank highest for this search mostly predate recent closures, and bubble tea shops open and close quickly. Checking every name against Google's business listings on 14 September 2026 turned up the following:

| Shop | Recommended by | Status now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Alley** | 10 of 15 sources | Holborn permanently closed. Its UK website says a store is "reopening soon" |
| **Biju Bubble Tea Room** | 8, incl. Time Out #5 | Soho and Camden closed. The only remaining listing, at the Southbank Centre, is temporarily closed |
| **Yunique Tea** | 8 | Little Newport Street temporarily closed |
| **Dragon Cat Café** | 7, incl. Time Out #2 | Hammersmith café and Chinatown wheelcake hut both permanently closed |
| **CoCo Fresh Tea & Juice** | 5 | Dean Street permanently closed, no other London branch found |
| **Quaker Street Coffee & Bubble Tea** | 2, incl. Time Out #8 | Permanently closed |
| **Boba Coma**, Leyton | 2, incl. Time Out #7 | Temporarily closed |

**Time Out's list was updated six months ago, and four of its top ten aren't serving.** That's no knock on Time Out. Shops close faster than any list can keep up. It does show why checking a list against today's listings matters as much as the list itself.

Two chains are open but not at the address most lists give. **Bubbleology** opened London's first bubble tea shop in 2011. Its Soho bar (the one serving boozy bubble teas) and its Bloomsbury shop have both closed, and its remaining London branch is at **Westfield Stratford City**. **Yi Fang** moved from Shaftesbury Avenue to [Gray's Inn Road](#yi-fang-fruit-tea).

## By style

**Brown sugar**: [Xing Fu Tang](#xing-fu-tang) for pearls cooked in small batches, [The Whale Tea](#the-whale-tea) for the Strand's "best in London", [HEYTEA](#heytea) with cheese foam on top.

**Classic milk tea**: [T4](#t4) and [Kissaten](#kissaten) for Earl Grey, [Happy Lemon](#happy-lemon) for Oreo, [Woo Tea](#woo-tea) for roasted oolong.

**Cheese foam**: [Min Tea](#min-tea) for a whole cheesy menu, [HEYTEA](#heytea) for the style's biggest name, [Happy Lemon](#happy-lemon) for rock salt cheese.

## Somewhere to sit

Most of the shops above are takeaway counters. These have seats:

- 🪑 **Pürcha**, 7 Kingsway (Holborn) and 82 Charing Cross Road. Tea brewed to order with purified mineral water, "plenty of comfy spots to chill out," and Taiwanese fried chicken. *Cited by 2 sources · #10 of 10, Time Out · [purcha.co.uk](https://purcha.co.uk/)*
- 🪑 **[The Whale Tea](#the-whale-tea)**, Queensway. A couple of tables, with Hyde Park at the end of the road.
- 🪑 **Cuppo Bubbo**, 167 Herne Hill, SE24. Time Out's number four: a café with more seats than most, arranged around a huge money tree plant. It brews tea to order and makes a rose bubble tea with real petals. *Cited by 1 source · #4 of 10, Time Out · [cuppobubbo.co.uk](https://cuppobubbo.co.uk/)*

## Chains and their branches

Nearly every name here is a chain, and the lists name the brand, not the branch. Here's where to go for each:

- **[Xing Fu Tang](#xing-fu-tang)**: Frith Street is the original. XFT City is at 122 Middlesex Street, near Liverpool Street.
- **[T4](#t4)**: Paddington, Strand, Westfield Stratford City and Westfield London. They're all much the same, so go to whichever is nearest.
- **[The Whale Tea](#the-whale-tea)**: Queensway to sit down, Wardour Street if you're in Chinatown.
- **Gong Cha**: 3 Garrick Street, Covent Garden. The Strand's number two, for milk foam matcha strawberry milk tea. The Goodge Street branch in older lists has closed. *Cited by 2 sources · #2 of 10, The Strand*
- **[Bubble Magik](#bubble-magik)**: Ealing Broadway, or the fourth floor of Selfridges if you're shopping on Oxford Street.
- **[Mooboo](#mooboo)**: Parkway in Camden, and dozens more around the country.

## Before you go

**No bubble tea shop takes bookings.** You walk in, order at the counter or on a screen, and wait. A few things make the visit easier:

- **Newport Court is the easiest place to start.** Four of the shops above are within a few doors of each other, two minutes from Leicester Square, and open until about 10:30pm. If one has a long queue, try the next.
- **Expect queues** at Xing Fu Tang (from opening time), Kissaten and Cuppacha. The Infatuation's advice for Cuppacha is to go on a weekday to avoid the weekend queue.
- **Choose your sweetness.** Most shops let you pick the sugar level. If you're new to bubble tea, ask for half sugar, because full sugar is very sweet.
- **Cheese foam isn't meant for a straw.** Drink from the rim so you get foam and tea together, then use the straw for the pearls.
