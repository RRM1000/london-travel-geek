Birthday freebies in London come two ways: **a reward in a restaurant's app or members' club, or a free meal or drink for the birthday guest when they book a table with friends.** The app ones need an account; several of the table ones check ID.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** For a free meal, **JRC Global Buffet** in Wembley feeds the birthday guest free with a full-paying adult, booked at least 24 hours ahead. **Las Iguanas** sends a £20 voucher through its app, and **TGI Fridays** a free dessert through Stripes Rewards. For a group, **Opium** in Chinatown gives a £25 bar tab to a table of six or more, and **PizzaLuxe** makes the birthday guest's bottomless free with four friends. **The Dickens Inn** at St Katharine Docks gives members a bottle of prosecco.

> ⚠️ **Several need you to register or book well before the day.** Opium's offer needs its form filled in at least four weeks ahead, and JRC Global Buffet's a booking 24 hours ahead.

*Offers as listed by each restaurant, November 2026.*
