**Pancake Day 2027 is Tuesday 9 February.** London marks it with two races in the City at lunchtime, both free to watch, and with queues at the pancake restaurants that do not take bookings on the day.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Shrove Tuesday is 9 February 2027.** The **Inter-Livery Pancake Race** in **Guildhall Yard** starts at **12pm**, with livery companies racing in fancy dress or full regalia; spectators are welcome and lemons and pancakes are on sale. **Leadenhall Market**'s relay, run by **The Lamb Tavern**, follows at lunchtime under the glass roof. For eating, **My Old Dutch** in Holborn and Chelsea takes **no bookings on Pancake Day**, so go on **Monday 8 February**, when its classic pancakes are **£7.50**. **Where The Pancakes Are** keeps about half its tables for walk-ins; its Borough café closes at 3pm on weekdays.

## The pancake races

### Inter-Livery Pancake Race, Guildhall Yard

*Free · Guildhall Yard, EC2V 5AE · Tuesday 9 February 2027, from 12pm*

**Teams from the City's livery companies race around Guildhall Yard tossing pancakes**, in fancy dress or in full livery regalia. The Worshipful Company of Poulters hosts it; the Gunmakers fire the starting gun, the Clockmakers time the races and the Fruiterers supply the lemons. **Pancakes are sold from stands in the yard** while the racing runs, the winners take home frying pans and a trophy, and the money goes to charity. The race has run every year for more than 15 years. **Races are due to start at 12pm**, and spectators are welcome ([OneCity listing](https://onecitylondon.com/event/inter-livery-pancake-day-race/)).

### Leadenhall Market Pancake Day Race

*Free to watch · Leadenhall Market, EC3 · Shrove Tuesday lunchtime*

**Teams of four in bowler hats run 20-metre relays over the market's Victorian cobbles, flipping as they go.** The Lamb Tavern, the market's pub, organises it. In 2026 it ran on Shrove Tuesday from **11am, with races from 12.30pm**; the winners' name goes on the Golden Frying Pan with a Lamb Tavern hamper and a £50 bar tab, and every runner gets a free pancake. **Entry is free for teams of four, booked in advance** through [The Lamb Tavern](https://www.lambtavernleadenhall.com/), and anyone can enter, not only City workers. Spectators line the walkways under the glass roof ([Leadenhall Market](https://leadenhallmarket.co.uk/pancake-day-race-2026/)).

Both races are in the City. In 2026 the Guildhall racing started at 12pm and the Leadenhall relays at 12.30pm, so the opening heats at Guildhall and the relays at Leadenhall can both be seen.

### Pancake Rave, Ministry of Sound

*18+ · Elephant & Castle · late*

Milkshake, a club night at Ministry of Sound, holds a **Pancake Rave** on Shrove Tuesday with free pancakes. The 2026 edition ran on **Tuesday 17 February from 10pm to 4am**, last entry 1.30am ([Skiddle](https://www.skiddle.com/whats-on/London/Ministry-Of-Sound/Milkshake-Ministry-of-Sound-Pancake-Rave-2026/41949443/)).

## Where to eat pancakes

### My Old Dutch, Holborn and Chelsea

*££ · 131–132 High Holborn and the King's Road · Dutch pancakes*

**Dutch pancakes, sweet and savoury**, plus poffertjes (small puffed Dutch pancakes), waffles and fruit beers. Besides the menu classics, you can build a pancake of your own. **On Pancake Day it takes no bookings at either restaurant, and queues form outside at peak times** ([My Old Dutch](https://www.myolddutch.com/our-story-pancake-day/)). The offers on [its offers page](https://www.myolddutch.com/my-special-offers/), all dine-in only and each mentioned on arrival:

- **Monday Madness, £7.50**: the classic sweet and savoury pancakes every Monday except bank holidays, which puts 8 February 2027, the day before Pancake Day, at £7.50 a pancake. The "You Choose" pancake and monthly specials are excluded.
- **20% off Tuesday to Friday, all day**, with the codeword "Pancake Power".
- **Buy one, get one free**, Tuesday to Friday from 5.30pm, with the codeword "2For1".
- **10% off for students** Tuesday to Friday, with a student card.
- **20% off for NHS staff** with an NHS card, **not valid on Mondays or on Pancake Day**.

### Where The Pancakes Are, Borough and Battersea

*££ · Southwark Bridge Road and Battersea Power Station · half the tables kept for walk-ins*

**Fluffy buttermilk stacks, Dutch babies (an oven-baked pancake that rises at the edges) and savoury plates**, made with organic flour from Shipton Mill. The founder grew up in the Netherlands and spent years in the US. The **Borough** café is in a railway arch at 85a Southwark Bridge Road, on a pedestrianised strip under the tracks between Waterloo and London Bridge, and **closes at 3pm on weekdays**, so a Pancake Day visit there is breakfast or lunch. **Battersea Power Station** stays open to 5pm on weekdays. **Roughly half the tables are kept for walk-ins** at both cafés; bookings are on the [Where The Pancakes Are site](https://www.wherethepancakesare.com/faq/). Its standing offers:

- **Continental breakfast, £15.50**: simple pancakes, a barista coffee or matcha and an orange juice, every day until 11am.
- **Lunch duo, £17.50**: a main and a juice, weekdays 1pm to 3pm.
- **The afternoon fix, £15.50**: any sweet pancake dish or sweet Dutch baby with a coffee, hot chocolate, matcha or Prosecco, Battersea only, weekdays 3pm to 5pm.
- **Children's build-your-own mini stacks** from £6.50 ([offers](https://www.wherethepancakesare.com/events-offers/)).

### CAJP, Victoria, Chiswick and Camden Market

*££ · Japanese soufflé pancakes · book at Victoria or Chiswick*

**Japanese soufflé pancakes**, cooked to order on **gluten-free and dairy-free bases**, alongside Japanese breakfast, brunch and matcha. The cafés are at 324 Vauxhall Bridge Road in Victoria and 195 Chiswick High Road, with a third café at Camden Market's Stables. Victoria and Chiswick take bookings; Chiswick makes vegan pancakes on request ([CAJP](https://cajp.uk/)).

### La Petite Bretagne, Hammersmith

*5–7 Beadon Road, W6 0EA · open every day*

**Breton crêperie**: savoury galettes made with **buckwheat flour**, which makes them crisp and gluten-free, and sweet crêpes after. Order a galette such as the raclette, with raclette cheese and gherkins. It is open **8am to 10pm every day**, kitchen closing at 9.30pm ([La Petite Bretagne](https://www.lapetitebretagne.co.uk/)).

### Senzala, Brixton Village

*41–42 Coldharbour Lane, SW9 8PS · open to 10pm on Tuesdays*

**A crêperie inside the covered Brixton Village market.** On a Tuesday it is open **10am to 10pm**, so it works for a Pancake Day dinner as well as lunch ([Senzala](https://www.senzalacreperie.co.uk/)).

### The Breakfast Club

*Cafés across London*

**American-style pancakes**, stacked with bacon or berries and maple syrup, alongside salted caramel and banoffee versions and a vegan blueberry stack. The chain has cafés in Soho, Spitalfields, London Bridge, Angel and St Pancras among others ([The Breakfast Club](https://www.thebreakfastclubcafes.com/)).
