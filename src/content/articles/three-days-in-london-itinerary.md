This three-day plan covers London's essential contrasts without treating the city like a checklist. Book the Tower of London, Westminster Abbey and any show in advance.

## Day 1: Westminster, royal London and the West End

**Morning:** Westminster Abbey, Parliament Square and views across Westminster Bridge.

**Lunch:** Westminster or Covent Garden rather than the immediate bridge approaches.

**Afternoon:** walk through St James's Park to Buckingham Palace, then continue through Trafalgar Square and the National Gallery.

**Evening:** dinner in Covent Garden, Soho or Chinatown; add a West End performance if energy allows.

## Day 2: Tower, City and South Bank

**Morning:** reserve an early Tower of London entry, then see Tower Bridge.

**Lunch:** Borough Market or the London Bridge area.

**Afternoon:** walk the South Bank past Shakespeare's Globe and Tate Modern. Choose either a focused Tate visit or continue to the London Eye area.

**Evening:** riverside dinner, a National Theatre performance or an early night.

## Day 3: Choose one museum district

**Option A – South Kensington:** Natural History Museum, V&A or Science Museum; select two rather than attempting all three. Finish in Hyde Park or Chelsea.

**Option B – Bloomsbury:** British Museum, British Library and King's Cross/Coal Drops Yard.

**Evening:** book a meal close to the hotel and revisit the neighbourhood you most enjoyed.

## What to omit

Greenwich, Camden, Notting Hill and Windsor are worthwhile, but forcing all of them into three days produces more transport than experience. Add them on a longer trip.

## Best areas for your visit

For maximum walking, use [Covent Garden](/articles/covent-garden-area-guide/), [Soho](/articles/soho-area-guide/) or the [South Bank](/articles/south-bank-area-guide/). For rail convenience, use [King's Cross](/articles/kings-cross-area-guide/) or [Bloomsbury](/articles/bloomsbury-area-guide/).
