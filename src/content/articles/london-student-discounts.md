Most student savings in London come from **free young-person schemes run by theatres and cinemas**, which usually go by age, not student status, so they work for anyone under 25 or 30 whether they study or not. Join them before you book: most are an online account with your date of birth.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** For theatre, the **National Theatre** sells £5 and £10 seats to 16 to 25s, the **Almeida** £5 seats to anyone 25 or under, and **Park Theatre**, **Hampstead Theatre** and **Sadler's Wells** £10 seats to under-30s. West End musicals are **£17.25 to £37.25** at London Theatre Direct's student prices. For films, **BFI Southbank** is £4 for 16 to 25s and **Young Barbican** £6 for 16 to 29s. For travel, an **18+ Student Oyster** takes 30% off Travelcards. Restaurant offers, mostly 20% to 30% off food, are in our [student restaurant discounts](/articles/student-discounts-london/) guide.

> ⚠️ **Bring ID that shows your age.** Young-person tickets are checked at the door or the box office, and a student card without a date of birth may not be enough.

## Exhibitions

**[Tate Collective](https://www.tate.org.uk/tate-collective) gets 16 to 25s into Tate exhibitions for £5**, and you can bring up to three friends the same age. **[Young Barbican](https://www.barbican.org.uk/join-support/young-barbican)** covers the Barbican's art exhibitions and concerts as well as its theatre and cinema, from £6. Both are free to join, and the permanent collections at the national museums are free to everyone.

## Travel

**The [18+ Student Oyster photocard](https://tfl.gov.uk/how-to-pay/discounts-and-photocards/18-plus-student-oyster-photocard) takes 30% off adult-rate Travelcards and Bus & Tram Pass season tickets.** It is for full-time students at a college or university registered with TfL's scheme, and costs £22 to apply. It does not discount pay as you go.

**A [16-25 Railcard](https://www.16-25railcard.co.uk/) takes a third off rail fares**, and once it is loaded onto a registered Oyster card it takes a third off off-peak pay as you go fares on the Tube and the DLR. Mature students over 25 in full-time education qualify too.

## Gyms

- **[PureGym](https://www.puregym.com/landing-page/student-membership/):** up to 30% off memberships for students through UNiDAYS; the discount depends on the gym and the plan.
- **[Gymbox](https://gymbox.com/students/):** up to 30% off monthly, three-month and 12-month plans for verified students, and a free trial at any club with no card details needed.
- **[Better](https://www.better.org.uk/leisure-centre/memberships/student):** a cheaper student membership at its council leisure centres, with no long contract, for anyone 16 or over on a course of 16 or more hours a week.

Day passes, joining-fee offers and the full price bands are in our [London gyms guide](/articles/best-gyms-london/).
