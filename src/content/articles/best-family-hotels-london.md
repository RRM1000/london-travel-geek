Twenty-one hotels here are named by two or more independent sources specifically for families: places parents, family-travel bloggers and the travel press keep pointing at for a room that fits two adults and two children.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **[The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences](#the-athenaeum-hotel--residences)** is named by more sources for families than any other hotel here - a Mayfair children's concierge, interconnecting rooms and serviced Residences with a bunk bed on request. **[The Landmark London](#the-landmark-london)** and **[St. Ermin's Hotel](#st-ermins-hotel)** have the biggest standard family rooms; **[The Goring](#the-goring)** and **[The Rubens at the Palace](#the-rubens-at-the-palace)** are the two closest to Buckingham Palace. On the South Bank, **[London Marriott Hotel County Hall](#london-marriott-hotel-county-hall)** and **[Park Plaza County Hall](#park-plaza-county-hall)** sit beside the London Eye. Further out, only one hotel earned its place: **[Town Hall Hotel and Apartments](#town-hall-hotel-and-apartments)** in Bethnal Green, for a pool and a two-Michelin-starred restaurant downstairs. Prices below are for one room sleeping two adults and two children, on a November weekday; the cheapest of them is **£257**.

## The winners

These eight are named by six or more independent sources.

### The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences

*Mayfair · 2 min from Green Park · Typical family night £1,066 · Cited by 10 sources · [Hotels.com](hotelscom:175165)*

![The Victorian red-brick facade of The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences on Piccadilly with wrought-iron balconies and window boxes](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/the-athenaeum-exterior.jpg)

*The Athenaeum Hotel & Residences, Piccadilly.*

The room that fits a family of four is the two-bedroom, two-bathroom Royal Parks View Suite, or one of the 18 serviced Residences: 550 sq ft with a kitchen, washing machine and, on request, a bunk bed for children (the hotel's FAQ says "bunk beds available"). Ordinary rooms run 204 to 280 sq ft and interconnect on request. The draw is the dedicated Children's Concierge: Mumsnet's verdict singles it out for supplying "kites, bikes and duck food" and pre-stocking the room with a child's favourite toys before arrival, and Kids Are A Trip's contributor found the connecting rooms "sufficient" for a family stay. Green Park is two minutes' walk, and Hyde Park Corner and Buckingham Palace are both under ten. There is no big playroom on site and no pool.

### The Landmark London

*Marylebone · 1 min from Marylebone station · Typical family night £963 · Cited by 8 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:the-landmark-london)*

![The glass-roofed Winter Garden atrium at The Landmark London with tall palm trees, dining tables and courtyard balconies](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/the-landmark-london-winter-garden.jpg)

*The Winter Garden atrium, The Landmark London.*

The Landmark's own site lists an Executive Family Room ("two Double size beds and an open plan comfortable lounge area") alongside a Marylebone Family Suite and Family Studio. Mumsnet's review puts the family room at 560 sq ft, big enough for the play tents the hotel supplies on request, and the relaunched spa has a vitality pool with children's hours of 9 to 11am and 3 to 5pm. Glamour UK's account adds a tepee, a head-torch and a teddy bear at check-in, plus breakfast in the glass-roofed Winter Garden. Marylebone station is across the road. The atrium hosts a bottomless Sunday brunch, so ask for a room away from it if you want a quiet evening.

### The Goring

*Belgravia · 4 min from Victoria · Typical family night £2,564 · Cited by 8 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:the-goring)*

![The entrance to The Goring on Beeston Place with Union flags, topiaries and a uniformed doorman](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/the-goring-belgravia-frontage.jpg)

*The Goring, Belgravia.*

The Goring's own family-rooms page lists interconnecting rooms "sleeping two adults and up to three children (an additional bed can be provided)," with cots, bottle warmers and babysitters arranged for babies. The same family has run it for four generations, and The Independent, Glamour UK and Visit London all report a dressing-up box of British costumes, a Bedtime Story Library and a cuddly "Baa-bara" sheep waiting in the room. There is no pool, and the Michelin-starred Dining Room is a grown-up space, but the Veranda's afternoon tea has a children's menu. Buckingham Palace's garden wall runs along the street behind the hotel, and Victoria station is four minutes on foot. Book the interconnecting rooms specifically, since the standard room alone does not sleep a family of four.

### St. Ermin's Hotel

*Westminster · 3 min from St James's Park · Typical family night £591 · Cited by 8 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:st-ermins-hotel)*

![The leafy paved courtyard driveway leading to the red-brick facade of St. Ermin's Hotel in Westminster](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/st-ermins-hotel-courtyard.jpg)

*The courtyard entrance, St. Ermin's Hotel.*

The biggest standard family room in this guide, per St. Ermin's own site: "two queen beds, two bathrooms, a sofa bed and a sitting area... ideal for a young family of two adults and up to four children." Room categories across the hotel run 16 to 60 sqm. A Reddit user on r/marriott calls it "delightful and in a very convenient spot near the tube in Westminster". Kids Are A Trip and MummyTravels both single out a kids' concierge, a spy-themed trail around the building (it was a WWII intelligence base) and a terrace beehive that supplies honey at breakfast. There is no pool. St James's Park station is three minutes away, and Buckingham Palace is an eight-minute walk. Ask for the two-bathroom family suite by name; other rooms here are not sized for four.

### Taj 51 Buckingham Gate Suites and Residences

*Westminster · 5 min from St James's Park · Typical family night £948 · Cited by 7 sources · [Hotels.com](hotelscom:193292)*

![A modern kitchen in a suite at Taj 51 Buckingham Gate with induction hob, built-in microwave and fitted cabinetry](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/taj-51-buckingham-gate-kitchen.jpg)

*A kitchen at Taj 51 Buckingham Gate.*

Taj 51 is a suites-and-residences hotel: one, two and three-bedroom suites with full kitchens and washer-dryers, and complimentary infant cots for children aged 0 to 2, per the hotel's own site. Santorini Dave calls the two and three-bedroom residences "one of the best choices in London for larger families who want an apartment without giving up 5-star hotel service," and Family Hotel Expert, who stayed, notes a made-to-order cooked breakfast with Asian dishes alongside the buffet. It sits on Buckingham Gate, a few minutes' walk from Buckingham Palace, with the Michelin-starred Indian restaurant Quilon attached. There is no pool. It is priced as a residence rather than a hotel room, so it suits a week's stay more than a single night.

### The Rubens at the Palace

*Victoria · 3 min from Victoria station · Typical family night £727 · Cited by 7 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:the-rubens-at-the-palace)*

![A bedroom at The Rubens at the Palace with a king bed, blue patterned carpet, gold drapery and views over the Royal Mews](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/the-rubens-at-the-palace-room.jpg)

*A room at The Rubens at the Palace.*

The Rubens' own site describes "family wings, which connect several rooms with internal doors" and "can accommodate four to 12 people, depending on availability", booked through the reservations team rather than online. Ordinary interconnecting rooms are also available. Overlooking the Royal Mews at Buckingham Palace, it runs a Prince and Princess children's menu, a cupcake-decorating class and, per MummyTravels and Family Hotel Expert, a children's afternoon tea. Kids Are A Trip's review calls the child check-in and the milk-and-cookies bedtime service "the best hotel in London for kids." There is no pool. Victoria station, with its Gatwick Express and mainline connections, is three minutes' walk. Call ahead for the family wings in school holidays.

### The Berkeley

*Knightsbridge · 5 min from Knightsbridge station · Cited by 6 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:the-berkeley)*

![The rooftop swimming pool at The Berkeley with striped sun loungers and views across the treetops of Hyde Park](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/the-berkeley-rooftop-pool.jpg)

*The rooftop pool, The Berkeley.*

The Berkeley's own homepage lists "two sublime pools," including a heated open-air rooftop pool for hotel guests, open in spring and summer. The Independent's account of a family stay adds that "interconnecting suites offer plenty of space for parents to unwind as little ones enjoy their own sleepover." Families staying in a suite get a complimentary second bedroom; a standard room plus a second room is half price. Every child's bed arrives with balloons, a mini bathrobe and child-safe toiletries, and the children's concierge runs piping masterclasses with pâtissier Cédric Grolet's team. Knightsbridge station and Harrods are both a five-minute walk. Check the rooftop pool is open before booking around it.

### Claridge's

*Mayfair · 4 min from Bond Street · Typical family night £4,131 · Cited by 6 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:claridges)*

![The red-brick and terracotta facade of Claridge's hotel on Brook Street in Mayfair under a blue sky](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/claridges-brook-street.jpg)

*Claridge's, Brook Street.*

Glamour UK reports connecting rooms, a personalised Claridge's teddy bear and umbrella for children, a choice of "comic" delivered with the grown-ups' papers, and warm milk and a cookie at turndown. Condé Nast Traveller and Conde Nast Traveler both list it on their family shortlists, and The Independent notes children's menus run across the hotel's restaurants, including a version of its afternoon tea. Babysitting is £60 for the first four hours. Bond Street's Elizabeth line platform is five minutes away. There is no pool. Request connecting rooms directly with reservations; Claridge's does not publish a self-contained family suite the way The Goring or St. Ermin's do.

*A suite at The Ritz London.*

The Ritz's own room pages list "family rooms" alongside its Junior Suites, with no layout or square-metre figure, unlike its Deluxe (42 sqm) and Grand Deluxe (55 sqm) rooms. Visit London and Glamour UK both describe the Kidz@TheRitz programme: a children's version of the Palm Court afternoon tea, in-room games consoles, and the dress code suspended for under-16s. *A suite at Royal Lancaster London.*

### London Marriott Hotel County Hall

*South Bank · 8 min from Waterloo · Typical family night £581 · Cited by 5 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:marriott-county-hall)*

![The 25-metre indoor swimming pool at London Marriott Hotel County Hall with loungers, palms and a glass roof](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/london-marriott-hotel-county-hall-pool.jpg)

*The pool at London Marriott Hotel County Hall.*

Mumsnet's review describes "big, airy family rooms with two double beds" in the same riverside building as the London Eye, plus a 25-metre pool in the hotel's health club, The Club at County Hall, free to guests from 7am to 9pm; there is no lifeguard, so children under 16 swim with an adult. Kids Are A Trip singles out the Executive Double Double, which sleeps four, and a two-bedroom suite for five. Marcie in Mommyland, writing from friends' reports rather than her own stay, warns that "some rooms are far from the elevators" in this converted historic building, and there is no direct step-free route to every room, so request a room near the lifts at booking, not at check-in. Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament are across the river.

## Further out, on a fast line

A hotel further out had to have two or more independent sources naming it for families and pass the direct-train rule in our [budget hotels guide](/articles/best-budget-hotels-london/): a direct train to the West End or the City in about 30 minutes. Only one cleared both.

### Town Hall Hotel and Apartments

*Bethnal Green · 5 min from Bethnal Green · Cited by 2 sources · [Hotels.com](hotel:town-hall-hotel)*

![The 14-metre indoor swimming pool at Town Hall Hotel in Bethnal Green with brass railings, mosaic tiles and overhead skylights](../../assets/articles/best-family-hotels-london/town-hall-hotel-pool.jpg)

## Booking essentials

**Connecting and interconnecting rooms sell out first**, at every hotel in this guide that offers them. Book as far ahead as the hotel allows, and call reservations directly rather than relying on a website filter to guarantee the pairing.

**Cots are free almost everywhere**, but a few hotels put an age limit on it: Taj 51 Buckingham Gate's complimentary cots cover ages 0 to 2, and The Georgian House's extra bed is for children up to 12. Ask when you book rather than on arrival: Marcie in Mommyland's family was told at check-in that all the cots were already out.

**The Georgian House Hotel has no lift**, and some rooms are up flights of stairs. London Marriott Hotel County Hall's historic building also means some rooms are a long walk from the lifts; ask for one nearby when you book.

**Every price here is one room that sleeps the four of you**, priced on Hotels.com for Wednesday 11 to Thursday 12 November 2026, two adults and two children, taxes and fees included. Six hotels carry no price because none of them sells a room for four on that night: at The Berkeley, The Ritz, Royal Lancaster, The Lanesborough, The Ampersand and Town Hall, a family of four is two rooms or an interconnecting pair, arranged with the hotel. For prices across the year, our [budget hotels guide](/articles/best-budget-hotels-london/) prices hotels on five dates.

**If you want a kitchen more than a concierge**, several aparthotels came up again and again: Cheval's Three Quays and Harrington Court, Citadines Trafalgar Square, SACO Holborn and Wilde Aparthotels among them. They get their own guide: see [London's aparthotels](/articles/aparthotels-london/). For a bed rather than a suite, [the best hostels in London](/articles/best-hostels-london/) covers the family rooms at YHA Earl's Court and YHA St Pancras.
