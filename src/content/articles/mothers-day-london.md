**Mothering Sunday 2027 is Sunday 7 March**, three weeks before Easter. The Ritz has published its Mother's Day lunch, and the restaurants and pubs below open their tables for the day on the dates in the table.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Sunday 7 March 2027.** **The Ritz** has published its **Mother's Day lunch: from £260 a head, £140 for children**, with Champagne, live music and a gift for every mother, paid in full when you book. For a **Sunday roast**, book on the day the pub's window opens: **the Harwood Arms** from about **7 December**, **the Canton Arms** from **7 February**. **Afternoon tea** is **£95 at The Ritz and Claridge's** and **£84 at Fortnum & Mason**, or **from £50 on a City Cruises boat** from Tower Millennium Pier. **Côte** served a Mother's Day set menu at **£32.50 for two courses** in 2026. **Columbia Road Flower Market** is on, as every Sunday, from 8am.

> ⚠️ **Some standing offers exclude Mother's Day.** Escocesa's half-price oysters in Stoke Newington, normally all day on Sundays, are not served on Mother's Day, Father's Day or bank holidays, and Côte replaced its main menu with the set menu in 2026. Check a deal's terms before you plan the bill around it.

## Mother's Day lunch

### The Ritz Restaurant, Piccadilly

*££££ · 150 Piccadilly · Sunday 7 March 2027 · pre-paid*

**A three-course Mother's Day Sunday lunch** cooked by Executive Chef John Williams MBE's brigade, with **a glass of Barons de Rothschild Prestige Cuvée Ritz Champagne**, live entertainment in the restaurant and **a gift for every mother**. **From £260 per adult and £140 per child**, and **full pre-payment is taken when you book** ([The Ritz](https://www.theritzlondon.com/offers/mothers-day-lunch/)). The Ritz Restaurant was named Restaurant of the Year at the National Restaurant Awards 2025. **Men must wear a jacket and tie, and jeans and sportswear are not allowed for anyone.**

### Côte

*££ · French brasserie chain · Soho and branches across London*

For Mother's Day 2026, Côte served a **set menu at £32.50 for two courses and £37.50 for three**, in place of its main menu from 11.30am: **potted crab with lobster butter** to start, then **moules marinière or steak frites**. Before 11.30am it served its usual breakfast with a Mother's Day dish, beetroot smoked salmon and scrambled eggs on seeded sourdough ([Côte](https://www.cote.co.uk/occasions/mothers-day)).

### Harvester

*£ · grill restaurant chain · Sunday set menu*

Harvester runs a **Sunday set menu of two or three courses from £22.99** every Sunday ([Harvester offers](https://www.harvester.co.uk/offers)).

## A Sunday roast

These are from our [Sunday roast guide](/articles/best-sunday-roast-london/), which has the full entries. **Book on the day the window opens.**

### The Harwood Arms, Fulham

*£££ · Walham Grove · Michelin-starred pub*

**London's only Michelin-starred pub**, with **wild venison** from the deer land run by one of its directors. Roasts run **all day Sunday until 8.15pm**. **Tables open 90 days ahead**, which puts 7 March in reach from about 7 December 2026, and **a no-show costs £50 a head** ([The Harwood Arms](https://harwoodarms.com/)).

### The Canton Arms, Stockwell

*££ · South Lambeth Road · walk-ins kept back*

**Eleventh in the Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026**, and it still keeps tables back for anyone who turns up. **Bookings open four weeks ahead**, so from 7 February 2027, then the rest go to walk-ins. **Sunday lunch stops at 3.45pm and there is no Sunday dinner** ([The Canton Arms](https://cantonarms.com/bookings/)).

### The Tamil Crown, Islington

*££ · Elia Street · all day Sunday*

**South Indian sharing plates in a restored Islington pub**, from the team behind The Tamil Prince. It serves **all day Sunday, 11am to 9.30pm** ([book for Sunday](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/thetamilcrown/reservations/create/search/)).

### Duck & Waffle, City of London

*££££ · 110 Bishopsgate · forty floors up · open 24 hours*

Not a roast, but a **meal forty floors up in the Heron Tower**, with the sealed floor-to-ceiling windows over the City. Order the dish it is named after: confit duck leg, a fried duck egg and mustard maple syrup on a waffle. **Reservations open two months ahead**, so 7 March becomes bookable in early January; it is card only ([Duck & Waffle](https://duckandwaffle.com/)).

## Afternoon tea

Our [afternoon tea guide](/articles/best-afternoon-tea-london/) covers more than thirty, with prices and dress codes. The ones most booked for Mother's Day:

### The Ritz, Piccadilly

*££££ · the Palm Court · dress code enforced*

**Five sittings a day, at 11.30am, 1.30pm, 3.30pm, 5.30pm and 7.30pm**, in the Palm Court with a pianist or harpist. Finger sandwiches, scones with Cornish clotted cream, and a cake trolley wheeled to the table. **£95 a head, £73 for children**, Champagne from £26 a glass. **Weekend sittings book months out**, and the jacket-and-tie rule applies here too, waived only for under-16s ([The Ritz](https://www.theritzlondon.com/dine-with-us/afternoon-tea/)).

### Claridge's, Mayfair

*££££ · the Foyer and Reading Room*

Served on the hotel's **jade-and-white striped china** with smoked Scottish salmon among the savouries and a house Claridge's Blend tea. **£95 a head, £110 with Champagne, £125 with rosé** ([Claridge's](https://www.claridges.co.uk/)).

### Fortnum & Mason, Piccadilly

*££££ · the Diamond Jubilee Tea Salon, fourth floor*

**The tea merchant's own salon**, opened by the Queen in 2012. Suffolk cured ham with piccalilli, warm plain and fruit scones, and **refills of the stand and the pot without asking**. **£84 a head** with a pot of Fortnum's tea ([Fortnum & Mason](https://www.fortnumandmason.com/)).

### On the Thames

*From £50 · 90 minutes · Tower Millennium Pier*

**City Cruises' afternoon tea cruise** sails once a day, **boarding at 3.15pm for 3.30pm to 5pm**: sandwiches, pastries and scones with **unlimited tea and coffee**. **Window tables seat two only**, so a family of four will not get one ([City Cruises](https://www.cityexperiences.com/london/city-cruises/afternoon-tea-cruise-river-thames/)). The [afternoon tea bus tours](/articles/afternoon-tea-bus-tours-london/) are the other way to eat and sightsee at once.

## Free things to do

- **[Columbia Road Flower Market](https://www.columbiaroad.info/)** in Bethnal Green trades every Sunday from **8am until about 3pm**, so it is open on Mothering Sunday.
- **[Sky Garden](/articles/sky-garden-guide/)** is free with a timed ticket. Tickets come out every Monday morning, three weeks ahead, a week at a time, so **the week of 1 to 7 March is released on Monday 8 February 2027**.
