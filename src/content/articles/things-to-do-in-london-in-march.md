March 2027 has the last Six Nations weekend, St Patrick's Day in Trafalgar Square and the Carabao Cup final, and then an early Easter: Good Friday is 26 March, and the clocks go forward on Easter Sunday. It is also the biggest month of the spring for exhibition openings, with six major shows starting between 4 and 26 March.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **England v Scotland is at Twickenham on Saturday 13 March at 4.40pm**, and the **St Patrick's Festival** fills Trafalgar Square the next day, **Sunday 14 March**. **The Carabao Cup final is at Wembley on Sunday 21 March.** **Easter is early: Good Friday is 26 March, Easter Monday 29 March**, and the school holidays start on the Friday and run to **9 April**. **The clocks go forward on Easter Sunday, 28 March**, and large shops are closed that day. **German Expressionism** opens at the National Gallery on **18 March**, **Mary Cassatt** at the National Portrait Gallery on **11 March** and **Punk to Pop** at the V&A on **13 March**. **Mothering Sunday is 7 March.**

## Easter — Good Friday 26 March to Easter Monday 29 March

**Easter Sunday falls on 28 March in 2027**, so the whole bank holiday weekend is in March: Good Friday on the 26th and Easter Monday on the 29th, both on [GOV.UK's bank holiday list](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays). The Easter school holidays in Camden, Lambeth and Royal Greenwich start on Good Friday and run to **Friday 9 April**, so the family attractions stay busy into April.

> ⚠️ **Large shops close on Easter Sunday.** Shops over 280 square metres must close by law, which takes in the department stores and most big supermarkets. Smaller shops, pubs and restaurants can open. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/trading-hours-for-retailers-the-law) has the rule.

The clocks also go forward on Easter Sunday, at 1am. The National Gallery closes only on 24 to 26 December and 1 January, so it is open all weekend. **St Paul's Cathedral opens for sightseeing Monday to Saturday only**, so Easter Sunday there means a service rather than a ticketed visit. With children, our [London with children guide](/articles/london-with-children/) has what is worth the queues.

## Mothering Sunday — 7 March

**Mothering Sunday is three weeks before Easter Sunday, so it falls on 7 March in 2027.** Afternoon tea is the usual booking, and the popular ones fill up weeks ahead; our [best afternoon tea guide](/articles/best-afternoon-tea-london/) has the options and the prices.

## St Patrick's Day — festival on Sunday 14 March

**The Mayor of London's [St Patrick's Festival](https://www.london.gov.uk/events/st-patricks-day) is on Sunday 14 March 2027, from 12pm to 6pm, and it is free.** The parade runs from Hyde Park Corner along Piccadilly to Trafalgar Square, where the music, food and family activities run through the afternoon. St Patrick's Day itself is the following Wednesday, 17 March.

## Sport

### England v Scotland at Twickenham — Saturday 13 March

**England's last Six Nations game of 2027 is against Scotland at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, on Saturday 13 March at 4.40pm**, with tickets from [England Rugby](https://www.englandrugby.com/tickets). England are away to Wales the week before, on Saturday 6 March. Gates, bag rules and the station queues are in our [Twickenham Stadium travel guide](/articles/twickenham-stadium-travel-guide/).

### The Carabao Cup final — Sunday 21 March

**The EFL's Carabao Cup final is at Wembley on Sunday 21 March.** Tickets go through the two finalists' clubs, not Wembley, once they are known ([EFL](https://www.efl.com/competitions/carabao-cup/)). The queues at Wembley Park after the final whistle are covered in our [Wembley Stadium travel guide](/articles/wembley-stadium-arena-guide/).

### Saracens at Tottenham, and the Head of the River Race — Saturday 20 March

**Saracens take their Premiership home game against Harlequins to Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Saturday 20 March** for The Showdown 7, with tickets [on sale from the stadium](https://www.tottenhamhotspurstadium.com/events/1181477/saracens-the-showdown-7). The same afternoon, the **[Head of the River Race](https://www.horr.co.uk/)** sends a procession of eights down the Thames from Mortlake to Putney, starting at 2.45pm: the Boat Race course in reverse, free to watch from the towpath. The Boat Race itself is on 11 April. The rest of the year's dates are in [London sporting events 2027](/articles/london-sporting-events-2027/).

## Exhibitions

### Opening in March

- **Catharina van Hemessen: Signature Works**, National Gallery, **4 March to 30 May**. The first UK exhibition of the Antwerp painter, the only 16th-century Flemish woman painter known to have signed her pictures: small portraits in Room 1, free. [National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/catharina-van-hemessen).
- **Mary Cassatt: Radical Impressionist**, National Portrait Gallery, **11 March to 13 June**. The first UK retrospective of the American painter, whose work the gallery calls central to Impressionism. Tickets are not yet on sale on the [National Portrait Gallery's listings](https://www.npg.org.uk/whatson/events-calendar/?what=exhibition).
- **Punk to Pop**, V&A South Kensington, **from 13 March**. Music and fashion from 1972 to 1985, made by a generation of artists working in punk's do-it-yourself spirit. Tickets are listed as available soon on the [V&A's page](https://www.vam.ac.uk/exhibitions/punk-to-pop).
- **Matt Copson**, Serpentine North, **16 March to 22 August**, free. [Serpentine](https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/whats-on/matt-copson/).
- **German Expressionism: Modern Painting 1900–1918**, National Gallery, Sainsbury Wing, **18 March to 1 August**. Fifty works from the years before the First World War scattered the movement's artists. Members go free; the [National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/german-expressionism-modern-painting-1900-1918) has not yet put a price on it.
- **Sonia Boyce**, Tate Britain, **26 March to 22 August**. Tate's first survey of her forty-year career: installation, photography, collage, film and the early pastel drawings. Price to be confirmed on [Tate's page](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/sonia-boyce).

### Closing in March

- **Gathering Ground**, Tate Modern, free, to **7 March**.
- **Andrius Arutiunian: They Tried to Count Us**, Hayward Gallery, free, to **7 March**.
- **Peggy Guggenheim in London**, Royal Academy, to **14 March**: the Cork Street gallery she opened in January 1938, which gave Kandinsky his first UK solo show. £17.
- **Nan Goldin: You Never Did Anything Wrong**, Hayward Gallery, to **14 March**: four of her recent films with photographs from her archive, her first UK institutional show since 2002. £19, closed Mondays; the gallery advises 12 and over. [Southbank Centre](https://www.southbankcentre.co.uk/whats-on/nan-goldin-you-never-did-anything-wrong/).
- **Multiplied wonders: Italian Renaissance woodcuts**, British Museum, free, to **21 March**.
- **Cosimo I de' Medici: Art and Dynasty**, a free display at the Wallace Collection, to **21 March**.

### Still running

**Monet: Painting Time** at Tate Modern runs to 27 June, at £26, and stays open to 10pm on selected dates from 22 March ([Tate](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/monet)). **Van Eyck: The Portraits** at the National Gallery is £18 and runs to 11 April; **Peter Blake: Pop Art Life** at the National Portrait Gallery runs to 23 May. The **Bayeux Tapestry** at the British Museum is sold out to 31 March; tickets for April to July are due in early 2027 on the [British Museum's page](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry). The whole year's openings, with how each sells tickets, are in [London exhibitions 2027](/articles/london-exhibitions-2027/).

## Practical: March in London

**Weather.** The Met Office's Heathrow average for March (1991 to 2020) is a daily high of **11.7C** and a low of **4.1C**, with **38.8mm of rain**, the least of any month, **3 air-frost days** and **120 hours of sunshine**. Our [London in the rain guide](/articles/london-in-the-rain/) has the indoor fallbacks.

**Daylight.** Sunset is **5.40pm on 1 March** and **6.24pm on Saturday 27 March**. The clocks go forward overnight, and sunset on Easter Sunday is **7.26pm**, then **7.31pm on the 31st**. The day grows from 10 hours 54 minutes to 12 hours 53 minutes.

**Closed.** The **Royal Observatory** in Greenwich is shut for refurbishment; the [Meridian Courtyard reopens in summer 2027](https://www.rmg.co.uk/royal-observatory/transformation), with tickets on sale from January.

**Crowds.** There is no half term in March, so the first three weeks are quiet at the family attractions until the Easter holidays start on the 26th. The Twickenham and Wembley weekends fill the trains in and out.

**Getting around.** Our [getting around London guide](/articles/getting-around-london-transport-guide/) covers the Tube and the alternatives, and [London transport costs](/articles/london-public-transport-costs-and-fares/) has the fares and caps.

**Choosing your month?** [Best time to visit London](/articles/best-time-to-visit-london/) compares March with the rest of the year. [February's guide](/articles/things-to-do-in-london-in-february/) covers the month before, and [April's guide](/articles/things-to-do-in-london-in-april/) has the Boat Race, the two-day Marathon and the second week of the Easter holidays.
