A bottomless brunch is a fixed price for a food dish plus unlimited drinks inside a set window, and in London that window is usually ninety minutes. Check **when the clock starts, and which drinks "bottomless" covers.** At Flight Club it covers the pizza and not the drinks. At Megan's it covers the drinks and not the food.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Big Easy** at around £41.50 buys two hours of unlimited drinks *and* unlimited barbecue. **Las Iguanas** at £39.95 gives you two full hours and a main course. **The Breakfast Club's Mayor's Boozy Brunch** at £45 includes cocktails. **No 32 The Old Town** in Clapham is £39. And **Megan's £25 headline is drinks only**: a main meal is compulsory on top.

> ⚠️ **Ninety minutes rarely means ninety minutes of drinking.** Revolution and Big Easy both stop taking drinks orders fifteen minutes before the end, and All Bar One and Revolution start the clock at your order time, not when you settle in.

## The best bottomless brunches in London

**Big Easy**: the weekend Boozy Brunch is £35.50 for **unlimited** home-smoked barbecue — ribs, chicken, pulled pork, cornbread, beans and slaw — with one drink included, and £5 to £6 more upgrades that drink to unlimited Prosecco, beer, frozen mojito or wine for two hours. It runs Saturday and Sunday until 4pm, with a maximum table of twelve and a last seating at 3.45pm. You pick one drink and stay with it, and drinks orders stop fifteen minutes before your two hours are up. Canary Wharf, Covent Garden and Chelsea.

**Las Iguanas** gives two hours where most rivals give ninety minutes. For £39.95 you get a main dish and a choice of twenty-five drinks including cocktails, beer, fizz and spirits, at £0.33 a minute. It is pre-booking only with no walk-ins, and the whole table has to take part. **The one trap:** bottomless brunch is not available at the company's event locations on event days, including the Royal Festival Hall on the South Bank, The O2 and Wembley. Check the date before you book the South Bank one.

**The Breakfast Club's Mayor's Boozy Brunch** is the one with cocktails. £45 buys two hours at The Mayor of Scaredy Cat Town, the speakeasy behind the Spitalfields branch, with bottomless fizz, beer **and** cocktails plus a brunch dish each, every Saturday. The cheaper Soho version at Backseat Becky is £35 for the same two hours, with fizz, mimosas and beer but no cocktails.

**No 32 The Old Town** in Clapham runs ninety minutes, Saturday and Sunday, 11am to 4pm, for groups of any size, in four tiers: **£39** for Prosecco, Pravha and mimosas, **£49** adding five cocktails, **£59** adding Whispering Angel and **£79** adding Veuve Clicquot. Every tier includes a shared hummus starter and your choice of brunch dish.

**Sticks'n'Sushi** is the one to book when the food comes before the drinking. £70 for ninety minutes is £0.78 a minute, the highest in the table, and buys a sharing menu — beef tataki, karaage, gyoza, tuna tartare, maki, nigiri, grilled sticks and a mango dessert. Every Saturday, noon to 5pm, last sitting 3.30pm, with bookings from one person up to twelve, so it works for a couple. **Budget for the 12.5% service charge on top**, which takes £70 to £78.75.

**Cottons**: £34.50 buys ninety minutes of bottomless rum punch, Prosecco, Cottons Lager or Caribbean Pimm's alongside a starter and a main, to a reggae soundtrack, in Camden, Shoreditch, Notting Hill or Vauxhall. **The whole £34.50 is taken at the time of booking**, not as a deposit. Plan on ninety minutes.

**Revolution**: two hours from £35 Sunday to Friday and £37.50 on Saturday, with unlimited Prosecco, mimosas, pints of Pravha and Kopparberg, and a cocktail list, plus a brunch dish. The clock starts when your order is taken, your current drink must be finished before the next is ordered, and last orders come fifteen minutes before the end.

**Flight Club**: £33 for two hours across Bloomsbury, Islington, Shoreditch and Victoria, with bottomless sharing pizza and an hour of darts. **The drinks are not bottomless**: each guest picks a bottle of Prosecco, four bottles of beer, a bottle of 0% Prosecco or four 0% beers.

## Cheap bottomless brunches, under £35

The cheapest headline numbers are almost all drinks-only packages, and the food you must buy alongside them closes the gap.

**Megan's Classic at £25 for ninety minutes** is drinks only and must be paired with a main meal; add a main and you are realistically at £40. You get unlimited Prosecco, mimosas, bellinis, spritzes and draught beer, served all day every day, across Clapham, Islington, Notting Hill and a dozen more.

**The Little Yellow Door in Notting Hill** runs the same model: buy any brunch dish and add ninety minutes of bottomless drinks for £23.50, every Saturday. For groups of ten or more it switches to a family-style set menu with drinks included at £38.50 a head.

**Slug & Lettuce**: from £33.95 for **two hours** with a brunch dish included, seven days a week, at Waterloo, Clapham Junction, Islington and Fulham. An upgrade is £5. Prices vary by site and time of day, so check the branch.

**Two more.** Las Iguanas runs a **student session at £24.95** for two hours, Monday to Thursday, with a main dish. **Mr Bao in Peckham prices bottomless drinks by the hour at £24** — prosecco, mimosas, spicy Bloody Marys and Bloody Marias — so you extend rather than get cut off. Food is separate.

Food arrives as a birdcage between two, so a six is served three. Deposits are low with the balance paid on the day.

**Swingers** bundles the drinking with something to do: £50 for ninety minutes of bottomless drinks, street food, a reserved area and thirty minutes of unlimited retro arcade play at the West End and City sites. ## How to read a bottomless brunch offer

**When the clock starts.** At All Bar One the ninety minutes run from the time of ordering, and at Revolution the two hours run from when your order is taken. **Turn up on time and order immediately.**

**When it really stops.** Revolution takes last orders fifteen minutes before the end. Big Easy requires drinks orders fifteen minutes before the two-hour table limit. A stated two hours is 105 minutes of ordering.

**One drink at a time.** All Bar One limits you to one drink per person at a time, and Revolution requires your drink to be finished before the next is ordered. **The pace of service, not the price, determines the value.**

**Whether food is included.** Megan's, The Little Yellow Door and Mr Bao all sell drinks-only packages with food charged separately. Barrio and Dirty Martini include food, but as one sharing board between two people. Flight Club inverts it entirely and makes the pizza bottomless while capping the drinks.

**Who else has to join in.** Megan's, Turtle Bay, Las Iguanas and Revolution all require the whole table to take the same package. Megan's makes allowances for groups with pregnant or dry members; ask at the others.

**What gets added at the end.** Sticks'n'Sushi adds 12.5%, taking £70 to £78.75. **Add an eighth to every number on this page and you will not be surprised.**

Book the **first sitting of the day**. And check the deposit before you commit: Revolution wants £5 a head, Ballie Ballerson wants fifty per cent up front, Cottons wants the entire £34.50, and Turtle Bay requires card validation for any booking of five or more.
