London has no coastline, but Cornish day boats, Devon crab, Scottish langoustines and oysters from Essex and Dorset beds reach its menus the morning after they leave the water.

Seafood here comes three ways: shucked at an **oyster counter**, cooked whole over **charcoal**, or bought across a **fishmonger's slab** and cooked in the room behind it. Every restaurant below is placed by how many independent awards, critics and reviewers name it, and the two most-cited, J Sheekey (since 1896) and Scott's (since 1851), are among the oldest.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **J Sheekey** is the most-cited seafood restaurant in London, and **Scott's** is second. **Wiltons** has been trading since 1742. **The Sea, The Sea** is a working fishmonger with a bistro above it. **Behind** and **Angler** are the starred fish kitchens. **Manzi's** and **Sam's Riverside** do £2 oysters. And **Billingsgate** is open to the public before dawn.

## The most-cited seafood restaurants in London

### J Sheekey, Covent Garden

*££££ · Leicester Square · Cited by 9 sources*

Nine independent sources name it: five editorial mastheads, two specialists and two video reviewers.

**Since 1896**, in a warren of small wood-panelled rooms off St Martin's Court, with a central crustacea bar and a fish pie. The Infatuation calls it a West End post-theatre hangout where you are guaranteed a good seafood meal; Time Out puts it fourth on its ranking and calls it the grand dame of theatreland.

Sit at the counter for half a dozen and the fish pie rather than booking the dining room.

![A silver platter on crushed ice holding a dozen oysters, a razor clam, a dressed scallop in its shell and lemon wedges](../../assets/articles/best-seafood-restaurants-london/j-sheekey-shellfish-platter.jpg)

*The crustacea counter at J Sheekey.*

**Book:** [j-sheekey.co.uk](https://j-sheekey.co.uk/) · 28–34 St Martin's Court, WC2N 4AL

### Scott's, Mayfair

*££££ · Green Park · Cited by 7 sources*

**Trading since 1851** in a Mount Street room with a crustacea counter. The order is oysters — Gillardeau, Lindisfarne Rocks and Jersey Rocks — whole grilled lobster, and Dover sole meunière on the bone. Hot Dinners describes it as a gold-plated celebrity hangout that also happens to be one of London's oldest restaurants.

There is a second site in Richmond with a Thames view, which The Infatuation rates for a long lunch. Sit at the crustacea counter rather than at a table.

Two policies to check before you book: **under-sixes are admitted only at weekend and holiday lunch**, and from May 2026 it takes **assistance dogs only**.

![The awnings and frontage of Scott's on Mount Street](../../assets/articles/best-seafood-restaurants-london/scott-s.jpg)

*Photo: [La Citta Vita](https://www.flickr.com/photos/49539505@N04/12620350394), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*

**Book:** [scotts-restaurant.com](https://scotts-restaurant.com/) · 20 Mount Street, W1K 2HE

### Bentley's Oyster Bar & Grill, Piccadilly

*££££ · Piccadilly Circus · Cited by 6 sources · Good Food Guide*

**Shucking since 1916** and now Richard Corrigan's. An oyster bar on the ground floor and a grill room above, where the Dover sole is; Hot Dinners treats them as two different evenings.

One of only eight restaurants here carrying a dated award, and Corrigan's own line in a 2026 video is about sourcing straight from farms and trawlers rather than through a wholesaler.

Sit at the oyster bar downstairs unless you want the full dinner.

**Book:** [bentleys.org](https://bentleys.org/) · 11–15 Swallow Street, W1B 4DG

### Wright Brothers

*£££ · Borough, Battersea and South Kensington · Cited by 6 sources · Good Food Guide*

**An oyster bar with fish arriving from Devon daily and a specials board that changes with the boat** — the company started as an oyster supplier and still farms its own in the Duchy of Cornwall's waters.

**Oysters** are the order: native and rock, listed by bed, with a proper shucking counter. Beyond them, whole fish, a **fruits de mer** for the table, and pies in winter.

**£££, book a few days ahead.** Borough Market, Soho and Battersea among others; the Borough site is the original.

![A whole plaice on the bone in a butter and parsley sauce, scattered with brown shrimp and diced cucumber, with a lemon half](../../assets/articles/best-seafood-restaurants-london/wright-brothers-plaice.jpg)

*Whole plaice at Wright Brothers.*

### Seabird, South Bank

*£££ · Blackfriars · Cited by 5 sources*

**Fourteen floors up on Blackfriars Road** from the team behind Brooklyn's Maison Premiere — a rooftop room with a terrace and a view south.

The proposition is oysters and the view: it claims **London's longest oyster list**, alongside ceviche, whole fish and a Mediterranean-leaning menu. **The oyster happy hour runs daily from 3pm to 6pm and is the cheapest way into the room.**

**£££ and it books weeks ahead** for a terrace table. Go at the happy hour and stay for sunset.

### Wiltons, St James's

*££££ · Green Park · Cited by 4 sources · closed Sundays*

**Founded in 1742** as a shellfish stall near the Haymarket.

A marble-topped oyster bar, Dover sole meunière and a carving trolley. It keeps a dress code. Saturday is dinner only, and Sunday it shuts.

> **It does not hold a Michelin star.** What it holds is a Michelin Guide listing.

![The frontage of Wiltons Restaurant on Jermyn Street at night](../../assets/articles/best-seafood-restaurants-london/wiltons.jpg)

*Wiltons, Jermyn Street. Photo: [Spudgun67](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=72552746), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).*

**Book:** [wiltons.co.uk](https://wiltons.co.uk/) · 55 Jermyn Street, SW1Y 6LX

### Parsons, Covent Garden

*£££ · Covent Garden · Cited by 4 sources · Good Food Guide · closed Sundays*

Tiny, chalkboard-only, and the menu changes every day according to what came in. Whole roasted turbot, gurnard, potted shrimp croquettes, Inverawe smoked salmon with Guinness bread.

An award, two mastheads and a specialist name it. The Infatuation calls it a sanctuary from the Covent Garden crowds.

**Book:** [parsonslondon.co.uk](https://parsonslondon.co.uk/) · 39 Endell Street, WC2H 9BA

### The Seafood Bar, Soho

*£££ · Tottenham Court Road · Cited by 4 sources*

**A Dutch import** — the family were **Volendam fishmongers from 1984** and opened the first Seafood Bar in Amsterdam in 2012. This is their only London site.

**The fruits de mer** is a tiered ice platter of oysters, langoustines, crab and prawns, brought out whole. Beyond it, grilled fish, fried plates and a raw bar, in a bright white-tiled room built around the ice counter.

**£££, book a few days ahead.** Dean Street.

![A round platter of ice holding oysters, mussels, clams, langoustines, a crab claw, smoked salmon and dressed crab, with three dipping sauces in the middle](../../assets/articles/best-seafood-restaurants-london/the-seafood-bar-fruits-de-mer.jpg)

*The fruits de mer at The Seafood Bar.*

### Randall & Aubin, Soho

*£££ · Piccadilly Circus · Cited by 4 sources · no bookings*

**The marble counters of the old butcher's shop are still in place and there is a disco ball over the dining room** — over twenty years on Brewer Street, and **it takes no bookings at all.**

Champagne and shellfish is the format: **oysters, lobster, a fruits de mer**, and rotisserie chicken. The music is loud and the seating is stools at high counters.

**£££, walk-in only** — put your name down and drink in Soho until they call. Busiest from seven; easiest at five or after ten.

## The old oyster houses

Several rooms still trading were founded before the First World War.

### Sweetings, City of London

*£££ · Mansion House · Cited by 3 sources · weekday lunch only*

**At its Queen Victoria Street site since 1889**, with a trading lineage back to 1830. You sit on stools at long marble counters while staff serve from behind.

West Mersea natives, **Dover sole at £31**, **fish pie at £13.50**, and a Black Velvet alongside. The Infatuation calls it "a historical landmark serving seafood to signet rings".

> ⚠️ **Weekday lunch only, 11.30am to 3pm, and no reservations.** There is no dinner service.

**More:** [sweetingsrestaurant.com](https://sweetingsrestaurant.com/) · 39 Queen Victoria Street, EC4N 4SF

### Manzi's, Soho

*£££ · Tottenham Court Road · Cited by 3 sources*

A two-storey seafood room off Soho Square with the oyster bar spilling into the alley:

* **Oyster Hour** — Poole rock oysters **from £2**, 3pm to 7pm Tuesday to Saturday and **all day Sunday**.
* **Lunch set menu** — two courses £16, three for £20.
* **Evening theatre menu** — three courses with a drink, £29.50.

It is **a 2023 opening from the Wolseley Hospitality Group**, not a continuation of the original Manzi's, which closed in Chinatown decades ago. It cooks the classics through a fish lens, so the Wellington is monkfish and the soufflé is smoked haddock.

**Book:** [manzis.co.uk](https://manzis.co.uk/) · 1 Bateman's Buildings, W1D 3EN

### The Oystermen, Covent Garden

*£££ · Covent Garden · Cited by 3 sources · Good Food Guide*

**A tiny Covent Garden room with about thirty covers**, built entirely around the shucking counter.

**Oysters** from British and Irish beds, listed and changed daily, with a short menu of whole crab, grilled fish and shellfish around them.

**£££, book a few days ahead** — it is small enough that walk-ins rarely work. Henrietta Street, two minutes from the piazza.

## Fishmongers that opened restaurants

You eat what the trade buys, in the building it is sold from.

### The Sea, The Sea, Chelsea

*££££ · Sloane Square · Cited by 3 sources*

**A fishmonger's counter with a chef's table behind it.**

By day it sells fish; in the evening it turns into a **counter tasting menu built entirely around what did not sell** — raw, cured and lightly cooked plates served to a handful of seats. The Hackney site does the same thing on a larger scale.

**£££ and the counter books weeks ahead.** Pavilion Road, Chelsea.

### Applebee's, Borough Market

*£££ · London Bridge · Cited by 2 sources · Time Out #3*

**A family-run Borough Market fixture since 1999 that started as a fishmonger and turned into a restaurant**, with **the counter still at the front and live-fire cooking behind it** — you buy fish at the door and eat it at the back.

The grill does whole fish and shellfish over charcoal: **whole seabass, king prawns, scallops in the shell**, plus a fish curry. Market produce means the board changes constantly.

**£££, book a few days ahead**, and note it keeps market hours — this is lunch and early evening rather than a late dinner.

### Behind, London Fields

*££££ · London Fields · Cited by 2 sources · Michelin star*

**Andy Beynon's eighteen-seat horseshoe counter took a Michelin star in January 2021 after roughly twenty days of trading.**

**A surprise tasting menu built entirely around fish**: you are told nothing in advance and the sequence depends on what arrived. Everything is cooked and served by the chefs across the counter, a foot from where you are sitting.

**££££, open Wednesday to Saturday only** — closed Sunday, Monday and Tuesday — **and it books weeks ahead.**

### Angler, Moorgate

*££££ · Moorgate · Cited by 2 sources · Michelin star · Good Food Guide*

**A rooftop dining room at the South Place Hotel** with **an all-seafood tasting menu built around British fish and shellfish**.

The menu is a fixed sequence of British catch treated at fine-dining level: **Cornish turbot, native lobster, Isle of Skye scallops**, with sauces reduced in the classical French manner. There is a terrace for the warmer months.

**££££ and it books weeks ahead.** Moorgate. The set lunch is significantly cheaper than the evening tasting menu.

### Faber, Hammersmith

*£££ · Hammersmith · Cited by 3 sources · Time Out #10*

**A light-filled corner room from a pair who ran a seafood pub in the East End first.**

**Watch the blackboard** — the menu changes with the catch — and the **chalkstream trout tartare** is the standing recommendation, a farmed British trout raised in chalk-fed rivers and served raw with sharp seasoning. Whole fish and shellfish behind it.

**£££, book a few days ahead.** Hammersmith.

### Orasay, Notting Hill

*£££ · Ladbroke Grove · Cited by 2 sources*

**Scottish and Irish seafood in a small Notting Hill room**, Jackson Boxer's kitchen.

**Hand-dived scallops, Hebridean langoustines, oysters from named beds**, cooked simply. The menu changes with the boats.

**£££, book a few days ahead.**

### Sam's Riverside, Hammersmith

*£££ · Hammersmith · Cited by 2 sources*

**A Thames-side room by Hammersmith Bridge with a terrace** — Sam Harrison's brasserie.

**Two courses at lunch**, built on whole fish, fish pie and a shellfish counter, in a bright room with the river through the windows. The à la carte runs to Dover sole and crab for anyone spending freely.

**£££, book a few days ahead** — the terrace goes first, so plan around the weather.

### Fish Market, Broadgate — closed

*Closed*

The 18th-century warehouse off Bishopsgate, once owned by the East India Company, **has closed**. Customers were sent to Liverpool Street Chop House.

*££££ · Green Park · Cited by 2 sources*

**Named in both The Handbook's survey and a 2026 video review.**

A Mayfair dining room built on British seafood at the luxury end: **native lobster, turbot, caviar service**, plated formally.

**££££ and it books ahead.**

## Oysters, and where they are cheap

Several oyster bars discount in the hours between lunch and dinner.

| Where | When | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Manzi's**, Soho | Tue–Sat 3–7pm, all day Sunday | Poole rocks **from £2** |
| **Sam's Riverside**, Hammersmith | Happy hour | Jersey Rock and Carlingford Lough **£2** |
| **Oyster Boy**, various | Standard | **£2 an oyster, six for £10** |
| **Seabird**, Southwark | Daily 3–6pm | Happy hour, fourteen floors up |
| **The Oystermen**, Covent Garden | Afternoons | Bubbles and oysters at the raw bar |

**And eaten standing up:**

* **Furness Oyster Bar**, Borough Market — 5 Bedale Street. A stall rather than a restaurant, named by both The Infatuation and Luxury London. Grab-and-go.
* **Richard Haward's Oysters**, Borough Market — wild rather than farmed, from the family's own patch of Mersea seabed.
* **Maltby Street Market**, Bermondsey — weekends only, in a railway arch.

> **Native oysters run September to April**; rock oysters are year-round. The natives cost more and are the ones the season applies to.

## The market

### Billingsgate Fish Market, Canary Wharf

*Free to enter · before dawn*

**The UK's largest inland fish market**, open from 4am to 8:30am, ten minutes' walk from the Canary Wharf towers — **the public can buy**.

Bring cash, bring a cool bag, and expect to buy in quantity — traders sell by the box rather than the fillet. **The traders' café does a bacon and scallop roll**.

**Free to enter, before dawn, and closed Sunday and Monday.** Wear non-slip closed shoes; the floor is wet throughout, and children under 12 and dogs are not allowed.

## What to know

* **Whole fish is priced by weight** and the waiter will show you the fish first. Ask what it will come to before agreeing.
* **Sweetings is weekday lunch only** and takes no bookings. Wiltons shuts on Sundays. Randall & Aubin takes no bookings at all.
* **Fish and chips is a separate guide** — a different meal with a different set of sources.
