Two-for-one in London is mostly a drinks offer: cocktail chains run it every day, and only a handful of restaurants do it on food. **The food deals are tied to one day of the week, one branch or one card, so check all three before you go.**

> 💡 **The Short Version:** For food, **Made in Italy** on the King's Road gives the second pizza by the metre free on Fridays if you book online with "2 X 1 PIZZA" in the notes. **Shiro** at Broadgate Circle does 2-for-1 maki on Mondays, and **Sambrook's Brewery Taproom** in Wandsworth two pizzas for one on Tuesdays. For drinks, **Turtle Bay**, **TGI Fridays**, **Banana Tree** and **Slug & Lettuce** pour 2-for-1 cocktails every day, and **Big Easy** does it Monday to Friday from 4pm to 6pm.

> ⚠️ **Most cocktail offers need two of the same drink, and none combine with another offer.** Order the pair together, and show any app or card before you order.

## 2-for-1 with a paid card

Tastecard, Gourmet Society and Dine Club each give 2-for-1 on mains at some of their restaurants, alongside 25% and 50% offers. Each costs a membership fee, and many of the restaurants on them don't accept the card on Friday or Saturday nights. Our [restaurant discount cards guide](/articles/restaurant-discount-cards-london/) compares the prices, the London lists and the small print.
