The first week was fireworks and ceremony. Ahead are Armistice Day and the Lord Mayor's Show, ten days of jazz, and Christmas lights, rinks and markets switching on in stages. The clocks went back on 25 October, and the sun sets before 4.40pm all month.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Armistice Day is Wednesday 11 November and the Lord Mayor's Show is Saturday 14th.** The **EFG London Jazz Festival** runs 13 to 22 November. **Bond Street's and Covent Garden's lights go on on 12 November**, **Winter Wonderland opens on 19 November** and the **London Museum** opens free in Smithfield on **28 November**. The **Bayeux Tapestry** is sold out to 31 March 2027. Bonfire Night, Remembrance Sunday and Diwali have passed.

## The Bayeux Tapestry: sold out to March 2027

*British Museum, Room 30 · to 11 July 2027*

**You cannot buy a ticket for a November visit.** Every ticket from 10 September 2026 to 31 March 2027 is gone, and the next release is in 2027. Tickets are on the [British Museum's Bayeux Tapestry page](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry). Adults pay **£33** (£36.50 with the £3.50 donation), or **£27** off-peak. A **£25** slot runs on term-time weekdays from 3.30pm to 4.20pm. Students, 16 to 18s and jobseekers pay £25, and under-16s go free with an adult, up to four per adult.

Bookings are capped at 10 tickets, each ticket can only be used by the person who booked it, and the museum may check ID. The Tapestry room is open Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 6pm and Thursday to Saturday 10am to 9pm, later than the rest of the museum, and the visit takes about 40 minutes. A free audio guide is in the British Museum Audio app; bring your own headphones.

**The women of the Bayeux Tapestry**, a talk on **20 November** (£50), falls in the month. The rest of the museum is in our [British Museum guide](/articles/british-museum-guide/).

## Fireworks and ceremony

### Bonfire Night: over for 2026

Bonfire Night was Thursday 5 November, and most displays moved to the weekend: **Alexandra Palace and Battersea Park both ran on Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November**, and Coram's Fields in Bloomsbury held the free display on the 5th. What each display cost, which ones sell out and the free viewpoints are in our [Bonfire Night guide](/articles/bonfire-night-london/), for planning next year.

### Remembrance Sunday and Armistice Day

**Remembrance Sunday was 8 November**, the second Sunday in the month. The National Service of Remembrance is held at the Cenotaph in Whitehall each year, and the Royal British Legion's Cenotaph Parade follows. [Our Westminster walk](/articles/westminster-walk/) passes it.

**Armistice Day is Wednesday 11 November**, with a Two Minute Silence at 11am.

### The Lord Mayor's Show — City of London, Saturday 14 November

The procession is free, over three miles long and with more than 7,000 people in 2025. It sets off from **Mansion House at 11am** and goes to the Royal Courts of Justice by St Paul's, Cheapside and Fleet Street.

The organiser's timetable puts the head of the procession at the Royal Courts by 11.26am. The return leg starts from Temple Place at **1.10pm** and comes back along the Embankment and Queen Victoria Street, with the Lord Mayor home at Mansion House at about 2.30pm.

**For the full noise, stand between Bank and St Paul's from 11am; for room, take the Embankment after 1.15pm.** The organiser's advice is to get off at Mansion House, Cannon Street or Blackfriars rather than Bank or St Paul's. Almost the whole City is closed to traffic, and every bus route that normally crosses it turns back or goes round. There are no fireworks.

St Paul's Cathedral marks the day with free entry to the Cathedral Floor and Crypt (last admission 4pm) and a £13.50 adult ticket for the Dome Galleries. See our [St Paul's Cathedral guide](/articles/st-pauls-cathedral-guide/).

Our [City of London walk](/articles/city-of-london-walk/) fills the daylight left after the Show.

### Diwali: Sunday 8 November

Diwali itself fell on **Sunday 8 November**. The Mayor of London's free Diwali on the Square in Trafalgar Square was due on 25 October and was cancelled the day before. The free **Diwali day at the National Maritime Museum** ran on **Saturday 7 November**, with dance and music, rangoli workshops, a lantern parade and, new in 2026, the Diwali Bazaar. Our [Greenwich guide](/articles/greenwich-area-guide/) has the museum and the rest of the day.

## EFG London Jazz Festival — 13 to 22 November

Ten days across more than 80 venues, from the Royal Festival Hall to clubs in Dalston and Chelsea. The Opening Gala is at the Royal Festival Hall on 13 November, with a Coltrane 100 night at the Barbican the same evening. Nineteen concerts are free. Tickets start at £5, and most shows are sold by the venues, so there is no single on-sale date.

The organiser's own listings are at [efglondonjazzfestival.org.uk](https://efglondonjazzfestival.org.uk/). Prices, the free rooms and how to get into a sold-out show are in our [London Jazz Festival guide](/articles/london-jazz-festival/).

## Christmas arrives in stages

### The lights

**Carnaby Street's lights went on on Wednesday 4 November**, and **Regent Street's on Thursday 5th**, with the Spirit of Christmas angels back above the street. **Oxford Street's go on today, Monday 9th**, and **Bond Street and Covent Garden switch on on Thursday 12 November**, Bond Street with the street traffic-free from 4pm to 8pm. The [Christmas lights walk](/articles/christmas-lights-walk-london/) links them all in one evening, about 5km. Start at about 4.30pm.

### Rinks, markets and Winter Wonderland

**Skate Leicester Square opened on Saturday 31 October, and Glide at Battersea Power Station on Friday 6 November.** Somerset House follows on **Wednesday 11th**, with adults at £13 to £28.50 and children at £11 to £15, plus £1.50 a ticket. The other rinks open from the 18th. [Our ice skating guide](/articles/ice-skating-london/) has every rink and what it costs.

**Hyde Park Winter Wonderland opens on Thursday 19 November and runs to 3 January 2027.** Entry is **£1 at off-peak sessions, Monday to Thursday through November**, with no booking fees, and everything inside is charged separately. Our [Winter Wonderland guide](/articles/hyde-park-winter-wonderland/) has the gates and prices. **Chelsea Winter Village & Illuminations**, a light trail at the Royal Hospital Chelsea, runs from **25 November to 28 December**. **Christmas at Kew** is on from 13 November. **King's Cross Winter Market**, new in 2026 and free to enter, fills Granary Square from **26 November to 3 January 2027**, with a bookable roller rink beside it.

The chalet markets are in the [Christmas markets guide](/articles/christmas-markets-london/). Two ticketed shopping fairs are still to come: **Good Housekeeping Live with the Country Living Christmas Market** at the Business Design Centre (11 to 14 November) and the **Ideal Christmas Show** at Olympia (26 to 29 November).

### For children

**Wundrful World of Christmas** runs on selected days from **13 November to 24 December** at Immerse LDN on ExCeL's waterfront, a short walk from Custom House. It is a timed walk-through of about 50 minutes for children aged 3 to 8, ending with Santa. Every child needs a ticket, babies included. More shows are in our [Christmas shows guide](/articles/christmas-shows-london/), and the full picture is in [Christmas in London](/articles/christmas-in-london/).

## New in November: the London Museum at Smithfield

**The Museum of London's new home, now called the London Museum, opens its permanent galleries on Saturday 28 November in Smithfield's Victorian General Market, EC1A 9AG, and they are free.** Three spaces run from the present to the Roman city. Real Time tells the story of present-day London from the city's data. Our Time has 13 large installations, including the Lord Mayor's Coach and Syd's Coffee Stall. Past Time is set at Roman street level. Objects include the Cheapside Hoard of Elizabethan and Jacobean jewellery, Roman writing tablets and Paul Simonon's smashed bass guitar.

**A six-metre window in the underground galleries looks onto the live Thameslink line.** The first spotlight exhibition, **London Tastes**, led by Ruby Tandoh and Jonathan Nunn, runs from November to August 2027. The museum's temporary exhibition space, in the Poultry Market next door, opens in 2028.

**Free tickets for 28 November to 31 December are on the [museum's booking page](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/whats-on/smithfield-general-admission/).** Walk-ups are welcome, but the museum recommends booking during the opening weeks. It is open 10am to 6pm Sunday to Thursday, with last entry at 4.45pm, and 10am to 11pm on Fridays and Saturdays, with the last ticketed entry at 7.15pm. **On the opening weekend the Elizabeth line, Circle, Hammersmith & City and Metropolitan lines are all closed**, which are the lines at Farringdon and Barbican. St Paul's, on the Central line, is not affected.

## Exhibitions

### Open since 30 October

- **Agatha Christie: A World of Mystery**, British Library, to **20 June 2027**. Rooms styled as a country house, a WWI dispensary, an Orient Express-style carriage, an archaeological dig and a West End theatre, with Christie's 1937 Remington typewriter and her early manuscripts. £25 at peak (weekends, bank holidays and the final month) and £20 off-peak until 21 February; [book on the British Library's site](https://events.bl.uk/exhibitions/agathachristie).
- **Kate's Knitalian**, Air Contemporary, Ham Yard, Soho, to **5 December**. Kate Jenkins has turned the gallery into an Italian restaurant and delicatessen in which everything is knitted or crocheted: spaghetti, ravioli, tins of sardines, antipasti, cannoli and a build-your-own pizza station. More than 100 new pieces, all for sale. Free, with no booking. Open Tuesday to Friday 10am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 5pm, **closed Sunday and Monday**. The gallery is in the courtyard of the Ham Yard Hotel, reached from Great Windmill Street or Denman Street, a few minutes from Piccadilly Circus. [Air Contemporary's exhibition page](https://www.air-contemporary.com/exhibitions/64-kate-jenkins-kate-s-knitalian/).

### Opening in November

- **Winston Taylor Photo Portrait Prize 2026**, National Portrait Gallery, open since **5 November** to 17 January 2027. £10 Monday to Thursday and £12 Friday to Sunday.
- **Vanessa Bell & Duncan Grant: Inside Bloomsbury**, Tate Britain, **12 November** to 11 April 2027. More than 300 works, including the artists' studio moved from Charleston, their Sussex home. £20, free for Tate Members, £5 for 16 to 25s.

Still running through the month: **Korea** at the British Museum and **Renoir and Love** at the National Gallery, both to 31 January 2027, and **Frida: The Making of an Icon** at Tate Modern to 3 January. ## Practical: November in London

**Weather.** The Met Office's Heathrow average for the month (1991 to 2020) is a daily high of **11.5C** and a low of **5.3C**, with **66.6mm of rain**, the most of any month there, and 68 hours of sunshine. Our [London in the rain guide](/articles/london-in-the-rain/) has the indoor fallbacks.

**Daylight.** Sunset is **4.35pm on 1 November**, **4.14pm on the 14th** and **3.57pm on the 30th**, with sunrise moving from 6.52am to 7.40am. Parks, viewpoints and river walks belong before 3pm by the end of the month.

**Crowds.** There is no school holiday or bank holiday, so museums are quieter than in October's half term. The busy days are Saturday 14th and from the Winter Wonderland opening on the 19th.
