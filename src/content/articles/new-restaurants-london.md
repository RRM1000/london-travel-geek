**November 2026.** London's new restaurants, bars and cafés from mid-October to the end of November 2026, the openings dated for December and January, and the notable closures. Each entry says what to order and whether you can book.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **[Ralphie's](#ralphies-belgravia)**, the former Dorchester Grill chef Tom Booton's own bistro, opens in Belgravia on 24 November 2026. **[Lenny's](#lennys-covent-garden)** serves New Haven-style charred pizza in Covent Garden. **[Dishoom Borough](#dishoom-borough-london-bridge)** opened at the Hop Exchange on 21 October and is mostly walk-in. **[Wright Brothers](#wright-brothers-covent-garden)** opens its oyster restaurant in Covent Garden on 28 November, and **[Din Tai Fung](#din-tai-fung-kings-cross)** brings its soup dumplings to King's Cross on 1 December. **[Café TPT](#café-tpt-chinatown)** in Chinatown closed in October after 23 years.

*Opening dates as announced by the restaurants and the trade press up to 24 November 2026. The page is refreshed at the end of each month.*

## Opened in October and November 2026

Newest first.

### Ralphie's, Belgravia

*Belgravia · 87 Pimlico Road, SW1W 8PH · Sloane Square · opened 24 November 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/reservations/ralphies)*

Tom Booton ran the kitchen at The Dorchester's Grill; this is his first restaurant of his own, a British bistro named after his dog, on the old Enoteca Turi site. [Hot Dinners reports](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026101515452/Gastroblog/Latest-news/tom-booton-ralphies-restaurant-belgravia-london) crispy cod cheek scampi with warm tartare sauce, a pork and mushroom pie and a vanilla soft-serve sandwich with hazelnut and fudge sauce, and a Sunday leg of lamb served with a Yorkshire pudding stuffed with shepherd's pie. Pritesh Mody wrote the cocktails, among them a freezer-cold beetroot Martini. Dogs get their own menu. Ralphie's takes bookings online.

### Schofield's, Covent Garden

*Covent Garden · 1A Earlham Street, WC2H 9LL · Leicester Square · opened 23 November 2026 · [website](https://www.schofieldsbar.com/)*

Brothers Joe and Daniel Schofield's Manchester bar has topped the UK's Top 50 Cocktail Bars list three times, and this is its London sibling, mostly below ground in the old Chick 'n' Sours site. The Manchester list is the guide to what is poured: their own versions of classic cocktails, from a Penicillin and a Hawaiian Stone Sour to the Negroni and the Manhattan, with cheese and charcuterie as the food ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026082815329/Gastroblog/Latest-news/schofields-bar-opening-london-covent-garden)). Schofield's lists its bars as closed from 23 to 26 December and from 1 to 7 January.

### Braai Bird, Nunhead

*Nunhead · 57 Nunhead Lane, SE15 3TR · Nunhead or Peckham Rye · opened 21 November 2026 · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/braaibird/)*

Patrick Williams and Amy Corbin moved Kudu to Marylebone and have reopened their old Kudu Grill site as a rotisserie chicken restaurant. The birds are roasted over wood and come with South African sauces, Monkey Gland and Cape Malay among them, plus chips, big sharing plates and South African wine ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026092115380/Gastroblog/Latest-news/braai-bird-kudu-rotisserie-chicken-nunhead-south-london-restaurant)). The room is a light update of Kudu Grill's, hung with work by South African artists. Hours and bookings are posted on its Instagram.

### Cervecería Los Primos, London Bridge

*Borough Yards · 4 Dirty Lane, SE1 9PA · London Bridge · opened 19 November 2026 · walk-in only · [website](https://www.cervecerialosprimos.co.uk/)*

The Harts Group, who run Barrafina, replaced their Parrillan at Borough Yards with a Spanish neighbourhood bar. The day's tapas sit on a steel-topped counter: tortilla, chorizo cooked in cider, fried crispy pig's ears, tuna empanadas, bomba stuffed with txistorra sausage and egg or chorizo bocadillos, with beer, wine, cider and vermouth on tap ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026093015405/Gastroblog/Latest-news/los-primos-spanish-bar-borough-yards-opening)). It takes no bookings.

### B. Penhale, Smithfield

*Smithfield · 57 West Smithfield, EC1A 9DS · Farringdon or Barbican · opened 16 November 2026 · [book a table](https://live.tebi.co/ecom/reservations/122130_c5c01a86c3f31b6e702f16991bc7f3a66679054528e97bf52dfb124577889f31)*

The team behind Camille in Borough Market cook British and Irish food here, in the old Club Gascon dining room across the road from the meat market. Chef Elliot Hashtroudi named it after his grandmother, who had links to the market. [Hot Dinners lists](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026091615369/Gastroblog/Latest-news/b-penhale-smithfield-farringdon-camille-team-restaurant) stout-battered oysters with bone marrow and horseradish, smoked jellied eel and turbot with shrimp sauce and laverbread, then a separate course of tea and biscuits served in antique teapots. The 60-seat dining room takes bookings; a small bar at the front keeps space for walk-ins, with its own chalkboard menu.

### Berryman's, Dalston

*Dalston · 508 Kingsland Road, E8 4AE · Dalston Junction · opened 13 November 2026 · walk-in only · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/berrymansbar/)*

Max and Noel Venning opened Berryman's two doors from Three Sheets, the Dalston bar they started ten years ago, and named it after their mother's maiden name. It serves Cornish oysters, a rotating list of Martinis and wine: the oysters come with garnishes such as strawberry mignonette and gherkin granita, and the bar snacks include dirty Martini crisps and a bikini, a thin pressed toastie of jamón, 'nduja, manchego and gouda ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026102115467/Gastroblog/Latest-news/berrymans-three-sheets-dalston-bar-london)). It holds 20 people, all standing, and takes no bookings.

### Dusty Knuckle, Blackstock Road

*Highbury · 81 Blackstock Road, N4 2JW · Arsenal or Finsbury Park · opened 9 November 2026 · [website](https://www.thedustyknuckle.com/)*

The Dusty Knuckle is a social enterprise bakery whose potato sourdough has a following across north London, and its Blackstock Road shop is a café as well as a bakery ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026060115325/Gastroblog/Latest-news/dusty-knuckle-finsbury-park-blackstock-road)). It sells bread, pastries, coffee, slices, toasties and soup, with breakfast sandwiches at weekends. It is open from 8am to 4.30pm every day, so come before the afternoon.

### Bar Douro, Soho

*Soho · 10 Greek Street, W1D 4DH · Tottenham Court Road · opened 4 November 2026 · [book a table](https://bardouro.co.uk/book-a-table-soho)*

Bar Douro's third restaurant, after London Bridge and the City, took over the address that was the restaurant 10 Greek Street. The Soho branch specialises in vaca velha, beef from cattle at least six years old, from northern Portuguese breeds including Maronesa, Minhota and Mirandesa. [Hot Dinners reports](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026080415270/Gastroblog/Latest-news/bar-douro-soho-opening-greek-street) the group's regular dishes alongside it, croquetes de alheira and bacalhau à brás among them, with a wine list drawn from all over Portugal. The Maronesa beef is not always on; when it is, it comes as a vaca velha ribeye.

### Lunette, Temple

*Temple · Level 11, St Clement hotel, WC2R 2NF · Temple · opened 3 November 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/lunette/reservations/create/search/)*

Florence Knight, who cooked at Polpetto and Sessions Arts Club, runs the rooftop restaurant of Nick Jones's St Clement hotel. It serves set menus only: four courses at lunch and eight in the evening ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026060115108/Gastroblog/Latest-news/lunette-florence-knight-restaurant-london-180-thames-rooftop)). Dishes at a preview included a pomme soufflé puff filled with smoked eel, and milk custard with onion sauce, caviar and baked petals. The room sits on the eleventh floor of a Brutalist building, with hand-blown glassware and stitched leather chairs. There is no à la carte, and tables are booked online.

### Lenny's, Covent Garden

*Covent Garden · 24 Henrietta Street, WC2E 8ND · Covent Garden · opened 27 October 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/lennys/reservations/create/search)*

Max Lewis ran Lenny's Apizza as a residency in the Bedford Tavern in Finsbury Park until earlier in 2026, and now has a permanent pizzeria with MJMK, the group behind Casa do Frango. New Haven pizza, or "apizza" (say "ah-beetz"), comes from Connecticut: it stays in the oven longer for a charred, chewy crust, with more tomato and less cheese than most pies. Order the tomato pie, or The Lenny, half tomato, garlic and pecorino and half mozzarella ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026072815255/Gastroblog/Latest-news/lennys-apizza-permanent-covent-garden-pizzeria-restaurant)). It opens at noon daily and stays open to 11.30pm Tuesday to Saturday. For more pizza, see [the best pizza in London](/articles/best-pizza-london/).

### The Talli Queen, Shepherd's Bush

*Shepherd's Bush · 412 Uxbridge Road, W12 0NR · Shepherd's Bush Market, then 15 minutes' walk west · opened 27 October 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/thetalliqueen/reservations/create/search/)*

Chef Avi Shashidhara, who launched the kitchen at Pahli Hill Bandra Bhai, has turned the old Queen Adelaide into an Indian pub modelled on Kerala's toddy shops, Bangalore's neighbourhood bars and southern India's military canteens. [Hot Dinners lists](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026042014991/Gastroblog/Latest-news/talli-queen-indian-pub-uxbridge-road-shepherds-bush-avi-shashidhara) toddy shop clams, military canteen-style meatballs, Kerala chilli beef with coconut and curry leaves, dosas and kebabs with kulcha flatbread, with 18 beers on draught. The refit kept the pub's main features. On Sundays the menu adds slow-cooked lamb shoulder and biryani.

### Dishoom Borough, London Bridge

*Borough · The Hop Exchange, SE1 9AB · London Bridge · opened 21 October 2026 · [Dishoom Borough](https://www.dishoom.com/borough/)*

London's eighth Dishoom fills two floors of the West Wing of the Hop Exchange, with a dining veranda at the back ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2025102714595/Gastroblog/Latest-news/dishoom-borough-market-london-bridge-plans)). The menu is the Bombay café one: a bacon naan roll at breakfast, house black daal, chicken ruby and biryani at dinner. Dishoom is mostly walk-in: anyone can book during the day, but after 6pm only groups of six or more can, and in the evening and at weekends the queue runs 30 minutes to an hour, with free chai while you wait.

### Bubala, Covent Garden

*Covent Garden · 14 Garrick Street, WC2E 9BJ · Leicester Square or Covent Garden · opened 19 October 2026 · [website](https://www.bubala.co.uk/)*

Bubala's fourth restaurant, and its largest, at about 90 seats with 30 more outside, cooks the same vegetarian Middle Eastern food as the Spitalfields original ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026052715092/Gastroblog/Latest-news/bubala-covent-garden-vegetarian-restaurant-opening)). Order the confit potato latkes with toum, a whipped garlic sauce, and the halloumi with chamomile and fennel honey, with bread and hummus with burnt butter. It takes bookings through its site. More meat-free places are in [the best vegetarian and vegan restaurants](/articles/best-vegetarian-vegan-restaurants-london/).

## Opening in late November and December 2026

### Osteria Panzano, Belgravia

*Knightsbridge · 10–12 William Street, SW1X 9HL · Knightsbridge · opens 27 November 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/osteriapanzano/reservations/create/search/)*

Arjun Waney, the restaurateur behind Zuma, La Petite Maison and COYA, opens his first Italian restaurant in the old Noura site, cooking Tuscan dishes with some produce from his own Tuscan estate. The opening menu includes cacio e pepe with black truffle, yellowfin tuna tartare and roast turbot, with whole fish and T-bone steaks from the grill ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026102115462/Gastroblog/Latest-news/osteria-manzano-arjun-waney-tuscan-italian-restaurant-belgravia)). A cheese and charcuterie shop sits alongside the restaurant. Bookings are open.

### Wright Brothers, Covent Garden

*Covent Garden · 45 Great Queen Street, WC2B 5AA · Holborn or Covent Garden · opens 28 November 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/wrightbrotherscoventgarden/reservations/create/search/)*

The oyster merchants who started in Borough Market 20 years ago take over the two-storey former Margot, and have closed their South Kensington restaurant after eleven years. Kevin Gratton, who worked with Mark Hix for years, writes the menu: butter-grilled oysters with black winter truffle, ink-braised cuttlefish with sea aster, fruits de mer platters and a new oyster gelato with olive oil and caviar ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026110415503/Gastroblog/Latest-news/wright-brothers-opening-in-covent-garden)). For oysters and a glass without a full meal, sit at the scallop-tiled counter bar at the back.

Hot Dinners Club members get 50% off food and a welcome drink at dinner on 30 November and 1 December 2026, through [Hot Dinners' soft-launch offer](https://www.hot-dinners.com/Offers/Hot-Dinners-Restaurant-Offers/wright-brothers-covent-garden-restaurant-offer); the club is a paid membership. {until=2026-12-01}

### London Museum's restaurants, Smithfield

*Smithfield · West Smithfield, EC1A 9AG · Farringdon · opens 28 November 2026 · [London Museum](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/smithfield/)*

The London Museum opens in Smithfield's old General Market on 28 November, and its food comes from chef Robin Gill and the bartender Ryan Chetiyawardana, known as Mr Lyan. Clerks House, the 140-seat all-day restaurant, serves Gill's Billingsgate sandwich of hand-dived scallop, crispy smoked bacon and scallop roe butter, and pies made with meat from the caterer's own farms ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026072115239/Gastroblog/Latest-news/london-museum-smithfield-reveal-bar-restaurant-robin-gill-mr-lyan)). The Temperance Cocoa Rooms, an alcohol-free café, serves hot chocolate, breakfast and a tamarind and date Chelsea bun, and stays open 24 hours from Thursday to Sunday. Museum entry is free, but book a timed ticket for the first weeks.

### The Poet, Smithfield

*Smithfield · House 5, London Museum, General Market, Farringdon Street, EC4A 4AZ · Farringdon · opens 30 November 2026 · [website](https://www.thepoetsmithfield.com/)*

The team behind Sune in Hackney (Spencer Large, Honey Spencer and Charlie Sims) open a corner pub in the museum's buildings, with Charlie Tayler, formerly of Aulis, cooking. The ground floor is a pub with a Scampi Fries Scotch egg; the first-floor dining room serves lamb shank suet pudding, côte de boeuf with hash brown chips and gentleman's relish, and whole Dover sole meunière ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026091815375/Gastroblog/Latest-news/the-poet-pub-sune-team-smithfield-london-museum)). A "vertical chicken" menu runs one bird across several courses, from a pretzel with chicken fat butter to a pudding made with chicken fat. Envoi, the ten-seat chef's table, and Shambles, the attic cocktail bar, follow in 2027.

### Din Tai Fung, King's Cross

*King's Cross · 1 Lewis Cubitt Walk, N1C 4DL · King's Cross St Pancras · opens 1 December 2026 · [website](https://dintaifung-uk.com/)*

The Taiwanese dumpling chain, which already has restaurants at Selfridges and Centre Point, seats 180 in the old Babel Beerhouse corner opposite Lightroom, with a terrace. Xiao long bao, soup dumplings filled with crab, pork, chicken or beef, are folded in a small kitchen in front of the diners, and the menu runs on to Shanghainese drunken chicken, noodles and salted egg yolk custard lava buns ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026090215338/Gastroblog/Latest-news/din-tai-fung-kings-cross-opening-london)). For more dumplings, see [the best dim sum in London](/articles/best-dim-sum-london/).

### Homer Lobster, Soho

*Soho · 5 D'Arblay Street, W1F 8DL · Oxford Circus or Tottenham Court Road · opens 1 December 2026 · [website](https://homerfoodgroup.com/homer-lobster)*

Homer Lobster started in Paris and ran a two-week pop-up on the South Bank in 2025 before taking this Soho shop, next door to Crème. The order is the lobster and crayfish roll; the other sandwiches include a crab roll, a tuna melt made with belly tuna, salmon pastrami and a lobster grilled cheese, and any of them can be topped with caviar ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026062615156/Gastroblog/Latest-news/homer-lobster-opening-london-soho)). More are in [the best sandwiches in London](/articles/best-sandwiches-london/).

### Caravan, Southbank Centre

*South Bank · Festival Terrace, Southbank Centre, Belvedere Road, SE1 8XX · Waterloo or Embankment · opens 3 December 2026 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/caravansouthbank/reservations/create/search/)*

Caravan's eighth London restaurant, in the old Ping Pong space, is its largest, with several dining rooms, two rebuilt railway arches, a cocktail bar, a listening lounge and a terrace ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026082415315/Gastroblog/Latest-news/caravan-opening-southbank-centre)). The all-day menu is the group's familiar one of small plates, pizzas and bowls, with larger dishes such as a Dingley Dell pork schnitzel with fried egg and a chargrilled lamb Barnsley chop. It is a short walk from the Royal Festival Hall and the National Theatre, and bookings are open from 3 December.

### Liman, Palmers Green

*Palmers Green · 91 Green Lanes, N13 4TD · Palmers Green or Bowes Park · opens 3 December 2026 · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/limanlondon/)*

Hasan Semay, the YouTuber and BBC judge known as Big Has, opens a Turkish Cypriot grill and fry shop with his cousin and uncle on the stretch of Green Lanes where he grew up. A coal-fired mangal and Cypriot-style döner are at the centre of the menu, with kebabs, whole grilled sea bass, sheftalia, the caul-wrapped Cypriot sausage, and Cypriot chips; he aims for a meal under £25 a head ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026111315524/Gastroblog/Latest-news/big-has-restaurant-green-lanes-london)). Liman sells no alcohol, so bring your own.

### Santo Cielo, Holborn

*Holborn · Unit 2, Sicilian Avenue, WC1A 2QD · Holborn · opens Tuesday 8 December 2026 · [website](https://santocielo.co.uk/)*

The Exmouth Market sandwich shop opens a second counter, with the bread baked behind it. The sandwiches are made on schiacciata, a flat Tuscan bread softer than focaccia: the Santo Special has prosciutto crudo, burrata, basil pesto, truffle honey and pecorino, and the Pronto Pistachio mortadella, stracciatella and pistachio pesto ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026111815534/Gastroblog/Latest-news/santo-cielo-italian-sandwiches-holborn-london-opening)). Hot Dinners expects queues on opening day; joining the waitlist on Santo Cielo's site gets a welcome gift that day.

### The Hero's Return, Battersea Power Station

*Battersea · Unit 29, Circus West, Battersea Power Station, SW8 4NN · Battersea Power Station · opens 16 December 2026 · [website](https://www.theherosreturn.pub/)*

The group behind the pasta restaurant Bancone opens a riverside pub in the old No 29 bar, next to Wright Brothers. The executive chef is Jonathan Woolway, chef-patron of The Shed in Swansea after 17 years at St John, and the menu promised includes ox heart with hand-cut chips and peppercorn sauce, and cold roast Gower lamb with celeriac remoulade and pickled walnut ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026072015236/Gastroblog/Latest-news/hero-s-return-pub-battersea-power-station)). The terrace on the river seats 40.

## Opening in January 2027

### Lucca, Chelsea

*Chelsea · 27 Cale Street, SW3 3QP · South Kensington or Sloane Square · opens 15 January 2027 · [book a table](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/luccalondon/reservations/create/search/)*

Lucca has run in Istanbul since 2004, and its London branch takes the Chelsea site Beefbar left for Soho. The Istanbul menu runs from pasta and pizzettas to poke bowls, with lemon sea bass and mint risotto as a signature, though [Hot Dinners notes](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026110915512/Gastroblog/Latest-news/lucca-restaurant-mediterranean-chelsea-london) the London menu has not been published. Bookings are open from 15 January.

### Kong, Spitalfields

*Spitalfields · 9–11 Folgate Street, E1 6BX · Liverpool Street or Shoreditch High Street · opens January 2027 · [website](https://www.kongiskong.com/)*

Andrew Wong, whose A. Wong in Pimlico holds two Michelin stars, and Dishoom co-founder Amar Radia call Kong a "Chinese public house": part pub, part all-day Chinese kitchen, on the site that was The Water Poet. The menu is unpublished; they promise "dumplings at dawn, noodles at lunch, grills with a cold pint at dusk" ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026060315112/Gastroblog/Latest-news/kong-andrew-wong-chinese-pub-restaurant-shoreditch-london)). Hot Dinners lists the opening for January 2027.

### Mirrors at the ICA, St James's

*St James's · The ICA, The Mall, SW1Y 5AH · Charing Cross or Piccadilly Circus · opens January 2027 · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mirrorslondon/)*

Jimmy Stephenson, who owns the wine bar Hector's, opens a café by day and restaurant and wine bar by night inside the Institute of Contemporary Arts, with Emily Masters (formerly of Hill & Szrok and The Eagle) and Cavan Power O'Keeffe (formerly senior sous chef at Planque) cooking. The evening menu includes tuna fricassée, prawn cappelletti and a condensed milk and almond crêpe; the day menu has salads, sandwiches and baking ([Hot Dinners](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026111115521/Gastroblog/Latest-news/mirrors-ica-restaurant-bar-opening-mall-london)). The wine list leans natural, and Hector's Negroni is on the cocktail list.

### Adelia, Clapham

*Clapham · 40 Abbeville Road, SW4 9NG · Clapham South or Clapham Common · opens January 2027 · [website](https://adeliarestaurant.com/)*

Thomas Texido, formerly in operations at Big Mamma and MJMK, opens a pasta bar in the old Bistro Union space, with pasta shipped from Gragnano. [Hot Dinners lists](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026111015515/Gastroblog/Latest-news/adelia-fresh-pasta-restaurant-abbeville-clapham) spaghetti al limone with butter, Parmigiano and basil, ziti alla genovese with slow-cooked beef shin and wild boar pappardelle, with meat from The Ginger Pig nearby. It is meant as a drop-in neighbourhood place, with counter seats for a quick bowl. Its site has a mailing list that announces when bookings open.

## Closed and closing

### Café TPT, Chinatown

Café TPT, the Cantonese café at 21 Wardour Street known for its late hours, closed in October 2026 after 23 years ([Restaurant Online](https://www.restaurantonline.co.uk/Article/2026/10/05/chinatown-restaurant-cafe-tpt-to-close/)). [The best food in Chinatown](/articles/best-food-chinatown-london/) has the places nearby that are still trading.

### Les 110 de Taillevent, Marylebone

The wine-led French restaurant, opened in 2015 as a sister to Le Taillevent in Paris, has closed; liquidators were appointed to its parent company on 9 October 2026 ([The Caterer](https://www.thecaterer.com/news/marylebone-restaurant-les-110-de-taillevent-closes-after-more-than-a-decade)).

### Big Kid, Soho

Big Kid's ice cream hatch on Kingly Street, known for flavours such as Marmite on toast, trades for the last time on 28 November 2026 after its landlord served notice, and its Whippies soft-serve shop in Seven Dials Market has already shut ([Broadsheet](https://www.broadsheet.com/london/food-and-drink/big-kid-ice-cream-closing)). See [the best ice cream in London](/articles/best-ice-cream-london/) for the rest.

### Brooklands by Claude Bosi, Belgravia

Claude Bosi's two-Michelin-star restaurant on the roof of The Peninsula closes in January 2027; Bosi returns to Michelin House in South Kensington, the former Bibendum, in spring 2027 ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/news/claude-bosi-brooklands-peninsula-closing-january-2027-110926)). Book now for a last meal.
