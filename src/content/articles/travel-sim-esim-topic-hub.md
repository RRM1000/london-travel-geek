## A simple decision

1. Check whether your home plan already includes the destination.
2. Confirm that the exact phone model is unlocked and supports eSIM.
3. Decide whether you need a local number or data only.
4. Estimate the data allowance.
5. Compare validity, coverage, high-speed limits, hotspot use and support.
6. Install before departure when possible, but do not activate early if validity begins immediately.
