**Bonfire Night is 5 November, and London's big displays are ticketed, sell out and cluster on the nearest weekend.** In 2026 the 5th was a Thursday and the large shows ran on Saturday 7th and Sunday 8th; only Coram's Fields, Wimbledon Park and Stow ran on the 5th itself. In 2027 the 5th is a Friday.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** The big displays are **Alexandra Palace**, **Battersea Park** and **Blackheath**, all ticketed and all sold out or close to it in recent years. The only free display is **Coram's Fields**, where adults must bring a child. For a free view, **Parliament Hill** has no gates and never closes, while **Greenwich Park shuts at 6pm**, before anything starts. Everything below shows what the 2026 displays ran and charged, which is the best guide to 2027.

## Alexandra Palace: the best-known one

**In 2026 it ran two nights, two kinds of event.**

| | Saturday 7 November 2026 | Sunday 8 November 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gates** | 16:00 | 16:00 |
| **Last entry** | 20:05 | 18:05 |
| **Display** | **8pm** | **6pm** |
| **The night** | Party night — headline DJ set from Eats Everything, and a German Bier Festival (18+, extra ticket) with a Queen tribute | **Family day, new for 2026** — smaller capacity, Barrioke with Shaun Williamson, a Junior Jungle kids' rave |

Also on site across the weekend: a bonfire, funfair, **ice skating** on the permanent indoor rink, street food and bars.

![The Alexandra Palace bonfire fully ablaze, with a large crowd silhouetted against the flames](../../assets/articles/bonfire-night-london/alexandra-palace-bonfire.jpg)

The event is called the Fireworks and Drone Festival. In 2026 hundreds of choreographed drones, flown by Celestial, runners-up on Britain's Got Talent 2026, flew formations over the park before the fireworks took over.

![A drone display forming a lit figure in the night sky above Alexandra Park, watched by a crowd](../../assets/articles/bonfire-night-london/alexandra-palace-drone-show.jpg)

> ⚠️ **It sells out.** In 2026 both nights were sold through [DICE](https://link.dice.fm/lb38fc167352), the ticket link on the festival's own site, and through Universe and Fever; the festival named those three as its only official partners.

**Alexandra Palace's own page said tickets started from £11.50, and the under-10 ticket was £6.50 including fees.** Universe listed tickets from £6.05 to £18.70 a night, fees included.

- Adult tickets are 16+. Children aged 11–15 and 0–10 each need their own ticket, and must be with an adult aged 21 or over.
- The site is cashless.
- No food or drink may be brought in, except for young children.
- The German Bier Festival (Saturday, 18+), ice skating, the Summit rooftop and the Sunday premium viewing area were add-on tickets, and none could be bought on its own.

![A band playing on a stage inside a marquee lit blue, with a crowd watching](../../assets/articles/bonfire-night-london/alexandra-palace-live-music.jpg)

The event **moved off Halloween** in 2026 (2025 ran on 31 October and 1 November with ghost tours and fancy dress), and Sunday was a family day, not a second party night.

**Getting there:** Alexandra Palace station, on Great Northern trains from Moorgate or King's Cross, is at the Wood Green entrance to the park. Wood Green on the Piccadilly line is a 20-minute walk. The W3 bus was diverted and did not stop at the Palace, and there is no public parking.

## Battersea Park: the other large paid display

**Battersea Park is the other large paid display in London**, and it sells out in much the same way. [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/things-to-do/bonfire-night-fireworks-displays-in-london) puts it at 50,000 ticketed spectators a night.

It is run by Wandsworth Council. In 2026 it ran on **Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November**. On the Saturday gates opened at 6pm, a Rudimental DJ set started at 7.30pm, last entry was 8pm, the fireworks started at about 8.30pm and the show ended at 11pm, with a Disco Ceilidh party after the display. On the Sunday gates opened at 4.30pm, the Lords of Lightning electrical show was at 5.45pm, last entry was 6.30pm, the fireworks started at about 7pm and the show ended at 9.30pm.

**General admission (16+) was £16.70, rising to £18.10 for the final release; youth tickets (5–15) were £11.50, and under-5s went free but still needed a ticket.** Tickets were advance only, sold through Kaboodle from the [Battersea Park Fireworks site](https://www.batterseaparkfireworks.com/), with no gate sales, no resale and no waiting list for sold-out dates. Bars and food stalls took cards only.

### Watching Battersea from the river instead

[London Party Boats](https://www.londonpartyboats.co.uk/) ran a three-hour cruise in 2026 that sailed up to Battersea and held position for the display, which is a way in if the park sells out.

## The one free display

**[Coram's Fields](https://coramsfields.org/)**, Guilford Street, Bloomsbury WC1N 1DN. In 2026 it ran on **Thursday 5 November**: gates 15:30, performances from 16:15, **display at 18:00**, closing 19:00. Free, no ticket, first come first served until the park was full.

> ⚠️ **Adults may only attend accompanied by a child.** Coram's Fields is a children's park, and that rule applies all year round. A paid Fast-Track ticket, booked in advance on Eventbrite, guaranteed entry and skipped the queue, but gave no better view and covered one adult and one child. Accessible-viewing tickets were free but had to be booked in advance on Eventbrite.

**No London borough council still funds a free public display.** The BBC asked all 32 under freedom of information and found **17 council events cancelled over cost in five years**, ten of them in Tower Hamlets alone. The displays that survive have migrated to cricket clubs, rugby clubs, schools, Scout groups and Rotary — and they charge.

## Chiswick Rugby Club: a club display for charity

**[Chiswick Rugby Club's Community Fireworks](https://www.chiswickrugby.co.uk/news/chiswick-rugby-community-fireworks-3000088.html)** is a club-run night at its ground on Dan Mason Drive, W4. In 2026 it ran on **Saturday 7 November**: doors at 5.30pm, the bonfire lit at 6.30pm, the fireworks at 7.30pm and live music from 8pm, with bars, street food and face painting.

![Chiswick Rugby's poster for its Community Fireworks Spectacular on Saturday 7 November 2026, with fireworks over the floodlit pitch and the night's times and ticket prices](../../assets/articles/bonfire-night-london/chiswick-rugby-fireworks-poster.jpg)

**It raises money for Maggie's, the cancer support charity:** £2 of every ticket, and £3 of a family ticket, goes to it. Adults were **£12**, children **£8**, and a family ticket was **£30**.

Tickets were sold through the [club's online shop](https://www.chiswickrugby.co.uk/payments/chiswick-rugbys-community-fireworks-spectacular-159271.html), which asked you to register before you could buy. There was no physical ticket; you gave the booking name at the gate.

## Watching for free, without a ticket

Some of the best-known viewpoints are **locked before the fireworks start**. Displays are at 7pm or 8pm, and several of the famous hills shut their gates at 4.30pm or 5pm.

| Viewpoint | November gates | Any good? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parliament Hill**, Hampstead Heath | **No perimeter gates to close** | ✅ **The pick** |
| **Alexandra Park** | **Open 24 hours, all year** | ✅ |
| **Primrose Hill** | Closes **10pm**, not dusk | ✅ |
| **Blackheath** | Unenclosed common, but **fenced on the night of the ticketed event** | ❌ On the night |
| **Hyde Park** and **St James's Park** | 05:00–**midnight**, all year | ✅ |
| Richmond Park | Pedestrian gates lock **20:00** during the deer cull | ⚠️ Tight |
| Greenwich Park | **18:00** | ❌ |
| The Regent's Park | **16:30** | ❌ |
| Crystal Palace Park | About **17:00** | ❌ |
| Telegraph Hill | **16:30** | ❌ |
| Nunhead Cemetery | **16:00** | ❌ |
| Horniman Gardens | Sunset | ❌ |
| Severndroog Castle | Viewing platform **Sundays, daytime only** | ❌ |

**Parliament Hill is the pick.** At 98 metres it has legally protected sightlines to both St Paul's and the Palace of Westminster, and the Heath has no perimeter gates to lock. Nearest stations are Gospel Oak and Hampstead Heath. Two drawbacks: the Heath is unlit, so bring a torch for the walk up, and the Westminster view is partly obscured by intervening construction.

**Alexandra Park** is open around the clock while the display happens inside it. Some of the park is fenced off for the ticketed arena, so you will not get the paying guests' view, but the fireworks go up over the whole hill.

**Primrose Hill** was locked from **10pm to 6am every night from 30 October to 9 November** in 2026, well after any display, and the Royal Parks ban fireworks and lanterns there. It is separately gated from The Regent's Park, so approach from the Chalk Farm side — the main park shuts at 16:30.

**Greenwich Park** shuts its gates at **6pm** in November, so the General Wolfe viewpoint is out.

**Richmond Park:** during the deer cull from 1 November, pedestrian gates lock at **20:00** — except on Friday and Saturday nights, when the park stays open. Car parks close at 16:30 regardless. And King Henry's Mound, the famous viewpoint, sits inside Pembroke Lodge Gardens, which are separately enclosed with no published closing time. It is not a fireworks viewpoint.

**Rooftops mostly close too early.** [Sky Garden](/articles/sky-garden-guide/) shuts its terrace at 18:00 on weekdays, Horizon 22 at 18:00, and The Garden at 120 at 18:30. The exception is **Tate Modern's Level 10 viewing floor**, free and open until 21:00 on Friday and Saturday, which makes it a real option on a Friday or Saturday night, though not on a Thursday.

>

## Practicalities

**Dress for standing still in the cold.** London's November average is about **11°C by day and 5°C overnight**, and November is one of the wetter months. No café is open on Parliament Hill at 8pm: bring a hat, gloves and a flask.

**Fireworks are banned in the Royal Parks and on Hampstead Heath**, and the rules are enforced by the police. Setting off your own is legal elsewhere until **midnight on Bonfire Night**, and 11pm on other nights.
