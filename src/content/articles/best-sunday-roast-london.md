The Sunday roast is the one meal where a London pub still beats a London restaurant. The best versions are not in the centre — they are in Stockwell, Camberwell, Highgate and Barnes, in rooms that spend the other six days of the week as ordinary local boozers.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** Go to **The Devonshire** if you want the one everyone is talking about and will queue for it; **The Camberwell Arms** or **Canton Arms** for the best value; **The Harwood Arms** for the only Michelin-starred pub roast in London; **Blacklock or Hawksmoor** for a large group; and **The Waterman's Arms** if the setting matters as much as the plate.

> 📘 **How We Selected These (Editorial Note)**
> These are cross-referenced across independent published guides — [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/food-and-drink/londons-best-sunday-lunches), [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/london/perfect-for/sunday-roast), [British Food Crew](https://britishfoodcrew.com/best-london-gastropubs-14-top-spots/), [Eater London](https://london.eater.com/maps/best-british-restaurants-london) and the [Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs](https://www.hot-dinners.com/2026012714793/Gastroblog/Latest-news/uk-top-50-gastrpoubs-announced-london-devonshire). Every entry below was named by at least two of them. We do not rank by customer star averages, which cluster too tightly to mean anything, and nothing here is a paid placement. Prices and serving times change — check the pub before travelling.

## The best Sunday roasts are not in central London

Nine of the twelve sit outside Zone 1, and the two central entries that do make it are both chains with multiple sites. If you are staying in the middle of town, a good Sunday roast means getting on a train.

| Where | Zone | Pubs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **North** | 1–2 to 3 | The Golden Tooth (Islington) · The Parakeet (Kentish Town) · The Red Lion & Sun and The Bull (both Highgate) |
| **South** | 2 | The Camberwell Arms · Canton Arms (Stockwell) |
| **West** | 2–3 | The Harwood Arms (Fulham) · The Waterman's Arms (Barnes) |
| **East** | 2 | The Marksman (Hackney Road) |
| **Central** | 1 | The Devonshire (Soho) · Blacklock · Hawksmoor |

Highgate is the only neighbourhood with two entries, which makes it the single best place to head if your first choice is full.

## Ten are pubs. Two are not.

Worth knowing before you turn up: **Blacklock and Hawksmoor are restaurants**, not pubs. You cannot arrive for a pint and decide to eat, and there is no bar to wait in. Everything else on this list is a working local that happens to cook well.

That also changes your odds of a table. Blacklock runs **five London sites** and Hawksmoor **seven** — so "fully booked" means trying another branch. The other ten are single pubs, where full is simply full.

## Pick by who you are going with

Sunday lunch is rarely a solo meal, and these divide more usefully by group than by ranking:

- **Big groups (six or more)** — The Devonshire, The Camberwell Arms, Blacklock. Blacklock's "All In" is priced per head and designed for exactly this.
- **Families with children** — The Camberwell Arms, Canton Arms, The Red Lion & Sun, The Bull, The Waterman's Arms. All proper pubs with room to move.
- **Two people** — The Marksman, The Parakeet, The Harwood Arms, The Golden Tooth. Smaller rooms, and the cooking rewards paying attention to it.
- **Anything with colleagues** — Hawksmoor is the only one on this list set up for it.

## How far ahead to book

> ⚠️ **Booking data on this page is incomplete.** We hold confirmed booking lead times for three of the twelve. The rest is inference from ranking and format, and we have flagged which is which rather than guessing. Always confirm with the pub.

**Book several weeks ahead:** The Devonshire, The Harwood Arms, Hawksmoor. These are the three with confirmed long lead times, and all three have a reason — the UK's number one gastropub, the only Michelin-starred pub in London, and a steakhouse that serves the roast on Sundays only.

**Two to three weeks is usually enough** for the neighbourhood pubs — The Marksman, The Parakeet, The Golden Tooth, The Red Lion & Sun.

**Worth trying on the day:** Canton Arms holds back space for walk-ins, which almost nothing else here does. The Camberwell Arms will take walk-ins but you will wait at peak time.

**Whatever you book, go early.** Most London pubs serve roast from around noon to 5pm and many stop when the beef runs out rather than at a fixed time. A 12:30 sitting gets you the full menu; a 3pm one often does not.

## 1. The Devonshire

*££ · Soho · 1 min from Piccadilly Circus · book weeks ahead · UK Top 50 Gastropubs 2026 - #1*

Crowned the best gastropub in the UK barely two years after opening, which has made getting in the hard part. The ground floor is a proper Guinness pub and half the queue is there for the pour rather than the food; the dining room upstairs is where the roast happens. Book weeks out or accept that you are drinking first.

**Booking:** [devonshiresoho.co.uk](https://devonshiresoho.co.uk/) — book through their site

**2 minutes from Blacklock.**

## 2. The Camberwell Arms

*££ · Camberwell · 10 min from Denmark Hill · UK Top 50 Gastropubs 2026 - #9*

Ninth in the UK and the highest-placed London pub after The Devonshire, but the thing people actually mention is the size of it. Portions are generous enough that most tables over-order and regret it happily. This is the one to pick when you want the best roast per pound rather than the most famous one.

**Booking:** [thecamberwellarms.co.uk](https://www.thecamberwellarms.co.uk/) — book through their site

## 3. The Marksman

*££ · Hackney · 16 min from Bethnal Green · 3 sources: The Infatuation, Eater London, British Food Crew*

Two pubs in one building. Downstairs is a Hackney Road boozer that has not been prettified; upstairs is the dining room that won Michelin's Pub of the Year. You can have a completely different Sunday depending on which staircase you take.

**Known for:** Brown butter and honey tart

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://www.opentable.co.uk/r/the-marksman-hackney-reservations-london?lang=en-GB&ot_source=website&restref=208518)

## 4. The Parakeet

*££ · Camden Town · 1 min from Kentish Town*

A Kentish Town pub rebuilt around an open fire, and most of what comes out of the kitchen has been over it. The room did more to make its reputation than any single dish — it is the rare recent refit that made a pub feel older rather than newer.

**Book:** [Reserve a table](http://www.theparakeetpub.com/)

## 5. The Harwood Arms

*£££ · Fulham · 5 min from Fulham Broadway · book weeks ahead · 3 sources: Balance Journal, The Infatuation, British Food Crew*

London's only Michelin-starred pub, and the venison comes from the owners' own stalking rather than a supplier. That is the whole argument: the sourcing is genuinely different, not just better presented. Expect roughly double a good neighbourhood pub, and tables go weeks ahead.

**Known for:** Venison scotch egg

**Booking:** [harwoodarms.com](http://www.harwoodarms.com/) — book through their site

## 6. Canton Arms

*££ · Stockwell · 7 min from Stockwell · 3 sources: Eater London, Time Out, British Food Crew*

An old South Lambeth boozer that never rebranded itself as anything else, and serves one of the most consistently praised roasts in the city at pub prices. Three separate guides name it. It keeps some room for walk-ins, which almost nothing else on this list does.

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://cantonarms.com/bookings/)

## 7. The Golden Tooth

*££ · Islington · 9 min from Canonbury · 2 sources: Time Out, The Infatuation*

A Newington Green pub kitchen that both Time Out and The Infatuation put on their roast lists, which is a narrow overlap to land in. Small, local, and the sort of place that would be full on reputation alone if it were three miles further south.

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://www.opentable.co.uk/booking/restref/availability?rid=483555&lang=en-GB&color=1&r3uid=cfe&dark=false&correlationId=152b2f99-82d4-44e4-bf9f-dcafff4b0e22&restRef=483555)

## 8. The Red Lion & Sun

*££ · Highgate · 9 min from Highgate · UK Top 50 Gastropubs 2026 - #20*

Twentieth in the UK list, and — more usefully — the roast that Oisín Rogers of The Devonshire sends people to. A recommendation from the man running the number one is worth more than most rankings.

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://booking.resdiary.com/widget/Standard/TheRedLionandSun/15205?)

**4 minutes from The Bull.**

## 9. The Bull

*££ · Highgate · 7 min from Highgate · 2 sources: Time Out, British Food Crew*

The other Highgate option, with its own brewery attached to the pub. Worth knowing about as the alternative when the Red Lion & Sun is full, which on a Sunday it usually is.

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://www.thebullhighgate.com/book-a-table)

**4 minutes from The Red Lion & Sun.**

## 10. Blacklock

*££ · Soho · 2 min from Piccadilly Circus · 3 sources; five London sites*

Not a pub at all, but a chophouse — and its "All In" roast is a run of every cut on the menu, priced per head. Built for groups in a way almost no pub kitchen can manage, and with five London sites your odds of getting a table are far better than anywhere else here.

**Known for:** All In - a run of every chop on the menu

**2 minutes from The Devonshire.**

## 11. The Waterman's Arms

*££ · Barnes · 5 min from Barnes Bridge · 2 sources: The Infatuation, British Food Crew*

Picked out for the setting as much as the plate. It sits on the river at Barnes, so the roast comes with a Thames view and a walk to work it off afterwards — the reason to choose it over somewhere closer with a marginally better kitchen.

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://www.watermansarms.co.uk/reservations)

## 12. Hawksmoor

*££££ · Spitalfields · 4 min from Shoreditch High Street · book weeks ahead*

The expensive answer. British beef, dry-aged and grilled over charcoal, with bone-marrow gravy and Yorkshire puddings the size of a fist. Sunday is the only day the roast runs and it books out first — several weeks ahead at the central sites.

**Known for:** Sunday roast with bone-marrow gravy

**Book:** [Reserve a table](https://www.opentable.com/booking/experiences-availability?rid=11335&restref=11335&experienceId=682033)
