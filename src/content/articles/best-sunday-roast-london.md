A Sunday roast is a joint of meat with a Yorkshire pudding, roast potatoes and gravy, served in pubs from about midday on Sundays. In London's ranked pubs a meat roast costs £24 to £33, and the best-known tables go weeks ahead. Every pub below is placed by how many independent awards, critics and reviewers name it, and by where it finished when a ranking exists.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **The Devonshire** in Soho is the most-cited roast in London and the UK's number one gastropub: rib of beef, £33, with tables released three weeks ahead every Thursday. **The Red Lion & Sun** in Highgate is #3 in the country and does a £15 children's roast. **Canton Arms** in Stockwell keeps tables back for walk-ins. **The Harwood Arms** is London's only Michelin-starred pub, at £64 for two courses. **Blacklock** is the group answer, at £28 a head for beef, lamb and pork together. For a deal, **Smith's Bar & Grill** in Paddington runs a bottomless roast for £42 a head.

> 📊 **The evidence behind this guide.**
> **20 sources carrying 106 citations** across **69 named pubs and restaurants**. **22 are named by two or more independent sources; 26 carry a dated ranking.**
> **Built on:** the [Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs](https://www.top50gastropubs.com/list/1-50) and the [National Pub & Bar Awards](https://www.nationalpubandbarawards.co.uk/the-regions-2026); five publications, led by Time Out's ranked list; twelve video creators; and r/london.
> *Evidence rebuilt 3 November 2026 · [How we rank →](/how-we-rank/)*

> 📅 **How far ahead to book.**
> **Three weeks, on the dot:** The Devonshire opens its Sunday tables every Thursday at 10.30am. **Up to 90 days:** The Harwood Arms. **Two to three weeks:** The Camberwell Arms, The Kerfield Arms and The Knave of Clubs. **Walk in:** Canton Arms, The Waterman's Arms, the bar at The Blue Stoops and Blacklock keep tables back, ROAST takes walk-ins every day, and The Audley takes no bookings at all.

## The most-cited roasts in London

### The Devonshire, Soho

*££ · Piccadilly Circus · Cited by 6 sources · #1, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026 · #1, Industry's Choice 2026*

The most-cited roast in this guide and the top-ranked gastropub in the UK. It also took the National Pub & Bar Awards' **[Industry's Choice Award](https://www.nationalpubandbarawards.co.uk/industrys-choice) for the second year running** in 2026, a top ten voted by 400 people who run pubs.

There is one roast: **rib of beef at £33**, with roast potatoes, creamed leeks, peas with onion and bacon, a Yorkshire pudding and gravy, served upstairs from 12pm to 4pm. The ground floor is a walk-in Guinness pub, with no children at weekends.

Three video reviewers who filmed here in 2026 all describe the same problem: getting in. **Tables are released every Thursday at 10.30am, for three weeks ahead.**

**The venue's site:** [devonshiresoho.co.uk](https://www.devonshiresoho.co.uk/) · **two minutes from Blacklock**

### The Red Lion & Sun, Highgate

*££ · Highgate · Cited by 3 sources · #3, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026 · #4, Industry's Choice 2026 · London Pub of the Year 2025*

Third in the UK, named London's pub of the year at the 2025 National Pub & Bar Awards and fourth in the trade's own 2026 vote, yet the editorial roast lists barely mention it: three sources here against The Camberwell Arms' four. It is also the roast **Oisin Rogers of The Devonshire sends people to**.

Order the **roast rib of beef (£32)**, or a côte de boeuf for two or three (£95); lamb shank, chicken, pork belly and a vegetable and chestnut roast fill out the menu, all with a Yorkshire pudding and red wine jus. Children get their own roast beef for £15.

The pub has two beer gardens in Highgate Village. Garden tables are booked by phone, 48 hours ahead and weather permitting.

### Canton Arms, Stockwell

*££ · Stockwell · Cited by 2 sources · #11, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026 · #19, Time Out*

**Eleventh in the country, and it still keeps tables back for anyone who turns up.** The South Lambeth Road pub takes bookings up to four weeks ahead, then seats walk-ins in what is left.

The menu is written each day around meat for the table. The showpiece is a **lamb shoulder for five**, £145 in Time Out's October 2026 list, which puts single mains from £24. Dogs are welcome; book a bar-area table if you bring one.

**Sunday lunch stops at 3.45pm and there is no Sunday dinner.** The kitchen is closed on Mondays.

**Book:** [cantonarms.com](https://cantonarms.com/bookings/)

**Seventeenth in the country in the 2026 Estrella Damm Top 50**, and in The Infatuation's description more a sit-down restaurant than a pub you can drink in.

Three plated roasts with roast potatoes, hispi cabbage and gravy: **dry-aged Highland beef rump with Yorkshire pudding and horseradish (£29)**, Swaledale pork shoulder with apple ketchup (£28), or chicken with bread sauce (£27). A Highland sirloin for two is £78. There is no vegetarian roast.

**Food is served 12pm to 4pm on Sundays, and The Baring is closed on Mondays.** [Book on SevenRooms](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/thebaring/reservations/create/search/).

### The Marksman, Hackney

*££ · Bethnal Green · Cited by 2 sources · #23, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026*

**Downstairs is a pub; upstairs is a dining room designed by Martino Gamper.** The Marksman was the first pub in London to be named Michelin's Pub of the Year.

Sunday is a set menu: **£42 for two courses or £48 for three.** The roast is Hereford beef rump with Yorkshire pudding and horseradish cream, or roast chicken; a Hereford wing rib for two costs £25 extra. The pudding to order is the **brown butter and honey tart**. Parties of nine or more can have it served family-style.

The kitchen is open on Sundays from 12pm to 4pm and again from 6pm to 9pm. [Book on OpenTable](https://www.opentable.co.uk/r/the-marksman-hackney-reservations-london?lang=en-GB&ot_source=website&restref=208518), and drink downstairs first.

### The Waterman's Arms, Barnes

*££ · Barnes Bridge · Cited by 2 sources · #33, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026*

**A Barnes pub on the Thames where Sunday lunch is cooked for two.** The sample Sunday menu is built on sharing plates: **piri-piri spit-roast chicken (£50)**, porchetta (£50) or lamb shoulder (£52), with roast potatoes as a £6.50 side and a £30 bavette for one. There is no Yorkshire pudding on it.

The balcony has six tables for two, first come, first served, and the pub keeps tables back for walk-ins.

**The kitchen stops at 4.45pm on Sundays, and it is closed on Mondays.** [Book on SevenRooms](https://www.watermansarms.co.uk/reservations).

### The Tamil Crown, Islington

*££ · Angel · Cited by 3 sources · #35, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026*

![The Tamil Crown's Sunday roast on a steel thali: tandoori chicken, roast potatoes, parathas, poppadoms, red cabbage and curry sauces](../../assets/articles/best-sunday-roast-london/tamil-crown-roast.jpg)

**The second pub from The Tamil Prince team, serving South Indian sharing plates in a restored pub on Elia Street.**

The Sunday roast arrives as a thali. **Roasted masala chicken (£42)**, masala lamb shank (£45) or sea bream (£42) comes with roast potatoes and gravy beside avial (vegetables in coconut and yoghurt), gobi 65 (fried spiced cauliflower), potato and pea masala, mango pickle and roti. The vegetarian version (£42) brings sambar and rice. There is no beef.

**It is served all day Sunday, 11am to 9.30pm.** Sibling to The Tamil Prince, also in Islington; two separate pubs. [Book for Sunday](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/thetamilcrown/reservations/create/search).

### The Harwood Arms, Fulham

*£££ · Fulham Broadway · Cited by 3 sources · #36, Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs 2026*

![A roast at the Harwood Arms on a board: two tall Yorkshire puddings, roast potatoes, pink beef and broccoli](../../assets/articles/best-sunday-roast-london/harwood-arms-roast.jpg)

**London's only Michelin-starred pub**, on Walham Grove. Two of its directors supply the kitchen: Mike Robinson's Deer Box sends wild venison from the 60,000 acres of deer land he oversees, and Brett Graham's Capability Graham sends Jersey beef and Iberian pork.

Sunday is **£64 for two courses or £79 for three**, and the roast is carved for two: Jersey beef sirloin (£7.50 a head extra), bacon-wrapped deer shoulder, Cumbrian pork belly or Cornish cod, with Yorkshire puddings and cauliflower cheese. Start with the £12 **venison scotch egg**, a soft-yolked egg fried in venison sausagemeat.

**Roasts run all day Sunday until 8.15pm.** Tables open 90 days ahead, and a no-show costs £50 a head. Book through [the venue's site](https://harwoodarms.com/).

## What the rankings do and do not tell you

Two judged, dated rankings reach this topic. The **[Estrella Damm Top 50 Gastropubs](https://www.top50gastropubs.com/list/1-50)** is the deeper of the two, with 24 London pubs across its ranked 100. The **[National Pub & Bar Awards](https://www.nationalpubandbarawards.co.uk/industrys-choice)** names a winner for each region and county, and runs an Industry's Choice top ten voted by 400 people who run pubs for a living — four of its 2026 top ten are London. The two rankings agree at the top — The Devonshire is first on both — and diverge below it: the trade puts The French House third where Estrella Damm has it 47th.

Neither is a roast award: both rank the pub across everything it does, every day of the week. Nobody scored the Sunday plate, and The French House's dining room does not open on Sundays at all.

## Time Out disagrees with everyone

Time Out's ranked 20 London Sunday roasts, last updated on 26 October 2026, shares three names with the whole Estrella Damm 100: The Camberwell Arms, Canton Arms and The Blue Stoops. The Devonshire, Blacklock, Hawksmoor and The Harwood Arms are not on it.

Its number one is **[Elm at The Montpelier](https://themontpelier.net/food/)**, the kitchen at a Peckham pub, which Time Out credits to Mike Bagnall of Auguste in London Fields. The Sunday menu is short: aged beef rump £24, Iron Age pork loin £22, and a quarter of a Somerset Saxon chicken or a wild mushroom and celeriac pithivier at £20, all with a Yorkshire pudding. Lunch runs 12pm to 5pm, with live jazz in the bar from 7.30pm, and [booking](https://bookings.designmynight.com/book?venue_id=51bed1410df690c03800027d) holds £25 a table on a card. No other source here names it.

Its #2 is **Ling Ling's**, a Cantonese residency at Godet, a natural wine pub on Essex Road; Time Out describes sesame-studded Yorkshires and miso gravy with five-spice sea bass or pork belly. It [books online](https://www.sevenrooms.com/explore/godet/reservations/create/search/) for roast sittings from 1pm to 4pm.

## The roasts that are not in a pub

Four of the best-supported roasts in London are served in restaurants: no bar to wait in, no walking in for a pint and deciding to eat, and usually no dogs.

### Blacklock, Soho

*££ · Piccadilly Circus · Cited by 5 sources, all video*

The second most-cited roast in this guide, behind The Devonshire. **All five Sunday roast citations are video**: no award, no masthead roast list.

The order for a table is the **All In: beef, lamb and pork together for £28 a head**, for two or more. Single plates are beef rump (£27), lamb leg (£26), pork loin (£25) or a barbecued cauliflower chop (£18), each with a beef-dripping Yorkshire, duck-fat potatoes and **unlimited gravy**.

The same menu runs at all five London sites (Soho, the City, Shoreditch, Covent Garden and Canary Wharf), all day, with the last table at 8pm and some kept back for walk-ins. Soho is a basement with no lift.

![A bone-in chop at Blacklock, carved at the table, with confit potato and bacon-wrapped sausages alongside](../../assets/articles/best-sunday-roast-london/blacklock-chop.jpg)

*Blacklock's bone-in chop.*

**Book:** [the Soho site](https://theblacklock.com/soho-reservations/) · **two minutes from The Devonshire**

### Hawksmoor, Spitalfields

*££££ · Shoreditch High Street · Cited by 2 sources*

**British beef dry-aged and grilled over charcoal.** Spitalfields was the first Hawksmoor; there are now eight in London, and every one serves the same Sunday roast.

That roast is **dry-aged rump (£29.50) or sirloin (£35)** with beef-dripping potatoes, a Yorkshire pudding, roasted garlic and bone-marrow and onion gravy. For two or more, Sunday Feasting brings a sharing cut from the blackboard, with the trimmings at £9.50 a head. Mention children when you book and the kitchen does them a smaller roast.

![Gravy being poured over a Sunday roast at Hawksmoor: beef, a Yorkshire pudding, roast potatoes, glazed carrots and greens](../../assets/articles/best-sunday-roast-london/hawksmoor-roast.jpg)

*The Sunday roast at Hawksmoor.*

**The roast is guaranteed only until 5pm.** [Book Spitalfields](https://thehawksmoor.com/book-a-table/?location=11335) for groups of up to 14.

### The Quality Chop House, Clerkenwell

*£££ · Farringdon · Cited by 2 sources · #12, Time Out*

**The dining room on Farringdon Road dates from 1869, and its wooden booths are Grade II listed.**

Sunday is a set lunch: **£49 for two courses or £59 for three.** On 1 November 2026 the roasts were Aberdeen Angus rump with smoked brisket, Middle White pork loin, Yorkshire pheasant, monkfish, celeriac, and a chicken for two. The house **confit potatoes**, thin slices pressed into a block and fried until the layers separate, are an £11 side.

**Lunch only, 12pm to 3.30pm, with no Sunday dinner; card only, and 13.5% service.** [Book on OpenTable](https://www.opentable.co.uk/the-quality-chop-house-reservations-london?restref=96150&lang=en-GB&ot_source=Restaurant%20website).

### Fallow, Piccadilly

*£££ · Piccadilly Circus · Cited by 2 sources, both video*

## What a roast costs: cheap, bottomless and children's roasts

A meat roast at a ranked London pub costs **£24 to £33**, and most of these kitchens add 12.5% service on top.

**Under £25 a plate.**

* **[Smith's Bar & Grill](https://www.smithsbarandgrill.co.uk/whats-on/sunday-roast), Paddington** — individual roasts **from £18**, served beside the Grand Union Canal at Sheldon Square, every Sunday from 12pm to 9pm.
* **Blacklock** — the cauliflower chop roast is **£18** and the pork loin £25, at all five sites.
* **Elm at The Montpelier, Peckham** — a quarter chicken or the pithivier at **£20**, the beef at £24.
* **Vegetarian roasts at ranked pubs** — The Parakeet's squash and mushroom wellington is £21, and The Red Lion & Sun's vegetable and chestnut roast and The Knave of Clubs' nut roast are both £22.

**Bottomless.**

* **Smith's Bar & Grill** — **£42 a head** for 90 minutes of unlimited small plates, roasts and puddings; bottomless drinks add £18 a head. Every Sunday, 12pm to 9pm, and the whole table has to take the same package.

**One price for the table.**

* **Every cut on one plate, two or more** — Blacklock's All In and The Knave of Clubs' three roasts are both **£28 a head**; Island's family-style roast is £40 and Maison François' Le Grand Roast £55.
* **[Roe](https://roerestaurant.co.uk/menu/sunday-feast-of-roe/), Canary Wharf** — the Sunday Feast, a roast platter to share, for **£60 a head**, two or more.
* **The Devonshire** — the feasting menu for **£60 a head**, tables of eight to fourteen.
* **Set lunches** — The Marksman £42 or £48, The Quality Chop House £49 or £59, The Harwood Arms £64 or £79.

**Children.**

* **The Red Lion & Sun** — a children's roast beef for **£15**.
* **[The George](https://thegeorge.london/), Fitzrovia** — a children's roast beef or chicken for **£15**, Sundays.
* **[Clapton Country Club](https://www.claptoncountryclub.co.uk/sunday-roast-bookings)** — a child's portion of any roast for **£12**.
* **Island at the George & Dragon** — children's roasts at **£18**.
* **[Humble Grape](https://www.humblegrape.co.uk/family-friendly-dining)** — three courses for **£12** at weekends at its Battersea, Canary Wharf and Islington wine bars, with a roast dinner as the Sunday main.
* **Hawksmoor** — a smaller roast for children on request when you book; no set price.
* **Leave the children at home for** Teal (no under-14s), Sessions Arts Club (adults only) and the ground-floor pub at The Devonshire (no children at weekends).

## What to know

* **Kitchen times vary more than you would think.** The Quality Chop House stops at 3.30pm, Canton Arms at 3.45pm, and The Devonshire and The Baring at 4pm; Hawksmoor guarantees its roast only until 5pm. For a late lunch, Blacklock seats until 8pm, The Harwood Arms serves until 8.15pm and The Tamil Crown until 9.30pm.
* **Check whether it is a pub or a restaurant.** Blacklock, Hawksmoor, The Quality Chop House and Fallow are restaurants, and The Baring is closer to one than not.
* **Vegetarian roasts are not universal.** The Devonshire and The Baring list none, and The Drapers Arms' vegetarian main is a risotto. Hawksmoor, Fallow, Blacklock, The Red Lion & Sun, The Bull & Last and The Tamil Crown all have one.
* **Budget for service.** Most kitchens here add 12.5%; The Quality Chop House, Teal and The Blue Stoops add 13.5%, and ROAST and Fallow 15%. The Quality Chop House and The Audley take cards only.
