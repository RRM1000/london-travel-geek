## Sightseeing cruises

The two main narrated services between Westminster and Greenwich both pass the Houses of Parliament, the London Eye, St Paul's, the Shard and the Tower, go under Tower Bridge and finish at the Cutty Sark. The difference is the piers and how you buy.

### City Cruises

Boats leave **Westminster Pier every 40 minutes from 10:00**, calling at **London Eye Pier, Tower Pier and Greenwich**. The captain gives a commentary in English, and the City Experiences app carries it in 14 other languages. Each boat has an open upper deck, a heated saloon, a bar and toilets with baby changing and disabled facilities.

- **Tickets (September 2026):** single **£15**, return **£21**; children 5 to 15 **£10.25** and **£15**; under-5s free. A [24-hour hop-on hop-off pass](https://www.cityexperiences.com/london/city-cruises/river-red-rover/) is **£24.95** (family of two adults and three children £49), and the [one-day pass](https://www.cityexperiences.com/london/city-cruises/1-day-hop-on-hop-off-river-pass/) is **£9**, child **£7.50**.
- **Online only.** The sightseeing ticket cannot be bought at the piers.
- **Journey times:** Westminster to Tower 40 minutes, Westminster to Greenwich 70 minutes, Tower to Greenwich 30 minutes.
- **Last boat to Greenwich** from Westminster: **16:00** until 25 October 2026, **14:40** from 26 October. The [timetable](https://www.cityexperiences.com/london/city-cruises/sightseeing-schedule/) lists every departure.
- Some circular trips change boats at Tower Pier.

### Thames River Sightseeing

A family-run operator using **Westminster Pier, Tower Bridge (Butler's Wharf Pier) on the south bank, and Greenwich**. Its hop-on hop-off pass adds **Embankment, Festival and Bankside** piers. The skipper and crew do the commentary live.

- **Tickets (September 2026, online):** [Westminster to Greenwich](https://thamesriversightseeing.com/booking-tickets/westminster-to-greenwich-thames-river-sightseeing/) from **£14.62** one way and **£19.12** return, child from **£9.60** and **£12.92**; Westminster to Tower Bridge from **£11.90**. The [1-Day River Pass](https://thamesriversightseeing.com/booking-tickets/1-day-river-pass-1-day-free-hop-on-hop-off) gives a second day free, from **£21.21**, child £14.41, family £49.90.
- **Buy online or at the pier.** Student and senior (60+) tickets are sold only at the ticket office. Refunds up to 24 hours before.
- **Any boat that day.** A ticket is accepted on any sailing on its date, so a missed boat costs nothing.
- **Timing:** Westminster to Greenwich about an hour; departures run between 10:00 and 17:00. The [winter timetable](https://thamesriversightseeing.com/information/timetable/) starts on 2 November, with no boats on 24, 25 and 26 December.
- Dogs are welcome on board.

### The London Eye circular cruise

A 40 to 45-minute loop from **London Eye Pier** and back, with live commentary. It suits anyone combining it with the wheel on the same afternoon; our [London Eye guide](/articles/london-eye-guide/) covers the combined tickets.

Golden Tours and Big Bus sell hop-on hop-off bus tickets with a river cruise bundled in. If you were going to take both, [our comparison of London tour buses](/articles/london-tour-buses-compared/) sets out which tickets include the boat.

## Uber Boat: the cheap alternative

[Uber Boat by Thames Clippers](https://www.thamesclippers.com/plan-your-journey/ticket-information) is the river's commuter service, not a cruise. It runs every 10 to 20 minutes at the central piers, faster than the sightseeing boats, with indoor seats and a small open deck at the back.

- **Westminster to Greenwich** (Central and East zones): **£13.70** by contactless or Oyster at weekends and after 09:27 on weekdays, **£13.50** online, **£17.70** at the pier.
- **Hop-on Hop-off** day ticket: **£29.30** online, **£32.60** at the pier; child £14.65; family £58.70. Online singles, returns and day tickets bought at least three days ahead are 15% off.
- **No commentary**, but a **£2.50 [audio guide](https://www.thamesclippers.com/explore-london/london-river-tour-audio-guide)** narrated by Rory Bremner plays over the boat Wi-Fi. Bring headphones.
- River fares do not count towards the Tube's daily cap. These contactless fares run until 31 October 2026.

Zones, piers, touching in and out and the Travelcard discount are in our guide to [using London's river boats](/articles/how-to-use-london-river-boats/).

## Speedboats

**Thames Rockets** runs open speedboats from **London Eye Pier**, departing hourly all year. Each boat carries a live guide who is an actor or comedian. The central stretch is slow sightseeing; after Tower Bridge the boat opens up to 35mph through the Docklands to a soundtrack. Waterproof coats, ponchos and goggles are available, and it sails in rain.

- [**Ultimate London Adventure**](https://www.thamesrockets.com/ultimate-london-adventure/): 50 minutes, adult **from £59.95**, child **from £49.95**, all ages.
- [**Break the Barrier**](https://www.thamesrockets.com/break-the-barrier/): 45 minutes from **Bankside Pier**, flat out from the start, **from £69.95**, recommended for ages 10 and up.
- [**Thames Barrier Voyage**](https://www.thamesrockets.com/thames-barrier-explorers-voyage/): 80 minutes out to the Barrier and back, **from £79.95**, child £64.95.
- **Thames Lates**: 18 and over, with drinks served on board, from £64.95.

**Who cannot go:** Thames Rockets does not carry pregnant passengers, advises anyone with a back or neck condition not to travel, and asks parents of babies under six months to phone first. Children 14 and under need an adult with them. Check-in is 15 minutes before departure, and wheelchair access depends on the pier, so call before booking.

## Greenwich, the Thames Barrier, Kew and Hampton Court

### Greenwich

Every sightseeing boat above ends at Greenwich Pier beside the Cutty Sark. Get off, see the Royal Observatory and the Painted Hall, and come back on a later boat: return tickets on both sightseeing lines allow it. Our [Greenwich area guide](/articles/greenwich-area-guide/) plans the day, and the [Canary Wharf to Greenwich walk](/articles/canary-wharf-greenwich-walk/) arrives beside the same pier through the foot tunnel. For The O2, the Uber Boat stops at North Greenwich Pier; the [O2 travel guide](/articles/the-o2-travel-guide/) has the last boats after a show.

### The Thames Barrier

The Westminster to Greenwich sightseeing boats turn at Greenwich. The choices are the Thames Rockets **Thames Barrier Voyage** above, or the Uber Boat to **Woolwich (Royal Arsenal) Pier**, which sails through the Barrier's gates for an ordinary fare.

### Kew, Richmond and Hampton Court

[Thames River Boats](https://www.thamesriverboats.co.uk/fares-sailings/fares) runs the passenger boats upriver from Westminster. The oldest of its three boats, the *Connaught*, was built in 1911, and the *Princess Freda* crossed the Channel to Dunkirk in 1940. Boats to Hampton Court pass through Teddington Lock.

- **Fares from Westminster (2026):** Kew **£20** single, **£27** return; Richmond **£22** and **£33**; Hampton Court **£27** and **£36**. Children 5 to 15 half price; seniors 60+ £17 to £21 single; family tickets for two adults and two children.
- **Times:** one departure a day from Westminster at **11:00** in September 2026, reaching Kew in about 1h 15m to 1h 45m and Hampton Court in about 3h 15m. The return from Hampton Court leaves at 15:00.
- **The one thing that decides it:** sailings follow the tide and change week to week, and there are **no Westminster sailings from 28 September to 6 October 2026**. The [upcoming sailings page](https://www.thamesriverboats.co.uk/river-tour-information/upcoming-sailings) lists every boat for the next fortnight.
- Tickets online or at the pier offices. A bar on board takes cash or card.

Going one way by boat and back by train makes the day workable: [Kew Gardens](/articles/kew-gardens-guide/) is 1h 15m from Westminster, and [Richmond](/articles/richmond-area-guide/) has a direct train to Waterloo. ## Evening, Christmas and New Year's Eve cruises

**Illuminated River tour.** Uber Boat's [Illuminated River Official Boat Tour](https://www.thamesclippers.com/whats-on-and-offers/illuminated-river-official-boat-tours) is a 50-minute loop from **Tower Pier** under nine lit bridges, with commentary from official City of London and City of Westminster guides. **Saturdays at 21:30 only**, until 8 May 2027. **£16.50**, child and concession £9.90, bought online in advance.

**Christmas.** City Cruises runs the [Christmas sailings](https://www.cityexperiences.com/london/city-cruises/christmas-cruises-in-london/): party cruises from £75 a head, and on Christmas Day a four-course lunch cruise of about 3h 15m and one-hour sightseeing cruises, both from Westminster Pier. Thames Rockets' **Meet Santa** ride, from £69.95, runs on selected December sailings from London Eye Pier with a gift for every child. For the rest of the season see [Christmas in London](/articles/christmas-in-london/).

**New Year's Eve.** Boats sell out months ahead:

- **Thames River Sightseeing:** 21:00 to 00:30 from Westminster, moored near Big Ben for the fireworks, £172 a head for adults and children. **Fully booked**; places may free up in December.
- **City Cruises Celestial:** from Tower Pier, family-friendly, £390 adult, £345 for ages 5 to 17.
- **City Cruises Gala Dinner:** from Tower Pier, 18 and over, £629.
- **Thames Rockets:** 90 minutes on a 12-seat open speedboat, £449.

Party boats and the rest of the night are in our [New Year's Eve in London guide](/articles/new-years-eve-london/).

## Accessibility

- **Uber Boat:** every boat takes wheelchairs, including electric ones, and mobility scooters; every pier is step-free except Cadogan, London Bridge City and Wandsworth Riverside Quarter, where the ramp can be too steep at some tides. Details on its [accessibility page](https://www.thamesclippers.com/plan-your-journey/accessibility).
- **Thames River Sightseeing:** most boats are adapted for wheelchairs, but electric wheelchairs are not allowed and you may wait for a suitable boat. Disabled passengers and one carer get 50% off; email 72 hours ahead for the online code ([details](https://thamesriversightseeing.com/information/accessibility/)).
- **City Cruises:** every sightseeing boat has disabled toilet facilities; state any access needs when booking a festive cruise.
- **Thames River Boats:** the historic boats cannot take a motorised wheelchair, and the Richmond and Hampton Court piers are reached by steps ([policy](https://www.thamesriverboats.co.uk/river-tour-information/disabled-policy)).
- For step-free routes to the piers, see [step-free London](/articles/step-free-london/).
