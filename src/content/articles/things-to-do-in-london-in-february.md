Fashion Week and the awards season take the second half, and two big exhibitions open. The light comes back fast: sunset moves from 4.49pm to 5.38pm across the month.

## Lunar New Year — from Saturday 6 February

**Lunar New Year's Day falls on Saturday 6 February 2027**, the start of the Year of the Goat. [Chinatown London](https://chinatown.co.uk/en/experience/whats-on/chinese-new-year-in-chinatown/) lists its Chinese New Year celebrations across the neighbourhood from **6 to 20 February**, so the lanterns and the New Year menus overlap half term. Our [Chinese New Year in London guide](/articles/chinese-new-year-london/) covers the celebrations in full, and the [Soho and Chinatown walk](/articles/soho-chinatown-walk/) ends among the lanterns in Newport Place.

## Valentine's Day — Sunday 14 February

**Valentine's Day is a Sunday in 2027**, and England v France kicks off at Twickenham at 3.10pm the same afternoon. Restaurants and things to book for the day are in our [Valentine's Day in London guide](/articles/valentines-day-london/).

## Six Nations at Twickenham

**England play two home games in February at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham: France on Sunday 14 February at 3.10pm, and Italy on Saturday 20 February at 4.40pm.** The [Six Nations fixture list](https://www.sixnationsrugby.com/en/m6n/fixtures/202700) has England opening away to Ireland in Dublin on Friday 5 February at 8.10pm, and the last home game is Scotland on Saturday 13 March.

> ⚠️ **Arrive at least an hour before kick-off.** Entry is only through the gate printed on your ticket, and it isn't guaranteed in the last 60 minutes. Twickenham station is the bottleneck afterwards; Whitton, 20 minutes' walk west, has no ticket gates.

Gates open three and a half hours before an England international. Near the station, **[The Cabbage Patch](https://www.cabbagepatch.co.uk/)** on London Road is the Fuller's rugby pub, with big screens and an 11am Saturday opening, and **[The William Webb Ellis](https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pubs/the-william-webb-ellis-twickenham)**, the Wetherspoon on the same road, opens at 8am. Trains, the shuttle bus, the parking zone and the bag rules are in our [Twickenham Stadium guide](/articles/twickenham-stadium-travel-guide/).

## February half term — 15 to 19 February

**Camden, Lambeth, Lewisham and Royal Greenwich all set half term for Monday 15 to Friday 19 February 2027**, and most London state schools follow the same week. The Natural History Museum warns of queues to get in during school holidays, the Harry Potter studio tour sells out school-holiday dates first, and the Wednesday is a Tube strike day. Our [February half-term guide](/articles/february-half-term-london/) has what's on for children, what to book and how to cut the cost.

**Imagine**, the Southbank Centre's festival for children aged 0 to 11 and their adults, runs every February half term with performances, music, stories, comedy and workshops, and much of it is free. The [Southbank Centre](https://www.southbankcentre.co.uk/whats-on/festivals-series/imagine-childrens-festival) says it returns in February 2027.

## Fashion Week and the awards

### London Fashion Week — 18 to 22 February

**The [British Fashion Council](https://www.britishfashioncouncil.co.uk/Global-Platforms/LFW-Designer-Applications) runs London Fashion Week from Thursday 18 to Monday 22 February 2027.** The catwalk shows are for buyers, press and invited guests. The part a visitor can join is listed on the official schedule at [londonfashionweek.co.uk](https://londonfashionweek.co.uk/schedule): designers' pop-ups, presentations and exhibitions. The [Soho guide](/articles/soho-area-guide/) covers the streets around most of them.

### The BAFTA Film Awards

**BAFTA holds its Film Awards in February; the 2026 ceremony was on Sunday 22 February at the Royal Festival Hall.** Free places in the red-carpet fan pens are allocated through Applause Store, and in 2026 applications closed two weeks before the ceremony. How to apply, and what a fan-pen place gets you, is in our [BAFTA red carpet guide](/articles/bafta-red-carpet/).

### The WhatsOnStage Awards — Sunday 28 February

**The theatre awards voted for by the public are at the London Palladium on Sunday 28 February at 6.30pm**, with seats sold by [LW Theatres](https://lwtheatres.co.uk/whats-on/27th-whatsonstage-awards/). The BRIT Awards, on Saturday 27 February, are at Co-op Live in Manchester, not in London. Every ceremony with a public way in is in our [award ceremonies guide](/articles/award-ceremonies-london/).

## Exhibitions

### Opening in February

- **Peter Blake: Pop Art Life**, National Portrait Gallery, **11 February to 23 May**. Portraits by Blake across his media, including found objects. £19 Monday to Thursday and £21 Friday to Sunday, on the [National Portrait Gallery's listings](https://www.npg.org.uk/whatson/events-calendar/?what=exhibition).
- **Monet: Painting Time**, Tate Modern, **25 February to 27 June**. Tate Modern's first exhibition devoted to Monet alone, built around how he painted time, from Gare Saint-Lazare to the Water Lilies at Giverny. £26, concessions £25, £5 for 16 to 25s on Tate Collective; [booking is open](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/monet).

### Closing in February

- **Nicole Eisenman**, a free display at the Royal Academy, to **5 February**.
- **Tim Walker's Fairyland: Love and Legends**, National Portrait Gallery, to **7 February**: £23 Monday to Thursday, £25 Friday to Sunday.
- **Yinka Shonibare and Thomas Gainsborough: A Conversation**, National Gallery, free, to **7 February**.
- **The 90s: Art and Fashion**, Tate Britain, to **14 February**: curated by Edward Enninful, with Juergen Teller and Corinne Day's photographs beside Damien Hirst, Sarah Lucas, Vivienne Westwood and Alexander McQueen, £22. It stays open until 10pm on Friday 12 February for Late at Tate Britain. [Tate](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/the-90s).
- **Light and Magic: The Birth of Art Photography**, Tate Modern, to **21 February**, £20.
- **Robert Ryman: The Real Thing**, Barbican Art Gallery, to **28 February**: square paintings made mostly in white paint, £20.50 including the booking fee. [Barbican](https://www.barbican.org.uk/whats-on/2026/event/robert-ryman-the-real-thing).

### Still running

**Van Eyck: The Portraits** at the National Gallery runs to 11 April, **Peggy Guggenheim in London** at the Royal Academy to 14 March, and **Vanessa Bell & Duncan Grant: Inside Bloomsbury** at Tate Britain to 11 April. The British Museum's free **Multiplied wonders: Italian Renaissance woodcuts** runs to 21 March. The **Bayeux Tapestry** at the British Museum is sold out to 31 March; tickets for April to July are due for release in early 2027 on the [British Museum's page](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry). **Rooms 6b, 7 and 8 at the British Museum are closed from 8 to 19 February.** The free museums are in our [best museums guide](/articles/best-museums-london/).

## Ice skating

**The outdoor Christmas rinks have all closed by February**, so skating means an indoor rink. Alexandra Palace's is open all year at **£11.75 off-peak and £12.75 peak**, skates included, and under-8s must be accompanied on the ice. Queens in Bayswater, the two Olympic-size rinks at Lee Valley Ice Centre in Leyton and Streatham are the others. Our [ice skating guide](/articles/ice-skating-london/) has the details.

## Trade shows

**Motorsport Expo is at ExCeL from Friday 26 to Sunday 28 February**, with more than 200 exhibitors, and **Car Culture UK**, from the same organiser, shares the venue on 27 and 28 February. The rest of the year's shows are in our [exhibitions and conventions guide](/articles/exhibitions-conventions-london/).

## Getting around

> ⚠️ **RMT Tube drivers strike for 24 hours from noon on Wednesday 17, Tuesday 23 and Thursday 25 February.** The [RMT](https://www.rmt.org.uk/news/tube-drivers-to-take-eight-days-of-strike-action-over-attacks/) set the dates; each strike ends at noon the day after. The 17th falls in half term. Check TfL Go before you travel.

The dispute is with London Underground's drivers. Our [getting around London guide](/articles/getting-around-london-transport-guide/) covers the alternatives, [London transport costs](/articles/london-public-transport-costs-and-fares/) has the fares and caps, and [how to use the Underground](/articles/how-to-use-the-london-underground/) covers the lines.

## Practical: February in London

**Weather.** The Met Office's Heathrow average for February (1991 to 2020) is a daily high of **9.0C** and a low of **2.6C**, with **45mm of rain**, **6.2 air-frost days** and **76 hours of sunshine**, against 59 in January. Our [London in the rain guide](/articles/london-in-the-rain/) has the indoor fallbacks.

**Daylight.** Sunset is **4.49pm on 1 February**, **5.13pm on the 14th** and **5.38pm on the 28th**, with sunrise moving from 7.39am to 6.48am. The day lengthens from 9 hours 10 minutes to 10 hours 50 minutes.

**Crowds.** Half term, 15 to 19 February, refills the family attractions, and the two Twickenham match days pack Twickenham station. In the ONS's last monthly breakdown, February had the fewest overseas visits to Great Britain of January to June 2024: 2.46 million, against 3.36 million in January.

With children, see [London with children](/articles/london-with-children/).
