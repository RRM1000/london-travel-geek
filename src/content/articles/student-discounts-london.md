Most London restaurant student discounts are a percentage off food, and **the proof each one asks for differs: a UNiDAYS code, a Student Beans ID or a physical student card.** Have it ready when you order.

> 💡 **The Short Version:** **Shoryu Ramen** takes 30% off food Monday to Wednesday with Student Beans, and 15% the rest of the week. **Frankie & Benny's** gives 30% off food any day. **Yo! Sushi** gives 25% off, **The Real Greek** 25% Sunday to Thursday, and **Ivan Ramen** in Clerkenwell 25% off ramen on weekdays from noon to 5pm. **Côte**, **Franco Manca** and **Rosa's Thai** give 20% Sunday to Thursday. For a fixed price, **Pizza Pilgrims** in Euston and Shoreditch sells a margherita or 'nduja pizza for £7.50, and **Patty & Bun** a burger, rosemary chips and a soft drink for £14.50.

> ⚠️ **Many student offers run Sunday to Thursday only.** Côte, Franco Manca, Rosa's Thai, Masala Zone and The Real Greek all exclude Friday and Saturday.

## Getting proof of student status

**UNiDAYS** and **Student Beans** are free apps that verify you through your university, then issue a code or an ID screen at the table. Restaurants name the one they accept: Côte takes UNiDAYS, while Shoryu, Franco Manca, Rosa's Thai and Greggs take Student Beans. Where a restaurant asks for a student ID, a dated university card is what it means; Four Seasons says so in its terms, and Govinda's also accepts NUS Extra and ISIC.

**Las Iguanas** on the South Bank runs a student bottomless brunch at £24.95 for two hours, Monday to Thursday, with a main dish. It's on our [bottomless brunch page](/articles/bottomless-brunch-london/) with every other package priced by the minute.
