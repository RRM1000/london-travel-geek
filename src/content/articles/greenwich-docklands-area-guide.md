Greenwich combines maritime history, a royal park and a village centre. Across and downstream, Canary Wharf and the Royal Docks offer modern architecture, river views, larger hotels and fast Elizabeth line or DLR connections.

> **Best for:** maritime history, families, views, concerts at The O2 and London City Airport. **Think twice if:** most of your plans end late in the West End.

## Best things to do

- Visit the Cutty Sark and National Maritime Museum.
- Walk uphill through Greenwich Park to the Royal Observatory.
- Take a scheduled river boat through central London.
- Explore Canary Wharf's public art and docks.
- Ride the London Cable Car between Greenwich Peninsula and Royal Docks.

Greenwich Market provides casual food, while Canary Wharf has a broad modern restaurant collection.

## Transport and airport fit

Greenwich has Southeastern rail and DLR; Cutty Sark DLR is closest to the historic centre. North Greenwich has the Jubilee line. Canary Wharf has Elizabeth, Jubilee and DLR stations that are separate but walkable.

London City Airport is exceptionally convenient by DLR. River boats are scenic but use separate fares.
