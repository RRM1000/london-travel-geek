> 💡 **The Short Version:** England play **France (14 February)**, **Italy (20 February)** and **Scotland (13 March)** at Allianz Stadium Twickenham in the Six Nations. Wembley has the **Carabao Cup final on 21 March**, the **Women's FA Cup final on 15 May** and the **FA Cup final on 22 May**, and the Red Roses play **France at Twickenham on 15 May** in the Women's Six Nations. The **Boat Race** is on **Sunday 11 April**, the centenary of the Women's Boat Race, and the **London Marathon** runs over **two days, 24 and 25 April**. Summer brings **Queen's (7 to 20 June)**, **Wimbledon's 150th Championships (28 June to 11 July)**, **two Ashes Tests**, at Lord's from **30 June** and the Oval from **29 July**, the **World Test Championship final** at the Oval from **9 June**, and the **London Athletics Meet** on **17 July**. The **Tour de France Femmes** races a team time trial through London on **Sunday 1 August**. **The big ones sell through ballots and members' priority first**: Wimbledon's and Lord's 2027 ballots have already closed, and Carabao Cup and play-off final tickets go through the two clubs.

## How tickets work

- **Ballots first.** Wimbledon's Public Ballot ran in September 2026, the Lord's ballot for the Ashes, Bangladesh Test and both ODIs has closed, and the darts at Ally Pally sold its whole run by ballot for the first time. Register with the venue or organiser well before the season starts.
- **Members get first go.** Surrey members can buy up to eight Ashes Test and eight Women's T20 tickets at the Oval before general sale. LTA Advantage members get a separate Wimbledon draw and a March sale of returned tickets.
- **Cup finals go through the clubs.** Wembley sells nothing for the Carabao Cup final or the play-off finals itself: tickets, accessible seats included, go through the two competing clubs.
- **Resell only through the official route.** Ally Pally darts tickets can be resold only on See Tickets Fan-to-Fan. For football, selling a ticket without the club's or organiser's written authority is a criminal offence under [section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/33/section/166).
- **Free events are free.** The Boat Race and the London Marathon need no ticket. For the Boat Race, Putney and Hammersmith Bridge are the crowded spots and the towpath pubs fill hours ahead; Barnes Bridge, near the finish, is quieter.
